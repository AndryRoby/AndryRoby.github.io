ARLing TikTok Publisher

This is the tool ARLing s. r. o. uses to publish its own short videos to its own TikTok account. It posts only content we made ourselves, and only after the operator checks every post on this page and clicks the button.

  • It reads your TikTok display name and profile picture, so you can see which account is connected.
  • It uploads a video you choose, either straight to your profile or as a draft to your TikTok inbox.
  • Nothing is ever posted automatically, and it never posts to any other account.
  • Only the ARLing operator can use it. Sign in keys stay on our own server in the EU, never in this page.
