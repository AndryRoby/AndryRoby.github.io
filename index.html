Small free tools for problems developers keep hitting.

Each one runs entirely in your browser: no account, no upload, no tracking of what you type. Built and maintained by ARLing, a one-person company in Bratislava that lets AI do most of the building.

tool · free

Redirect Doctor — Expo + Supabase OAuth

Enter your Expo scheme, runtime, Supabase URLs and provider redirect URIs. Get the expected redirect URL for every runtime, the exact mismatches, and copy-paste fixes.

 tool · free

Supabase Auth Redirect Doctor — web apps

Next.js, Vite or SvelteKit app where Supabase OAuth redirects to localhost in production? Enter your Site URL, redirect allow-list, callback route and provider settings and get the exact mismatch and the fix.

 tool · free

Supabase Auth Deep Link Doctor — Flutter

OAuth or magic link never comes back to your supabase_flutter app? Enter your scheme, host, Info.plist and AndroidManifest values, Supabase URLs and provider settings. Get the mismatches across all three files and the fixes.

 tool · free

Google OAuth redirect_uri_mismatch Doctor

Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch? Paste the URI from the error and your Google Cloud Console list, pick your stack (NextAuth, Supabase, Firebase, Passport, Django, Expo, Python…) and see the exact character that differs, plus the string to add.

Expo Universal Links & App Links Doctor

Universal link opens Safari instead of your app? Paste your apple-app-site-association, assetlinks.json and app.json values. Get the mismatches in team ID, bundle ID, fingerprints, intent filters and paths, with the fixes.

SEPA pain.001 Doctor — slovenské banky

Tatra banka, SLSP, VÚB alebo ČSOB odmietli váš XML s hromadným príkazom? Vložte súbor a zistite presne, ktorý element je zlý: IBAN, BIC, sumy, počty, symboly, znaky. Nič sa neodosiela, všetko beží v prehliadači.

bookapp — home library in millimetres

A spatial digital twin of your home library: shelves, cabinets, drawers and books modelled in real millimetres, so it knows exactly what fits and where. Free public beta for Android and Windows, in English and Slovak.
