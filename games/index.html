Fourteen games, a new puzzle every day.

Small logic games from ARLing. Every puzzle is generated fresh for its date, checked by the program to have exactly one solution, and played in your browser. No account, no ads, no infinite feed.

Every game we run.

Each one has its own week: easy on Monday, hardest on Sunday. Every game keeps hints that explain, a two step Check, practice sets with no date attached and a free archive going back a year.

  1. HedgehogsTwo hedgehogs in every row, column and flowerbed, and no two of them may touch.Free new every day
  2. MagpiesA picture hidden in numbers: follow the counts along every row and column.Free new every day
  3. OttersDraw one closed river along the grid lines so every number counts its own sides.Free new every day
  4. SquirrelsCross sums: every run of hollows adds up to its sign, with no number twice.Free new every day
  5. CranesJoin the sandbanks with straight walkways until every number has its bridges.Free new every day
  6. SwansOne loop through every swan: straight at the white ones, turning at the black.Free new every day
  7. VolesShade the flood so every island carries one number and no water is trapped.Free new every day
  8. BadgersSudoku with dotted cages that give their total, and nothing given at the start.Free new every day
  9. HeronsJoin each pair of nests with a path and use every cell of the marsh exactly once.Free new every day
  10. HaresSudoku where two burrows a knight's leap apart never hold the same number.Free new every day
  11. DormiceA logic grid: the clues leave exactly one way to match every dormouse.Free new every day
  12. OwlsDay owls and night owls, as many of each in every row and column, never three alike.Free new every day
  13. BeaversGive every tree a lodge beside it; lodges never touch and the numbers count each line.Free new every day
  14. FoxesPlace every fox from the numbered clues; the one left alone with the lost thing has it.Free new every day
  15. Field NotesA word search in a naturalist's notebook: every planet, tree or bird you find is inked in and painted, with one short fact and its source.Free new every day, and endless

Known elsewhere as Star Battle (Hedgehogs), Nonogram (Magpies), Slitherlink (Otters), Hashi (Cranes), Masyu (Swans), Nurikabe (Voles), Kakuro (Squirrels), Numberlink (Herons), Killer Sudoku (Badgers) and anti-knight Sudoku (Hares). Dormice is a logic grid puzzle, Owls a binary puzzle, Beavers a puzzle of trees and lodges, and Foxes a placement puzzle read from clues.

Prefer paper or an e-ink tablet? Ten of the fourteen are also puzzle books of 100 puzzles with solutions, 4.90 € a book and 19.90 € for all ten, with a free sample of every one.
