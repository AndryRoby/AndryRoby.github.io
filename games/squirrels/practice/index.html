Practice

Practice sets.

Seventy-two woods with no date attached, three sets of eight for each level. They never change, so you can come back to one, and they do not touch your daily streak. Your progress in each set stays in this browser.

Back to today's wood · How to play · Archive

A set turns green when you have solved all of it, and the dot in its corner means every wood in it went clean, with no hint and no check.

Easy.

Six by six. Every wood here can be solved with the plain layer 1 rules: the last empty hollow of a run, a total that can only be made one way, and the single number both crossing runs still allow.

  • Easy practice 18 woods, 6×6
  • Easy practice 28 woods, 6×6
  • Easy practice 38 woods, 6×6

Medium.

Eight by eight. The same rules, with more runs crossing each other before a wood opens up.

  • Medium practice 18 woods, 8×8
  • Medium practice 28 woods, 8×8
  • Medium practice 38 woods, 8×8

Hard.

Ten by ten. Patterns inside a run and, sometimes, a one step trial before it opens up.

  • Hard practice 18 woods, 10×10
  • Hard practice 28 woods, 10×10
  • Hard practice 38 woods, 10×10
