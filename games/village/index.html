Puzzle Village.

A little island of houses, and in each one lives a family of animals with a new logic puzzle every day. Tap a house to look inside and watch them work it out, then play today’s puzzle yourself.

Drag to look around. Pinch, or hold Ctrl and scroll, to zoom. With a keyboard: arrows move, + and − zoom, Tab visits the houses and Enter opens one.

Hold Ctrl and scroll to zoom

The rule.

Play today’s puzzle

Printed in nine inks, drawn entirely in code.Evening comes at six by your own clock.

One family of animals, one kind of puzzle, a new one every day with exactly one solution.

About the village.

Puzzle Village is a map of our free daily logic puzzles. Every house, tree, animal and ripple on it is drawn by a small program right in your browser, the way a risograph prints: flat inks laid one over another, a little grain, each colour a hair off register. There is no photo and no picture generator in it.

We made it light on your computer. The still parts are printed once and reused, only the small things that move are redrawn, and the village rests completely after forty quiet seconds, or as soon as you scroll away or switch tabs. If your system asks for reduced motion, it stays a still print.

Our anonymous statistics (self-hosted Umami) count the page visit, as on every page of ours, and which house was opened and which puzzle link was followed. Ideas or trouble: support@arling.sk.

Everything in the village is free. If you like small puzzles on paper too, we also make books.
