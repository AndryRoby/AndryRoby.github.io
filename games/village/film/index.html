Puzzle Village, in nineteen seconds.

A little island where every house keeps a new logic puzzle each day. Solve one and its window lights up; light all fourteen and the evening comes. The film is drawn by the village’s own code and scored right here in your browser.

Walk through the village

Sound starts only after you tap. It pauses when you scroll away.

What the film shows.

  1. The whole island in the evening, printed like a risograph poster: fourteen houses with every window lit, a ripple of light running across them, fireflies over the meadows and the water. The name Puzzle Village, the line 14 daily logic puzzles. Free. and arling.sk/games.
  2. Morning comes back in a circle from the hedgehogs’ cottage and every window goes dark, because tomorrow starts dark.
  3. The view glides down to the hedgehogs’ cottage and stays there. Its windows fill with light, its name appears, and the hedgehog asleep under the leaves climbs out. Solve a house’s puzzle today and its window lights up.
  4. On to the swans’ lake: the lantern at the end of the jetty lights up and one loop is drawn round the lake through every swan.
  5. On to the herons’ lookout: its window lights up and three flight paths are drawn over the marsh one after another, never crossing. A new puzzle in every house, every day.
  6. The view rises and sweeps across the village, and the other eleven windows light up one after another as they come into view, each house with its own little bell.
  7. With all fourteen lit, the evening pours over the print from the last window, fireflies come out over the meadows and the water, and the view rises over the whole island. Evening comes when all fourteen are lit.
  8. The whole island in the evening again: Puzzle Village. 14 daily logic puzzles. Free. arling.sk/games. It is the same picture the film began with, so it can play round and round.

Everything in the film is the village itself: the same program that draws Puzzle Village prints the island, the houses, the animals, the windows and the fireflies, in the same nine inks. The film only moves the view, chooses which windows light up and when, brings the morning and the evening in with a circle, and adds the names, the lines and the music. The little scenes in the houses are illustrations; the puzzles themselves are in the games.

About Puzzle Village.

Puzzle Village is a map of our fourteen free daily logic puzzles. Every house belongs to one puzzle and its family of animals, with a new puzzle every day and exactly one solution. Solve a house’s puzzle today and its window stays lit until the day ends; tomorrow starts dark. Evening comes at six by your own clock, or when all fourteen are lit.

Walk through the village, or go straight to all fourteen daily logic puzzles. Free, no account, no ads.

How the film is made.

There is no video file on this page. Every frame is drawn on a canvas by a small script that uses the village’s own drawing code, and every sound, from the first chord to the bell of each window, is synthesised by Web Audio in your browser after you tap. No photo, no recording, no picture generator. The same script renders the film frame by frame into a video.

It is built to be light: the whole island is printed once when the page opens and the closer views only when the browser is idle or you press play, every print is reused, the film stops when it ends, when you scroll away or switch tabs, and it shows a still picture if your device asks for reduced motion. Questions: support@arling.sk.

Our anonymous statistics (self-hosted Umami) count the page visit, whether the film was started or replayed, and clicks on the links to the village and the puzzles. Nothing else.

Everything in the village is free. If you like small puzzles on paper too, we also make books.
