Hedgehogs, in nineteen seconds.

Two hedgehogs in every row, column and flowerbed, and they never touch. One real step on a real garden. The film is drawn and scored in code, right here in your browser.

Play today's garden

Sound starts only after you tap. It pauses when you scroll away.

What the film shows.

  1. A solved garden of 8 by 8 cells, split into eight flowerbeds of different colours, with sixteen orange hedgehogs. A wave of light runs across it and the hedgehogs hop as it passes. The name Hedgehogs and the lines: A new garden every day. Two hedgehogs in every row, column and flowerbed, never touching.
  2. The hedgehogs hide and the empty garden moves to the middle.
  3. Ten hedgehogs drop into their cells, each row with its own note. Two hedgehogs in every row, column and flowerbed.
  4. The garden dims except the third row, which already has one hedgehog. Where does its second hedgehog go? Seven cells are free.
  5. Not next to a hedgehog, not even at a corner: prickly zones appear around three hedgehogs, and five of the free cells touch one of them. They stay empty and get a dot, as in the game.
  6. Not in a column that already has two: a beam runs up the first column, which holds two hedgehogs, and one more cell stays empty.
  7. Only one cell is left, in the second column, and the hedgehog drops in.
  8. The last five hedgehogs fill the garden, a wave of light runs across it and its frame glows green, as in the game when a garden is solved.
  9. The name appears again: Hedgehogs. A new garden every day. Two hedgehogs in every row, column and flowerbed, never touching. Free at arling.sk/games.

The garden in the film is the Easy garden of 1 June 2026 from the Hedgehogs archive, made by the same generator as every daily garden, with exactly one solution. The order in which the hedgehogs drop is ours, chosen for the music. The step in the third row is a real deduction: with the ten hedgehogs shown, the rules alone leave one cell for the second hedgehog of that row.

About Hedgehogs.

Hedgehogs is our free daily logic puzzle in the browser. Place two hedgehogs in every row, every column and every flowerbed. No two hedgehogs may touch, not even at a corner: they prickle. Every garden has exactly one solution. Easy on Monday, hardest on Sunday, with hints, practice sets and a free archive.

Play today's garden, or see all our free daily logic puzzles.

How the film is made.

There is no video file on this page. Every frame is drawn on a canvas by a small script, and every sound, from the first chime to the chord at the end, is synthesised by Web Audio in your browser after you tap. No photo, no recording, no picture generator. The same script renders the film frame by frame into a video for YouTube and Instagram.

It is built to be light: what does not move is drawn once and reused, the film stops when it ends, when you scroll away or switch tabs, and it shows a still picture if your device asks for reduced motion. Questions: support@arling.sk.

Our anonymous statistics (self-hosted Umami) count the page visit, whether the film was started or replayed, and clicks on the links to the game. Nothing else.
