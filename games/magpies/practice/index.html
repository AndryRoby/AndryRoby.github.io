Practice

Practice sets.

Twenty-four pictures with no date attached, three sets per level. They never change, so you can come back to one, and they do not touch your daily streak. Your progress in each set stays in this browser.

Back to today's picture · How to play · Archive

Easy.

Eight by eight. Every picture here can be solved with the simple steps Hint knows: an overlap forced by a block bigger than half the line, and a line where the numbers, with their gaps, add up to exactly its length.

  • Easy practice 38 pictures, 8×8

Medium.

Ten by ten. Marks you have already placed start narrowing the room left for a block, and Hint reasons from them the same way it reasons from the numbers alone.

  • Medium practice 18 pictures, 10×10
  • Medium practice 28 pictures, 10×10
  • Medium practice 38 pictures, 10×10

Hard.

Twelve by twelve. More passes of row-and-column logic before the picture opens up.
