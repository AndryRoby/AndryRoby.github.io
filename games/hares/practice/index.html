Practice

Practice sets.

Twenty-four meadows with no date attached, three sets per level. They never change, so you can come back to one, and they do not touch your daily streak. Your progress in each set stays in this browser.

Back to today's meadow · How to play · Archive

A set turns green when you have solved all of it, and the dot in its corner means every meadow in it went clean, with no hint and no check.

Easy.

Six by six, blocks of two rows by three columns, the knight’s leap. Every meadow here can be solved with the plain layer 1 rules: a burrow with one number left, and a number that fits only one burrow of a row, a column or a block.

  • Easy practice 18 meadows, 6×6
  • Easy practice 28 meadows, 6×6
  • Easy practice 38 meadows, 6×6

Medium.

Nine by nine, the knight’s leap. The same rules, with more of the board narrowed down before a meadow opens up.

  • Medium practice 18 meadows, 9×9
  • Medium practice 28 meadows, 9×9
  • Medium practice 38 meadows, 9×9

Hard.

Nine by nine, the knight’s leap and the touching rule. Patterns held in place by the leap or the touch, and sometimes a one step trial before it opens up.

  • Hard practice 18 meadows, 9×9
  • Hard practice 28 meadows, 9×9
  • Hard practice 38 meadows, 9×9
