From the listing

7 sheets.

  • Start here: 5 steps with a link to every sheet
  • Settings: year, how often you are paid (weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month or monthly), first payday, usual take home pay, what you save from each paycheck, 30 spending categories
  • Bills: up to 60 bills, typed once: amount, first due date and how often (monthly, every 2 weeks, weekly, every 3 months, yearly or once). Times per year, total and next due date are calculated.
  • Paychecks: every payday of the year (up to 54). For each one: the pay, the bills due before the next payday with their total and the first 8 names sorted by due date, saving, spending in that period, and what is left. A red Left shows a paycheck that comes up short.
  • Spending: up to 500 purchases. Each one goes to the paycheck it was spent from.
  • Dashboard: your next payday and the bills it must cover, the whole year (pay, bills, saved, spending, left over), short paychecks, bills due before your first payday, and a Data check
  • Calc: every due date of every bill, visible and locked, so each number can be traced

From the listing

How it works.

  • You type in Bills once and in Spending as you go. Everything else is calculated.
  • A bill belongs to the last payday before its due date. A bill due on the 31st is due on the last day of shorter months.
  • A different amount on one payday (overtime, a bonus)? Type it next to that payday.
  • Gray cells are calculated and locked without a password, so nothing breaks by accident. One click (Review, Unprotect Sheet) unlocks them.
  • Data check finds a missing first payday, bills without an amount or due date, bills typed twice, spending without a date and more. It says All good or tells you what to fix.
  • Any currency: type your symbol in Settings.
