02 / Check the fit

Try a real file in your layout.

  1. Open the sample ZIP and place a -puzzle.svg in your page.
  2. Resize it with its aspect ratio locked. The artwork is square, 760 × 760 SVG units, including a margin around the dots.
  3. Put the matching -solution.svg on your answer page. Check the digits and line weights in your exported proof.

The digits are editable text using Arial, Helvetica or your system sans-serif fallback. Fonts are not embedded. SVGs have a transparent background; use a light page behind the black puzzle artwork.

03 / What we checked

A defined set, with a defined test.

Each of the 25 puzzles has exactly one solution according to our solver. We also checked the answer against every clue, and checked that no two items in the pack have the same puzzle or answer.

Pattern is our label for puzzles solved by the engine using local and pattern rules. Its trial layer is disabled. This is a program check, not a difficulty rating from a panel of human solvers.

We compared the set against our current daily Slitherlink archive and existing Otters book. This does not establish worldwide novelty or exclusivity.
