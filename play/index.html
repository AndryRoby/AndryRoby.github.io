Before you download

When can I get the apps?

All four are in review at Google Play. We add the Google Play button here on the day each one goes live, and not before.

Do the apps need an account or the internet?

No account. The puzzles travel inside the apps, so they play offline.

Are the films real gameplay?

The films are drawn and scored in code from each app's own colours, rules and puzzles, not screen recordings. Quiet Grids shows real boards the app carries; Prism 5 shows a valid example grid.

Who makes them?

ARLing s. r. o., Bratislava, Slovakia. Support: app support.
