5Advertising

The app shows ads from Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Where the law requires it, mostly in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent form asks for your choice first. A full-screen ad can appear only when you leave the result of a finished match with Home: the sixth time you do so at the earliest, then at most every third time, at least 5 minutes after the previous full-screen ad and at most 4 a day. After a purchase or a rewarded ad, there is no full-screen ad until 5 minutes and three such exits have passed. Two optional rewarded ads play only when you ask for them: a head start on the result screen, once the winner agrees, and coins in the shop. No ad is ever required to play a match.

Refusing personalised advertising does not switch ads off. What Google's advertising library collects, and how to change your choice later, is in the privacy policy.

6Purchases in the app

All purchases are optional one-time purchases through Google Play Billing. Nothing is bought until you tap a price and confirm the payment in Google Play, which shows the price in your currency before you confirm; in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom that price includes VAT. Duel offers:

Coin packs of 200 and 700 coins
Consumable: the coins are added to your balance once and are used up as you spend them in the game. Product IDs in Google Play: coins_small and coins_medium.
Remove ads
No ad when you leave a finished match. The optional ads for coins and for a head start stay and play only when you tap them. One-time and lasting: Google Play keeps the record, and Restore purchases brings it back on the same Google account. Product ID in Google Play: remove_ads.

Coins. In Duel coins only buy board themes and confetti; nothing bought changes a round. You also earn coins by playing. Coins have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. They are stored on your phone: they come back only if Android restores a backup or a transfer from your old phone, and otherwise uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes them. Restore purchases does not bring back coin packs already delivered.

Delivery. A purchase is delivered as soon as Google Play confirms the payment and the app has checked, on your phone, the receipt signed by Google. A payment that Google Play holds as pending adds nothing until Google Play confirms it. If the app cannot verify a purchase, it adds nothing and does not confirm it, and Google Play refunds a purchase that stays unconfirmed for three days. If something you paid for is missing, write to support@arling.sk with the Google Play order number.

Purchases in the app are not shared with your Google Play family group.
