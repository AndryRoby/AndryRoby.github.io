        Want a mystery you can hold? The Missing Birthday Cake is a printable detective party game for children aged 8 to 12, 6.90 €.

        Missed a day? The last seven cases stay open.

          How it works.

          Every morning a few neighbours in the village borrow something and head out. The clues tell you what is true; the grids are where you write down what you know. A tick means a match, a cross means the clues rule it out. Each person matches exactly one thing in every grid. When every neighbour has an answer to every question, close the case; the other grids are there for your notes.

          Every case is made by our own code from the date, so everyone gets the same case on the same day. Before a case is published, our solver checks that the clues, read as written, allow exactly one answer, and that you can reach it step by step without guessing. The hint button does the same reasoning from where you are and tells you which clue or which of your marks the next step comes from.

          No sign up, no ads. Your marks, time and streak stay in this browser only. Miss a day and your streak waits for you one extra day.

          Our self-hosted statistics (Umami) count the page visit, taps on the buttons, the first mark on a case, hints, and a closed case with its number and how many hints it took. No names, no answers, nothing else.
