SupportARLing Android gamesUpdated 21 September 2026

Something wrong with one of our games?

Write to support@arling.sk. The mail goes to Andrej in Bratislava, who wrote the games and answers the mail himself. There is no ticket system and no queue.

Write to support

Answer
Usually the same day, at the latest within three working days.
Language
English or Slovak, whichever you prefer.
Cost
Nothing. The games are free and so is asking.
Company
ARLing s. r. o., Bratislava, Slovakia, company ID 56583486.

1The games

Word Search. Find hidden words in a letter grid. 150 topics, a daily puzzle, a journey map, coins earned by playing. Privacy policy: arling.sk/play/word-search/privacy/.

Duel: 2 Players. Two people, one phone, 27 mini-games, best of five. Privacy policy: arling.sk/play/duel/privacy/.

Prism Night. An idle game about light falling through ten nights. Privacy policy: arling.sk/play/prism-night/privacy/.

2What to put in the mail

Which game, what you did, and what happened instead. If the game showed a message, the exact words help more than a summary of them. The phone model and the Android version help with anything about drawing, sound or performance.

A screenshot is welcome. Please do not send passwords, card numbers or identity documents; we never need them and we do not want them on our mail server.

3Things we are asked often

My progress is gone. Every one of these games keeps its progress on the phone alone. There is no account and no cloud save, so clearing the app storage, uninstalling the game or moving to a new phone starts it over, and we cannot restore it. That is a real limitation and we would rather say it than pretend.

How do I change my advertising choice? Word Search: the information button, then Privacy choices. Duel and Prism Night: Settings, then Privacy options. The row appears only where the law requires a choice. You can also reset the advertising ID for the whole phone in Android Settings, under Privacy and then Ads.

How do I delete everything the game knows? Android Settings, then Apps, then the game, then Storage, then Clear storage. Duel can also clear just its statistics, and Prism Night just its progress, from inside the game.

Can I pay to remove the ads? Not today. None of the three games sells anything at the moment.

An ad got stuck or did not play. Close it and keep playing. A full screen ad in these games only ever appears at the end of something, never in the middle of a puzzle or a round: Word Search shows one between completed levels, Duel only on the way out of a finished match, and Prism Night has none at all. If a rewarded ad did not pay out, tell us which game and roughly when, and we will look at it.

4Who you are writing to

ARLing s. r. o., Ivanská cesta 19112/32E, 821 04 Bratislava, Slovakia, company ID 56583486, VAT ID SK2122352100. Written contact: support@arling.sk. What happens to a mail you send us is described in each game's privacy policy, section on retention.
