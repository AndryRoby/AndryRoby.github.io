SupportARLing Android gamesUpdated 30 September 2026

Something wrong with one of our games?

Write to support@arling.sk. The mail goes to ARLing s. r. o. in Bratislava, a one-person company where AI agents do most of the work and a person makes the decisions (see How we work). There is no ticket system and no queue.

Write to support

Answer
Usually the same day, at the latest within three working days.
Language
English or Slovak, whichever you prefer.
Cost
Nothing. The games are free to play, purchases in them are optional, and asking is free.
Company
ARLing s. r. o., Bratislava, Slovakia, company ID 56583486.

1The games

Word Search. Find hidden words in a letter grid. 150 topics, a daily puzzle, a journey map, coins earned by playing. Privacy policy: arling.sk/play/word-search/privacy/.

Prism 5. Five gems in a 5x5 grid, each gem once in every row and every column, four new daily grids every day, one for each grade, and levels. Privacy policy: arling.sk/play/prism5/privacy/.

Quiet Grids. Eleven kinds of logic grid with a new puzzle of each every day, playable offline. Privacy policy: arling.sk/play/quiet-grids/privacy/.

Duel. Two people, one phone, 27 mini-games; a match is best of five by default, and best of three or nine can be chosen before it starts. Privacy policy: arling.sk/play/duel/privacy/.

2What to put in the mail

Which game, what you did, and what happened instead. If the game showed a message, the exact words help more than a summary of them. The phone model and the Android version help with anything about drawing, sound or performance.

A screenshot is welcome. Please do not send passwords, card numbers or identity documents; we never need them and we do not want them on our mail server.

3Things we are asked often

My progress is gone. Every one of these games keeps its progress on the phone alone. There is no account and no cloud save, so clearing the app storage, uninstalling the game or moving to a new phone starts it over, and we cannot restore it. That is a real limitation and we would rather say it than pretend. Purchases are the one exception: Remove ads, and in Quiet Grids No adverts and The whole archive, are kept by Google Play and come back through Restore purchases on the same Google account. Bought coins do not come back.

How do I change my advertising choice? Word Search: the information button, then Privacy choices. Prism 5: Settings, then Privacy choices. Quiet Grids: More, then Privacy choices. Duel: Settings, then Privacy options. The row appears only where the law requires a choice. You can also reset the advertising ID for the whole phone in Android Settings, under Privacy and then Ads.

How do I delete everything the game knows? Android Settings, then Apps, then the game, then Storage, then Clear storage. Duel can also clear just its match statistics, with Reset statistics in its Settings.

Can I pay to remove the ads? Yes, in the versions that have purchases. Word Search, Prism 5 and Duel offer Remove ads and three packs of coins; Quiet Grids offers No adverts, The whole archive and three packs of coins. All are optional one-time purchases through Google Play. Each privacy policy says from which version a game offers purchases, and the Google Play page of a game says whether it has in-app purchases. We never see your payment details.

An ad got stuck or did not play. Close it and keep playing. A full screen ad in these games only ever appears at the end of something, never in the middle of a puzzle or a round: Word Search shows one between completed levels; Prism 5 only after a solved practice grid or a solved level from level 11 on, once you tap Next or Levels, never after a daily grid; Quiet Grids only when you leave a finished board from the archive, never after the puzzle of the day; Duel only on the way out of a finished match. If a rewarded ad did not pay out, tell us which game and roughly when, and we will look at it.
