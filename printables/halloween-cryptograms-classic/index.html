Details

Inside the book

  • 30 cryptograms, one per page, with big code letters and a line under each for your answer
  • 3 levels of 10: Easy (4 letters given, shorter quotes), Medium (2 letters given) and Hard (1 letter given, longer quotes)
  • A clue on every page: the author and the work the quote comes from
  • A key on every page to keep track of the letters you have found
  • A How to play page with a worked example in three steps
  • All 30 answers at the back with author, work and chapter
  • A sources page and a contents page you can click in the PDF

The quotes

Every quote is copied word for word from public domain texts (sources listed in the book): The Raven, Annabel Lee, Eldorado, The Gold-Bug, The Fall of the House of Usher and more by Poe; The Legend of Sleepy Hollow by Washington Irving; Frankenstein by Mary Shelley; and Dracula by Bram Stoker. We chose lines about autumn, night, dreams and courage, and left out gory passages. Some quotes are part of a longer sentence.

How it works

Each letter of the quote is swapped for another letter, and the swap stays the same across the whole puzzle. No letter stands for itself. Spaces and punctuation are kept, which gives you word lengths and patterns to work from.

Who it is for

Adults and teens who like word puzzles and classic literature, book clubs and English classes in October. Text is in English.

Printing

Print at 100% / Actual size. Black ink is enough; the given letters and a few headings are red. You may print as many copies as you need for yourself, your family or your classroom. Please do not share or resell the file.

Questions

Before you buy.

How do I get the files?

No physical book is shipped. After Etsy confirms payment, download your files from Purchases and reviews on Etsy.com in a web browser; downloads are unavailable in the Etsy app. Guest buyers can use the download link in their Etsy receipt email.

Which files do I get?

Two files: ARLing-Halloween-Cryptograms-30-A4.pdf and ARLing-Halloween-Cryptograms-30-US-Letter.pdf. The same pages in A4 and US Letter, so you print the one that matches your paper.

What if a file does not open or print?

For a file or printing problem, send an Etsy Message with your order number, file name and the error you see. Do not send card or payment details.
