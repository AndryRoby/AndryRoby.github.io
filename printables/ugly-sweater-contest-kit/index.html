Ugly Sweater Contest Kit Printable

Run an ugly sweater contest at the office, a club or a family party.

4.90 €

Four printable PDF files: A4 and US Letter, each in color and in black and white, 15 pages each. A digital download from Etsy: nothing is shipped.

Buy on Etsy, 4.90 €

Etsy handles the payment and the instant download. Etsy may show the price in your currency and add tax for your country; it shows the final total before you pay.

Contestants sign up and pin on a big number, guests vote on ballots, two or three judges score every sweater, and six awards go home with the winners. Clear counting rules are included, so nobody argues about who won.

Four PDF files in A4 and US Letter.

  • Paper sizes: A4 and US Letter.

Details

Inside

  • Page 1: start here, how to run the contest and how the winners are found
  • Page 2: contest poster (date, time, place, judging, how to sign up, prizes)
  • Page 3: sign up sheet for contestants 1 to 30
  • Pages 4 to 8: contestant numbers 1 to 30, six per page, to pin on the sweater
  • Page 9: guest ballots, 8 per page: Ugliest overall, Most festive, Best handmade, Most creative, Best duo or team
  • Pages 10 and 11: judge scorecards for contestants 1 to 15 and 16 to 30: tacky factor, festive spirit, creativity and handmade effort, 1 to 5 points each
  • Page 12: tally sheet for 3 judges and 5 guest categories
  • Pages 13 to 15: six award certificates, two per page (Ugliest Sweater, People's Choice, Most Festive, Best Handmade, Most Creative, Best Duo or Team)

How the winners are found

The Ugliest Sweater award goes to the highest total from the judges. The other five awards go to the contestant with the most guest votes in that category. A tie? The judges decide together.

Fillable fields

You can type in fields on 8 of the 15 pages: the poster, sign up sheet, scorecards, tally sheet and awards. The numbers are printed and the ballots are for writing by hand. The fields are standard PDF form fields. Before release we filled every field, saved the file and read the values back with two independent PDF tools. We have not tested every PDF app, and not phone or tablet apps. If your app does not let you type, print the page and write by hand.

Printing

Print at 100% (actual size). Print one ballot per guest and one set of scorecards per judge. You need scissors and safety pins.

Questions

Before you buy.

How do I get the files?

No physical item is shipped. After Etsy confirms payment, download your files from Purchases and reviews on Etsy.com in a web browser; downloads are unavailable in the Etsy app. Guest buyers can use the download link in their Etsy receipt email.

Which files do I get?

Four files: ARLing-Ugly-Sweater-Contest-Kit-A4-black-and-white.pdf, ARLing-Ugly-Sweater-Contest-Kit-A4-color.pdf, ARLing-Ugly-Sweater-Contest-Kit-US-Letter-black-and-white.pdf and ARLing-Ugly-Sweater-Contest-Kit-US-Letter-color.pdf. The same pages in A4 and US Letter, so you print the one that matches your paper.

What if a file does not open or print?

If a file does not open or print well, send us an Etsy message with your order number and the file name. Please never send card or payment details.
