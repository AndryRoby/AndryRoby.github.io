Details

How you solve it

Put pages 19 to 21 in an envelope before you start. Read the file: a timeline, witness statements, a cloakroom ledger and period papers. Match a photograph to the floor plan, decode a dealer's price code and check the train times. Then fill in the logic grid (guests, rooms, items and times) until one guest is left in the Long Gallery at 9:12. Stuck? The hints come in three levels, from a nudge to the full step.

The fair play rule

Every statement and document in the file is true. The guilty guest did not lie; they simply did not tell the whole story.

One answer, checked by a program

A computer solver confirmed that the logic grid has exactly one answer and that all 14 pieces of evidence are needed to reach it. No group has timed it at a real table yet, so the playing time below is our estimate.

Who it is for

Age 14 and up. It suits people who like logic grid puzzles and classic whodunits. 1 to 6 players: alone, read everything and then fill in the grid; in a team, share out the documents, read them aloud and sum them up for each other. About 45 to 90 minutes (alone, allow about an hour), plus about 10 minutes to print and get ready. It is a case file you read and reason through, not an escape room.

A gentle mystery

Nobody is hurt: the crime is a theft, and the painting is safe and unharmed. All people, places and firms are invented.

You need

A printer, plain paper, pencils, an eraser and an envelope. No scissors. Print one-sided at 100% (actual size); black and white is fine.

Good to know

This is a digital download. Nothing will be shipped. You get the two PDF files right after Etsy confirms your payment. Print as many copies as you need for your own household, club or classroom; please do not share or resell the files.
