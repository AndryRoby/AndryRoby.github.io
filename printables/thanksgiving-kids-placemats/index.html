Printable placemats, on Etsy

Thanksgiving Placemats for Kids

Eight printable activity placemats for the kids table on Thanksgiving.

2.90 €

Two printable PDF files: A4 and US Letter, 13 pages each. A digital download from Etsy: nothing is shipped.

Buy on Etsy, 2.90 €

Etsy handles the payment and the instant download. Etsy may show the price in your currency and add tax for your country; it shows the final total before you pay.

Each one is different and has a turkey and fall pictures to color, a maze, a word search, a secret picture, a plate to draw dinner on and lines for what they are thankful for. The answers are at the back.

Pictures

A closer look before you buy.

These are the pictures from the Etsy listing, in the same order. Click or tap a picture to open it larger.

AI was used to create the background scene of the listing images. Texts and puzzles, including the word lists, were created with the help of AI tools.

What you get

Two PDF files in A4 and US Letter.

  • ARLing-Thanksgiving-Kids-Placemats-A4.pdf (13 pages, landscape)
  • ARLing-Thanksgiving-Kids-Placemats-US-Letter.pdf (13 pages, landscape)

The same placemats in two paper sizes. Plain PDF files, no ZIP.

  • Paper sizes: A4 and US Letter.
  • File type: PDF, printed at home or at a print shop.
  • Digital download: nothing is shipped by post. The files are in Purchases and reviews on Etsy.com after payment.

Details

Inside

  • Page 1: how to use, for grown ups
  • Pages 2 to 9: 8 different placemats, one per page, each with a Name line
  • Pictures to color: a big turkey and a row of small fall pictures (pumpkin, leaf, acorn, apple, pie) on every placemat
  • Maze: 9 x 9 squares, a picture at the start and the finish (pumpkin and pie, bunny and carrot, acorn and house and more), exactly one way through
  • Word search: 8 x 8 letters, 6 Thanksgiving words per placemat (48 words in all), across and down only
  • Secret picture: color the squares row by row (Row 3: C to H) to find a pumpkin, an apple, an acorn, a leaf, a slice of pie, a heart, a house or a warm mug
  • Draw your plate: an empty plate with a fork and a knife
  • I am thankful for: lines to write or draw on
  • Pages 10 to 13: answers, two placemats per page

How the puzzles are checked

Our program builds every maze and word search and a second program checks them: each maze has exactly one path, each word appears exactly once in the grid (we check all 8 directions), the grid has no rude words hidden by chance, and each secret picture is rebuilt from the printed row instructions to make sure it matches.

Who it is for

Kids at the Thanksgiving or Friendsgiving table, school parties and family dinners. Suggested ages about 5 to 10; that is our estimate, and younger kids may need a grown up for the word search and the secret picture. Text is in English (American spelling).

Printing

Print at 100% / Actual size; the pages are already set up in landscape. Black and white, made to be colored in. You may print as many copies as you need for your family or your classroom. Please do not share or resell the file.

Questions

Before you buy.

How do I get the files?

No physical item is shipped. After Etsy confirms payment, download your files from Purchases and reviews on Etsy.com in a web browser; downloads are unavailable in the Etsy app. Guest buyers can use the download link in their Etsy receipt email.

Which files do I get?

Two files: ARLing-Thanksgiving-Kids-Placemats-A4.pdf and ARLing-Thanksgiving-Kids-Placemats-US-Letter.pdf. The same pages in A4 and US Letter, so you print the one that matches your paper.

What if a file does not open or print?

For a file or printing problem, send an Etsy Message with your order number, file name and the error you see. Do not send card or payment details.
