Recipe Cost Calculator Spreadsheet for Excel

A recipe cost calculator for Excel for home bakers and small food makers.

7.90 €

The Excel file (.xlsx) and a copy with sample data, plus a quick start guide PDF in A4 and US Letter. Works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (tested on Windows). A digital download from Etsy: nothing is shipped.

Buy on Etsy, 7.90 €

Etsy handles the payment and the instant download. Etsy may show the price in your currency and add tax for your country; it shows the final total before you pay.

Type what you pay for each pack of flour, butter or eggs, and it works out the cost of every recipe per batch and per piece, with your time, overhead and packaging, and the price for the margin you want.

What you get

The spreadsheet, a sample copy and a guide.

  • arling-recipe-cost-calculator.xlsx: the empty file, ready for your own data
  • arling-recipe-cost-calculator-sample.xlsx: the same file with sample data, so you can see every sheet working first
  • Quick start guide, PDF, 2 pages, in A4 and in US Letter size

Plain files, no ZIP.

  • Paper sizes: A4 and US Letter (the guide).
  • Files: Excel .xlsx and PDF.
  • Digital download: nothing is shipped by post. The files are in Purchases and reviews on Etsy.com after payment.

Details

6 sheets

  • Start here: the steps, with a link to every sheet
  • Pricing: Up to 50 recipes: ingredients, work, overhead, packaging, cost per batch and per piece, price for target margin, profit and margin at your price.
  • Compare: Recipes with your price ranked by margin, with bars.
  • Recipes: Up to 600 ingredient lines with the cost of each line and a check.
  • Ingredients: Up to 200 ingredients with pack size, unit and price.
  • Settings: Currency, hourly rate, overhead percent, target margin, and the unit table.

How it works

  • Ingredients: pack size, unit and price. Cost per 100 g, per 100 ml or per piece is calculated.
  • Recipes: amounts in g, kg, oz, lb, ml, l, tsp, tbsp, fl oz, cup, gal or each. Weight converts to weight and volume to volume.
  • Pricing: ingredients, work (minutes x your hourly rate), overhead (% of ingredient cost) and packaging give the cost per batch and per piece.
  • Price for your target margin = cost per piece / (1 - margin). Type your own price to see profit per piece and margin.
  • Compare ranks your recipes by margin. Check marks a line that cannot be priced, for example flour bought by weight but used in cups.

Works in

Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (tested on Windows). No macros, no add ins, no account. Not tested: Excel for Mac, Excel for the web, Google Sheets, Apple Numbers, phone and tablet apps.

How we test it

Before release both files are opened, recalculated and saved in Microsoft Excel. The sample file has 4,594 formula cells and none shows an error; 1,891 calculated cells were compared with a separate calculation written in Python and all matched, in the sample file and in the empty file. The pictures in this listing are printed from the sample file by Excel.

Good to know

  • Weight and volume are not converted into each other (a cup of flour and a cup of honey weigh differently).
  • It does not include marketplace fees, shipping or taxes.
  • Text and labels are in English.
  • For your own home or business use. Please do not share or resell the file.
  • Questions or a problem with the file: send us an Etsy Message and we will help.
