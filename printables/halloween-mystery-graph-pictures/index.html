Halloween Mystery Grid Pictures, 20 Grid Coloring Worksheets with Answer Keys

20 Halloween mystery pictures to find on a grid. Each page gives a list of squares, like (C, 4).

Children find the letter along the bottom, go up to the number, shade the square, and a picture slowly appears: a pumpkin, an owl, a lantern, an acorn, a black cat, a mushroom and more. A quiet October activity for the classroom, homeschool or a rainy afternoon.

Details

The 20 pictures

Pumpkin, bat, wrapped candy, spider, black cat, autumn leaf, star, owl, jack-o'-lantern, candy corn, sitting cat, candle, lantern, moon and bat, hot cocoa, apple, acorn, mushroom, maple leaf and sunflower. No witches, ghosts or skulls.

How the pages work

Letters run along the bottom (and again along the top), numbers up the side (and again on the right), with 1 at the bottom. The squares in each list are in order, all the A squares first, then B, and so on, so children can tick them off as they go. Each page has a line for a name and a line to write what the picture is.

Checked by a script

A script read the square lists back out of both finished PDF files and rebuilt every picture: all 20 match the answer keys square for square, with no square listed twice.

Who it is for

Our suggestion is grades 2 to 4, or younger children with a grown-up. The squares use a letter and a number, like a map grid; there is no plotting of x and y points on axes. It practises reading grid references and careful checking. It is not written to any particular curriculum or standard.

Good to know

This is a digital download. Nothing will be shipped. You get the two PDF files right after Etsy confirms your payment. Print at 100%. Print as many copies as you need for your own family or your own classroom; please do not share or resell the files.

Ten of the pictures also appear as nonogram puzzles in our Halloween Logic Puzzle Book, and a few in our Christmas puzzle books; here they are a different activity for younger children. The pictures are our own pixel drawings.
