30 easy, symmetric mosaic patterns to color square by square.

Every square with a number gets the color of that number, squares with no number stay white, and a geometric pattern appears: stars, crosses, rings, diamonds, zigzags and tiles. Details

The pages

Every square is at least 7.5 mm (about 0.3 inch) wide, with the number printed in gray. The pattern pages are black and white except a thin colored title line; apart from that, only the small color key swatches and the finished pictures at the back use color ink. Each pattern has its own color key with plain color names, such as Navy, Ochre or Sage green. The names are only a guide and are not matched to any brand of pencils or markers, so use any shades you have.

Is it for you

These are simple geometric tiles, not detailed mystery pictures of animals or scenes. If you like fine pixel art with 20 or more colors, this set will feel too easy. If you want large squares, few colors and a pattern you can finish in one sitting, it is made for that.

The 30 patterns

Village Zigzag, Evening Cross, River Hook, Village Ring, Harvest Diamond, Meadow Steps, Evening Zigzag, Evening Seeds, Sunday Star, Harvest Cross, Meadow Diamond, Orchard Hook, Candle Leaf, Meadow Cross, Candle Diamond, Forest Steps, Evening Star, Lantern Cross, Mountain Steps, Harvest Ring, Harvest Hook, Morning Star, Village Star, Meadow Zigzag, Market Ring, Garden Cross, Garden Zigzag, Evening Seeds II, Evening Tile, Harvest Ring II.

Checked by a script

A script read the numbers back out of both finished PDF files and rebuilt every pattern: all 30 match the finished pictures square for square. No two patterns are the same, even when turned or mirrored.

Good to know

The A4 file uses British spelling (colour), the US Letter file American spelling (color). Print as many copies as you need for yourself and your family; please do not share or resell the files.

The patterns are our own designs, made with our pattern generator.
