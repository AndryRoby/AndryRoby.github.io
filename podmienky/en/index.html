7SEPA file check

The SEPA file check is a one-off paid service for 149 €, a final price with no further fees and no subscription. After you pay by card through Stripe we redirect you to a page where you upload your pain.001 file. Within 24 hours of the upload we send the corrected pain.001 file and a written report to the e-mail address from the order: what needs fixing in the file, including addresses that are not structured, on which line, and exactly what to send to the vendor of your accounting software. The check is a format check; it is not tax or legal advice, and a clean result does not guarantee that the bank will accept the payment. A finding that there is nothing to fix in the file is also delivered in writing; the service is then performed and we do not refund in that case, because the work has been done.

As a consumer you also have the right to withdraw from this service within 14 days (section 5). The service, however, starts as soon as you upload the file. By ticking the box on the upload page and uploading the file you expressly agree to the following wording: “I agree that the service starts before the withdrawal period ends and I acknowledge that I lose my right of withdrawal once the corrected file and the report are delivered.” Once the service has been fully performed, that is once the corrected file and the report have been delivered, the right of withdrawal ends under Act No. 108/2024 Coll. If you withdraw before we deliver the corrected file and the report, we refund the full 149 €.

If we do not deliver the corrected file and the report within 24 hours of the upload, or if we cannot fix the file, we refund the full 149 €. Write to andrej@arling.sk. Report an error in the delivered file or in the report to the same address; we settle the complaint within 30 days at the latest. We use the uploaded file only for this check; it is stored with our processor Cloudflare for at most 30 days and then deleted automatically. Details are on the service page.

The self-service file fix for 29 € in the SEPA pain.001 Doctor tool is digital content delivered immediately: the fixes (addresses split into fields, country codes, IBAN without spaces, recalculated totals) run in your browser and the file downloads right after payment. Before paying you tick your consent to immediate delivery and acknowledge the loss of the right of withdrawal (Directive 2011/83/EU, Art. 16(m)). If the file does not download after payment or is fixed wrongly, write to andrej@arling.sk; we fix it by hand or refund.
