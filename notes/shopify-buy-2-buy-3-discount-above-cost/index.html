Buy 2, save on Shopify: a quantity discount for one product that stays above your cost

ARLing s. r. o. · 13 November 2026

A product sold one at a time leaves a simple offer unused: a lower price per item when the customer takes two or three. Shopify describes a bundle as a set of two or more related products, commonly offered at a discount, and building bundles of different products needs a bundles app. For more of the same product you don't need one: Shopify's own Amount off products discount with a minimum quantity of items does the job. Shopify applies automatic discounts in the cart and at checkout on its own, once the customer has added all eligible items. This note shows how to set up two such discounts, how to check them against your cost before you save, and the limits worth knowing first.

Before you start: cost per item and named variants

Shopify's Cost per item is how much a product or a variant costs you; for resold goods it is typically what you paid the manufacturer, without taxes or shipping. You enter it in the Price section of the product, and for a product with variants you need to add it to each variant; the bulk editor or a CSV import with the Cost per item column handles many products at once. Shopify then shows a projected profit and margin in the Price section, with margin calculated as price minus cost, divided by price, times 100. It does not show profit and margin for a product that charges tax when your store's prices include tax.

Then look at the variants. Shopify describes a variant as a combination of option values, for options such as size or color, and it lists variants, including the option name, among the product details that AI platforms and shopping sites consider. Give each option a clear name and values that tell the variants apart, because the cart test below uses two different variants of the product.

The check: every variant stays above cost

Do this on paper for each variant, because cost per item is entered per variant:

  1. Cost per item excludes shipping, so add your shipping cost per item to it.
  2. Work out the price per item after the 2-item discount and after the 3-item discount.
  3. Both prices must stay above the number from step 1.
  4. The saving per item for 3 items should be larger than for 2 items, or the 3-item offer adds nothing over the 2-item offer.

The discount steps we read have no field for your cost, so this check is yours to make.

Set up the two discounts

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Discounts, click Create discount and select Amount off products.
  2. Under Method, select Automatic discount.
  3. Give it a title, for example the product name with Buy 2.
  4. In Discount value, choose a percentage or a fixed amount. With a fixed amount, Shopify also offers Only apply discount once per order, which limits the number of times the discount applies; otherwise the discount applies to all eligible products in the order, so leave it unchecked if you mean the amount off each item, and confirm it in the cart test below.
  5. Under Applies to, select Specific products and add the product.
  6. Set the minimum requirement to Minimum quantity of items and enter 2.
  7. In Combinations, leave the product discount classes unchecked.
  8. Set the start date in Active dates and click Save.

Repeat with a minimum of 3 and a larger value. With 3 items in the cart, both discounts fit; when discounts don't combine, Shopify always applies the best discount for the customer's cart. Then test in your own cart with 1, 2 and 3 items, and also with two different variants of the product, because the help pages we read do not say how the minimum counts mixed variants.

Limits worth knowing first

  • A store can have at most 25 active automatic discounts, app-based discounts included. Two per product means at most 12 products with this offer, if you run no other automatic discounts.
  • You can add several products to one discount to save slots, but Shopify says only that the listed items count toward the minimum quantity. It does not say they have to be the same product, so one item each of two products may qualify too, which is no longer a discount on more of the same product; test that in your cart before you rely on it.
  • Automatic discounts apply in the cart and at checkout, and only when the customer has added all eligible items. A shopper who does not know about the offer has no reason to add the second item, so state it on the product page: a text block in the main section of your product template, added in the theme editor, is enough.
  • Two product discounts on the same line item can combine only on Shopify Plus.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a bundles app for buy 2, save?

Not for more of the same product: an automatic Amount off products discount with a minimum quantity of items covers it. To build bundles of different products, Shopify says you need a bundles app, and if you sell through Google & YouTube, that channel supports fixed product bundles only.

Why doesn't the 3-item discount add to the 2-item discount?

When discounts don't combine, Shopify applies the best one for the customer's cart. Combining two product discounts on the same line item is available only on Shopify Plus.

Will Shopify stop a discount that goes below my cost?

The discount steps we read have no cost field. Shopify shows projected profit and margin in the Price section once you enter a cost per item, unless the product charges tax and your prices include tax. Either way, the check above is up to you.
