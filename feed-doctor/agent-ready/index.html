Can shopping agents read your store?

Find gaps in your public product data, product pages and store policies. Get a checklist with sources and specific fixes.

The same check also looks for three EU rules of 2026: the withdrawal function, the notice on the legal guarantee and generic environmental claims. All EU dates for online shops in 2026 and 2027.

Not Shopify, or a specific product?

Reads public pages only: robots.txt, the home page, up to 20 products from the public export, the sitemap (at most 2 files and 1 redirect, only the beginning of each, up to 1 MB), up to 3 product pages (while looking for them, also at most 2 pages that turn out not to be product pages), policy and contact pages, and an optional llms.txt. On product pages it reads JSON-LD, and Microdata as a fallback when a page has no JSON-LD Product or ProductGroup. Cannot see your private catalog, non-public barcodes, categories or alt text, or how AI assistants behave. Limit: 6 checks per hour per IP, counted by an IP hash, never by the store address. The check needs no email and does not save page contents. If you ask below the result for it to be emailed to you, we save the store address with the result in our database until it is sent; a copy of the email stays in our mailbox. We keep no request logs with the store address; Cloudflare, which hosts our server, keeps only error messages, for up to 7 days.

  1. Enter a public store address. Add a product URL for another platform.
  2. Read the score together with coverage and the exact pages checked.
  3. Fix the evidence gaps, then repeat the same sample.

What the result means

We inspect up to 20 public feed products and 3 product pages. Other shops can supply a product URL. The score is the percentage of passed weights among checks we could evaluate. Coverage is the share of scoring weights evaluated. Missing public fields can differ from your private catalog.

EU rules 2026

Three findings outside the score: the withdrawal function (since 19 June 2026), the notice on the legal guarantee and generic environmental claims (since 27 September 2026). For each we say what we found or did not find in the same pages, with the article of the law. They carry no weight in the score and are not legal advice.

What this check cannot establish

No JavaScript rendering, private Shopify Catalog, Merchant Center settings, real bot network access, review authenticity, legal compliance or checkout tests. Description length is a heuristic, not a measure of writing quality. Optional llms.txt, ratings and training-bot preferences carry no penalty.

Before you change your store

Does a high score mean ChatGPT will recommend my products?

No. This tool checks observable data. It does not test ranking, inclusion, paid ads or buyer demand.

Should I unblock every AI bot?

No. Search crawlers, training crawlers and user-triggered fetchers have different roles. Review each preference against its documentation and your policy.

Why are barcode or alt-text checks not verified?

The public Shopify export may omit those fields even when they exist in your admin. Verify the private catalog before changing it.

Does the tool verify customer reviews?

No. It can inspect published rating numbers. Only you can substantiate the underlying reviews. Never add invented ratings.

Does the check tell me whether my shop is legal?

No. The three EU findings say only what we found in the public pages we read, with the article of the law. They cannot see your checkout, customer account or images, and they are not legal advice.
