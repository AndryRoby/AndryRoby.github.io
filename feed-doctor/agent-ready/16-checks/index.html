The 16 checks of Agentic Product Check: a Shopify product data checklist

ARLing s. r. o. · Rules from our methodology version 2026-10-16 · Shopify admin paths checked 19 October 2026

Agentic Product Check, our Shopify app, runs these 16 checks on every active and unlisted product in your admin, with one permission: read products. The app is waiting for Shopify App Store approval and cannot be installed yet, so this page lists the checks for you to run by hand. Each check says where it comes from: a Shopify Catalog requirement, a catalog risk, a field Shopify recommends, or our own heuristic.

Passing all 16 does not guarantee a place in Shopify Catalog or in any AI answer. Shopify also reviews your store's eligibility and classifies each product itself, and other factors of each AI platform affect the results. Weights, thresholds and what the app cannot see are in How we count.

Shopify Catalog requirements

A product that fails one of these may be left out of Shopify Catalog.

  1. Product title

    Fails when: The product title is empty.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: In Products, open the product and fill in Title in the Title and Description section.

    Source: Shopify Catalog requirements

  2. Product image

    Fails when: The product has no image with an https address.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: Add at least one image in the Media section of the product.

    Source: Shopify Catalog requirements

  3. Price above zero

    Fails when: A variant has a price of zero or less, or a price that is not a number, or the product has no variants.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: Set a price greater than zero for every variant, in the Pricing section or on each variant.

    Source: Shopify Catalog requirements

  4. Published to the online store

    Fails when: The product is not published to the online store sales channel. The app cannot see Hydrogen or headless storefronts.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: In the Publishing section of the product, which lists your sales channels and markets, include the online store.

    Source: Shopify Catalog requirements

  5. Not unlisted

    Fails when: The product status is Unlisted. Unlisted products open only from a direct link and do not meet the catalog requirements.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: In the Product status section, change the status from Unlisted to Active.

    Source: Shopify Catalog requirements, product details page

  6. Not hidden from search engines

    Fails when: The seo.hidden metafield is set to 1, which hides the product from search engines.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: Shopify's way back: delete the value of the SEO Hidden metafield, leave the field empty and save. How to find these products: our guide to seo.hidden.

    Source: Shopify Catalog requirements, hiding a page from search engines

Catalog risk

Not a requirement, but a known risk.

  1. Category set

    Fails when: No category is set, so the product may be classified as Uncategorized, which the catalog excludes. Shopify's own classification can still differ from the category you set.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: Choose a standard product category in the Category field of the product (Shopify's Standard Product Taxonomy).

    Source: Shopify Catalog requirements

Fields Shopify recommends

Shopify names these among the product details that AI platforms and shopping sites consider.

  1. Product type

    Fails when: The product type field is empty. It is a recommended field, separate from the category.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: Fill in Type in the Product organization section; for many products at once, see our guide to filling product type in bulk.

    Source: Optimizing your products for AI platforms, product types

  2. Vendor or brand

    Fails when: The vendor field is empty.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: Fill in Vendor in the Product organization section with the true manufacturer or brand. Never invent one.

    Source: Optimizing your products for AI platforms

  3. Named variant options

    Fails when: A product with several variants has no named options, an option named Title, an option without values, or a variant without an option value.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: In the Variants section, name every option and give it values that tell the variants apart.

    Source: Optimizing your products for AI platforms

  4. Description

    Fails when: The product has no description text.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: Write the Description: material, size and use. To find every product without one, see our guide.

    Source: Optimizing your products for AI platforms, product descriptions

  5. Valid barcode

    Fails when: A barcode has an invalid GTIN check digit. A missing barcode is not an error. This check is shown as an issue but does not count in the score.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: In the Inventory section, correct the barcode from the manufacturer, or remove it. If the manufacturer assigned one, add it; never invent one. More in our guide to GTIN, MPN and brand.

    Source: Shopify developer docs: the variant object

Our own heuristics

Our own checks with our own thresholds, not platform requirements.

  1. Title of 3 characters or more

    Fails when: The title has fewer than 3 characters, after spaces at the start and end are removed.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: Give the product a descriptive title, not just a code.

    Source: related: Optimizing your products for AI platforms

  2. Description of 80 characters or more

    Fails when: The description has fewer than 80 characters of plain text, after HTML tags are removed and entities decoded.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: Add material, size and use details to the description.

    Source: related: product descriptions

  3. Image alt text

    Fails when: The first product image has no alt text.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: Open the product, click its first media item, then Add alt text and Save alt text. Shopify recommends brief, descriptive alt text of 125 characters or less.

    Source: related: adding alt text to media

  4. In a collection

    Fails when: The product is not in any collection.

    Fix in your Shopify admin: Add the product to at least one collection in the Collections field of the Product organization section.

    Source: related: Optimizing your products for AI platforms

Check what is visible from the outside

Our free public check needs no install and no email. It reads up to 20 products from your public product export and up to 3 product pages, so it shows several of these checks from the outside: titles, images, prices, product type, vendor, variant options and descriptions. Categories, metafields such as seo.hidden, barcodes the export leaves out and image alt text stay outside it.
