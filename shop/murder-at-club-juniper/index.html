How the evening works.

A murder mystery dinner at home in four steps: three rounds over dinner, the host plays along, and nobody has to act.

  1. Invite. Once the date is set, send the general invitation. About a week before, each guest gets the invitation for their role, with a few lines about the character and a costume tip, by e-mail or printed.
  2. Arrive. On the night, you read the introduction aloud, about ten minutes: Chicago 1927, the club, the guests and the dead man. The plan of the club goes in the middle of the table.
  3. Three rounds over dinner. With the appetizer, the main course and dessert, everyone opens the next part of their booklet and gives their statement. Then the clue cards for the round go on the table, and everyone questions everyone.
  4. Accusation and solution. After the third round, everyone writes down in silence who did it, how and why. Then the host opens the envelope with the solution and reads it aloud.

Whoever hosts plays a role too and does not know the solution beforehand. If the table gets stuck, three hints wait in the “Help” envelope; you open them one at a time, in order.

A murder mystery party for New Year’s Eve.

At Club Juniper the police arrive at midnight, which suits New Year’s Eve: by our estimate of 3 to 3.5 hours, if you start around 8:30 p.m., you reach the solution between about 11:30 p.m. and midnight. A meal that keeps everyone at the table works best, such as a fondue or a buffet set out before the first round.

Dress code: 1920s evening wear, so sequins, bow ties and feathers. For the toast, the PDF also has an alcohol-free “Juniper Fizz.”

A murder mystery party for Christmas.

A holiday dinner often has three courses, and the game has three rounds: with the appetizer, the main course and dessert. That makes it a fit for an evening between Christmas and New Year’s with friends or family, recommended for ages 16 and up.

As a gift: buy the PDF, print the general invitation and put it under the tree. The game is not made for an office party of 20 or more; it is for 6 to 8 people.

Before you buy.

How long does the murder mystery dinner take?

About 3 to 3.5 hours with dinner, our estimate. The number comes from our schedule for the evening (introduction, three rounds over three courses, accusation and solution), not from a timed game: the game is new, and your table may be faster or slower. For preparation with printing, allow about 1 to 2 hours, also an estimate.

What do I need to prepare?

Print the PDF on A4 or US Letter paper; black and white is fine. Print the beginning of each role booklet double-sided, each round single-sided so you can fold and seal it, and the cards single-sided to cut out. The cards for each round go into an envelope, the three hints into the “Help” envelope and the solution into one of its own; templates for the envelopes are included. Send the general invitation as soon as the date is set, and the role invitations about a week before. The menu suggestions can be prepared the day before or in the afternoon, so on the night all that is left is plating, warming up and pouring. Nobody has to memorize anything.

Which file do I print, A4 or US Letter?

Both are included in the price, with the same content. In the United States and Canada, US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) fits the paper in your printer; most other countries use A4 (210 x 297 mm).
