The Dragon Who Slept on the Key.

An escape room you print at home. Crumble the dragon has fallen asleep on the key to the toy cupboard, and the Winter Toy Fair opens at five. Only Grandpa Tobias’s music box can wake him, and Grandpa has locked it inside a chain of 8 puzzles. Every answer opens the next envelope.

What you get

Two PDF files of the same 16 pages, one A4 and one US Letter. You print them, fill 8 envelopes and play at a birthday party, a sleepover, in class or on a rainy afternoon. Nothing is shipped.

7.90 €one payment, VAT included

How the apprentices play.

Four steps, repeated for each of the 8 puzzles. A grown-up reads the story aloud and holds the envelopes; the children do the rest.

  1. Solve a card. Each card is one puzzle. The first two are a coded note and a maze.
  2. Look it up. Every answer on The Key Ring has a picture. Some pictures belong to no envelope at all.
  3. Ask for the envelope. If the picture is the one on top of your pile, they get it. If not, Crumble is still snoring, so they check their work or take a hint ticket (with two teams, see page 12 of the kit).
  4. Wake Crumble. They open the music box, sing the Wake-Up Song and get the key to the toy cupboard, where you hid a small treat.

Before you buy.

What age is it for?

Children aged 7 to 10. 7 year olds may need a grown-up to help with the code wheel. Quick 9 and 10 year olds who love puzzles will be at the short end of the time. It is not made for 11 and up: some cards take an older child only a minute or two.

Does the grown-up need to know the answers?

No. The children find their answer on The Key Ring page and ask for the envelope with that picture. You keep the envelopes in a pile in the order of a one page table and hand over only the top one. If they ask for another picture, the answer is not right yet (with two teams, see page 12 of the kit).

To be honest, children can still ask for pictures at random and sometimes get lucky, and skip a puzzle that way. You hold the envelopes, so you see it when it happens.

Can I print it in black and white?

It is designed for that: every shape that matters differs in form, not only in colour. We checked this on the pages, not yet on a real black and white printer. If anything is hard to read, write to support@arling.sk and we fix it or send the money back.

Has it been played with real children?

Not yet, and we would rather say so. Every page was checked at print size by our own check script, and the maze, the code, the logic puzzle and the boat pieces were checked by a computer. The counting and mirror puzzles cannot be checked that way, so we checked that each answer appears on The Key Ring exactly once.

What happens after I pay?

Stripe sends you back to this page, the page checks that the payment went through, and download buttons for the A4 and US Letter PDF appear in place of the buy button. The same links are sent by e-mail to the address you paid with. The links on this page work for 7 days and the ones in the e-mail for 30 days; after that, write to support@arling.sk and we send fresh ones.

The unlock is remembered in this browser, so you can come back to this page later on the same device. Keep the files once you have downloaded them.

How many copies may I print?

As many as you need for your own family, party, classroom or club. Please do not share or resell the files.

Who made it?

ARLing s. r. o. in Bratislava wrote the story and the puzzles and drew the pictures, with the help of AI tools. The drawings are simple shapes drawn in code, and our own program typesets every page.

Do I get a receipt?

Yes. Stripe handles the sale through Managed Payments as the merchant of record, and ARLing supplies the files. At checkout you will see “Sold through Link”, your bank statement will show LINK.COM* ARLING, and Link sends you the receipt.

Can I get my money back?

By law, no, and we would rather say it before you pay. Digital content delivered immediately with your consent carries no right of withdrawal: in Slovakia that is section 19 paragraph 1 letter m) of Act No. 108/2024 Coll. on consumer protection, and across the EU Article 16 letter m) of Directive 2011/83/EU. Clicking the payment button is that consent, because the files are shown at once.

That is the law, not our customer service. All sixteen pages are shown above so you can decide before paying. If a file is broken or never arrived, write to support@arling.sk and we will fix it or send the money back. Terms and withdrawal form.
