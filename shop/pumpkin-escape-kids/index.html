The Pumpkin Fair Mix-Up.

An escape room you print at home for a Halloween or autumn party. Big Marigold, the biggest pumpkin at the Maple Hill Pumpkin Fair, has gone missing with her red wagon before the contest. Nobody took her to be unkind: somebody got mixed up. The children play 7 fair games in a chain, and every answer opens the next card.

What you get

Two PDF files of the same 16 pages, one A4 and one US Letter. You print them, fold the seven card pages and play at a Halloween party, a birthday, a sleepover, in class or on a rainy afternoon. Nothing is shipped.

How the Fair Helpers play.

Four steps, repeated for each of the 7 puzzles. A grown-up reads the story aloud and holds the pile of cards; the children do the rest.

  1. Solve a card. Each card is one fair game. The first one is Olive’s note in picture code.
  2. Look it up. Every answer on Olive’s Answer Board has a picture. Some pictures belong to no card at all.
  3. Ask for the card. If the picture is the one on top of your pile, they get it. If not, they check their work or you read them a hint.
  4. Find Big Marigold. The last card tells the end of the story and sends them to the treat you hid.

Before you buy.

What age is it for?

Children aged 6 to 10. Six and seven year olds need someone to read the cards and the Answer Board with them. Quick nine and ten year olds who love puzzles will be at the short end of the time; it is not made for 11 and up.

Is it scary?

No. It is a Halloween and autumn party game without anything spooky: a pumpkin fair, pies, paper lanterns and a costume parade (an astronaut, a knight, a pumpkin and a bumblebee). The missing pumpkin was taken by mistake, and everything ends well.

Does the grown-up need to know the answers?

No. The children find their answer on Olive’s Answer Board and ask for the card with that picture. You keep the folded cards in a pile and hand over only the top one. If they ask for another picture, the answer is not right yet.

To be honest, children can still ask for pictures at random and sometimes get lucky; you hold the pile, so you see it when it happens.

Do I need envelopes or scissors?

Not to play. Each card folds once so only its picture shows. The badges and certificates need scissors if you want to cut them out.

Can I print it in black and white?

It is designed for that. Every shape that matters differs in form, not only in colour; we have not yet tried a real black and white printer. If anything is hard to read, write to support@arling.sk and we fix it or send the money back.

Has it been played with real children?

Not yet, and we would rather say so. The times are our estimate. The picture code, the maze, the logic puzzle, the sticker puzzle and the Answer Board were checked by a computer. The counting and spot the difference pictures were checked by counting the drawn objects in the files.

What happens after I pay?

Stripe sends you back to this page, the page checks that the payment went through, and download buttons for the A4 and US Letter PDF appear in place of the buy button. The same links are sent by e-mail to the address you paid with. The links on this page work for 7 days and the ones in the e-mail for 30 days; after that, write to support@arling.sk and we send fresh ones.

The unlock is remembered in this browser, so you can come back to this page later on the same device. Keep the files once you have downloaded them.

How many copies may I print?

As many as you need for your own family, party, classroom or club. Please do not share or resell the files.

Who made it?

ARLing s. r. o. in Bratislava wrote the story and the puzzles and drew the pictures, with the help of AI tools. The drawings are simple shapes drawn in code, and our own program typesets every page.

Do I get a receipt?

Yes. Stripe handles the sale through Managed Payments as the merchant of record, and ARLing supplies the files. At checkout you will see “Sold through Link”, your bank statement will show LINK.COM* ARLING, and Link sends you the receipt.

Can I get my money back?

By law, no, and we would rather say it before you pay. Digital content delivered immediately with your consent carries no right of withdrawal: in Slovakia that is section 19 paragraph 1 letter m) of Act No. 108/2024 Coll. on consumer protection, and across the EU Article 16 letter m) of Directive 2011/83/EU. Clicking the payment button is that consent, because the files are shown at once.

That is the law, not our customer service. All sixteen pages are shown above so you can decide before paying. If a file is broken or never arrived, write to support@arling.sk and we will fix it or send the money back. Terms and withdrawal form.
