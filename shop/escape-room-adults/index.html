The Clockmaker’s Workshop.

An escape game you print, cut and play at home. Master clockmaker Ottoline Wren has gone into hiding, and her workshop door has locked behind you. For a year a dealer has been selling clocks with her mark that she never made. Solve ten puzzles to find her proof of the forgery, then work out where she is waiting.

What you get

Two PDF files of the same 24 pages, one A4 and one US Letter. You print them on plain paper, cut out a paper decoder, a grille, a stencil and a torn receipt, fold 18 answer cards (no glue) and play at a table. Nothing is shipped.

How it plays.

Every answer is three digits, and a paper decoder tells you whether it is right. Three puzzles are open from the start, so a group can split up.

  1. Solve a puzzle. Each one ends in three digits.
  2. Set the Regulator. Turn the three paper wheels to your digits and read three letters at twelve.
  3. Find the card. The right card tells you what opens next and gives you a letter. No card, or a different symbol, means the answer is wrong, and a few cards explain common mistakes.
  4. Open the lock. With all ten letters, one last gear train gives you the numbers for the door. Then read the last page.

Before you buy.

How much preparation does it take?

Our estimate is about 30 to 45 minutes before the first game; nobody has timed it yet. That is more than a game you only print, on purpose: the decoder, the grille, the watch, the stencil and the receipt are paper objects you cut and handle. There is no gluing: each answer card stays joined at its fold. It is best done by someone who is not playing.

Can I prepare it alone and still play it fresh?

Mostly. The backs of the answer cards are printed upside down and the correct cards do not show their answer, so you are unlikely to read one by accident while folding. We cannot make that impossible, though, so if someone else can fold the cards, let them.

Has it been played?

Not yet at a real table, and here that matters more than in a detective kit, so we say it plainly. What a computer checked: every answer, every card code, that each puzzle has only one answer, and the finished PDF itself. In the PDF, text on every piece you cut out sits at least 4 mm inside its cutting line and away from the edge of the wheel that lies on it, except the torn receipt, whose text runs across the tears on purpose (its job number is about 10 mm tall, and a cut 2 mm off the line still leaves at least two thirds of each digit), no text is smaller than 6.5 pt, and the stencil can sit up to 2.5 mm off and still show the whole digit. We built it so that nothing depends on paper weight, light shining through, glue or a perfectly straight cut. What no one has done yet is print it and play it as a group, so the times are our estimates. If anything does not work at your table, write to support@arling.sk and we fix it or send the money back.

Can I print it in black and white?

It is designed for that: no puzzle uses colour as a clue. We rendered every page of the PDF in greyscale and measured each text against its background: every one keeps a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1. That is a measurement of the file, not of a real print, and it covers text, not drawings; the pale gear wheels in puzzle II have a darker outline for that reason. If anything prints too faint, write to support@arling.sk and we fix it or send the money back.

What happens after I pay?

Stripe sends you back to this page, the page checks that the payment went through, and download buttons for the A4 and US Letter PDF appear in place of the buy button. The same links are sent by e-mail to the address you paid with. The links on this page work for 7 days and the ones in the e-mail for 30 days; after that, write to support@arling.sk and we send fresh ones.

The unlock is remembered in this browser, so you can come back to this page later on the same device. Keep the files once you have downloaded them.

How many copies may I print?

As many as you need for your own home, family or friends. Please do not share or resell the files.

Who made it?

ARLing s. r. o. in Bratislava wrote the story and the puzzles and drew the pictures, with the help of AI tools. The drawings are simple shapes drawn in code, and our own program typesets every page.

Do I get a receipt?

Yes. Stripe handles the sale through Managed Payments as the merchant of record, and ARLing supplies the files. At checkout you will see “Sold through Link”, your bank statement will show LINK.COM* ARLING, and Link sends you the receipt.

Can I get my money back?

By law, no, and we would rather say it before you pay. Digital content delivered immediately with your consent carries no right of withdrawal: in Slovakia that is section 19 paragraph 1 letter m) of Act No. 108/2024 Coll. on consumer protection, and across the EU Article 16 letter m) of Directive 2011/83/EU. Clicking the payment button is that consent, because the files are shown at once.

That is the law, not our customer service. All 24 pages are shown above so you can decide before paying. If a file is broken or never arrived, write to support@arling.sk and we will fix it or send the money back. Terms and withdrawal form.
