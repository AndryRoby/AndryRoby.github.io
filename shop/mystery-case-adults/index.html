The Ashgrove Heron.

A case file you print at home. October 1964: during a power cut at a private view in a country house, someone took a painting out of its frame and hung a copy in its place. Six guests had the chance. The auctioneer has sent you everything he knows, from letters and a cloakroom ledger to statements and a photograph. Work out who swapped the painting, how the original left the house, and why.

What you get

Two PDF files of the same 21 pages, one A4 and one US Letter. You print them at home, with no cutting, and play alone, as a couple or with friends around a table. Nothing is shipped.

9.90 €one payment, VAT included

VAT included, nothing added at checkout. You buy the two files once, keep them and print a copy for everyone at your table.

  • A4 and US Letter, both in the price

  • One answer, checked by a program. A computer solver confirmed that the logic grid has exactly one answer and that all 14 pieces of evidence are needed to reach it.
  • The fair play rule. Every statement and document in the file is true. The guilty guest did not lie; they simply did not tell the whole story.
  • No violence. Nobody is hurt: the crime is a theft, and the painting is safe and unharmed. All people, places and firms are invented.

How you solve it.

Four steps. Pages 19 to 21 go in an envelope before you start, so the hints and the answer stay out of sight.

  1. Read the file. A letter from the auctioneer, a timeline of Friday evening, witness statements, a cloakroom ledger and period papers.
  2. Put the papers together. Match a photograph to the floor plan, decode a dealer’s price code in his notebook and check the train times.
  3. Fill in the grid. Guests, rooms, items and times. Cross out and tick until one guest is left in the Long Gallery at 9:12.
  4. Open the envelope. Hints in three levels if you are stuck, a sealed letter that tells what really happened, and the full solution step by step.

Before you buy.

What kind of mystery is it?

A logic mystery in the form of a case file. The answer comes from putting the documents together on a logic grid, not from guessing a motive: every guest has a reason, and only one of them was in the gallery. If you want a spooky story or a murder, this is not it. Nobody is hurt; the crime is a theft, and the painting is safe and unharmed.

How many can play, and how long does it take?

1 to 6 players, age 14 and up. About 45 to 90 minutes; alone, allow about an hour. Getting ready takes about 10 minutes and no cutting. Stuck? Page 19 has hints in three levels, from a nudge to the full step.

Has it been played?

Not yet at a real table, and we would rather say so. The logic was checked by a computer solver, not by players, which is why the time is our estimate. One clue asks you to compare a photograph with the floor plan; if that one does not click, the hints name it. If anything does not work for you, write to support@arling.sk and we fix it or send the money back.

What happens after I pay?

Stripe sends you back to this page, the page checks that the payment went through, and download buttons for the A4 and US Letter PDF appear in place of the buy button. The same links are sent by e-mail to the address you paid with. The links on this page work for 7 days and the ones in the e-mail for 30 days; after that, write to support@arling.sk and we send fresh ones.

The unlock is remembered in this browser, so you can come back to this page later on the same device. Keep the files once you have downloaded them.

How many copies may I print?

As many as you need for your own household, club or classroom. Please do not share or resell the files.

Who made it?

ARLing s. r. o. in Bratislava wrote the story and the puzzle and drew the pictures, with the help of AI tools. The drawings are simple shapes drawn in code, and our own program typesets every page. All people, places and firms in the story are invented.

Do I get a receipt?

Yes. Stripe handles the sale through Managed Payments as the merchant of record, and ARLing supplies the files. At checkout you will see “Sold through Link”, your bank statement will show LINK.COM* ARLING, and Link sends you the receipt.

Can I get my money back?

By law, no, and we would rather say it before you pay. Digital content delivered immediately with your consent carries no right of withdrawal: in Slovakia that is section 19 paragraph 1 letter m) of Act No. 108/2024 Coll. on consumer protection, and across the EU Article 16 letter m) of Directive 2011/83/EU. Clicking the payment button is that consent, because the files are shown at once.

That is the law, not our customer service. All 21 pages are shown above so you can decide before paying. If a file is broken or never arrived, write to support@arling.sk and we will fix it or send the money back. Terms and withdrawal form.
