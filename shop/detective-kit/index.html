The Case of the Missing Birthday Cake.

A detective party you print at home. Granny Badger's honey cake has vanished from the kitchen windowsill just before her birthday tea. Five helpers were in the garden, and only one of them was at the kitchen window at ten past three. Your detectives hunt for eight clues, crack a secret note with a code wheel and fill in a logic grid until only one name is left.

What you get

Two PDF files of the same 15 pages, one A4 and one US Letter. You print them at home, cut out the cards and play at a birthday party, a sleepover, in class or on a rainy afternoon. Nothing is shipped.

6.90 €one payment, VAT included

VAT included, nothing added at checkout. You buy the two files once, keep them and print as many copies as your family, party or class needs.

How the detectives play.

Four steps, about an hour. A grown-up reads the start page and hides the clue cards; the children do the rest.

  1. Hunt for the evidence. Eight cards hide around the room, and each one rules somebody in or out.
  2. Crack Dot’s code. Three warm-up codes first, then the code wheel decodes the secret note.
  3. Fill in the grid. Cross out and tick until one helper is left at the kitchen window. Four hint tickets help a team that is stuck.
  4. The reveal. Read the top secret letter aloud and everyone goes to find the cake. The letter says it is in the pantry, so hide the real cake under a big bowl there, or wherever you keep it.

Before you buy.

What age is it for?

Children aged 8 to 12 can play it on their own in teams. Younger brothers and sisters can join a team with an older child or a grown-up: nobody is hurt in the story and there are no scary scenes.

How many children can play, and how long does it take?

2 to 12 players, or 1 child with a grown-up. With 5 or more children, play in teams of 2 to 4. The game takes 45 to 60 minutes, plus about 20 minutes to print and cut, or about 5 minutes with the no scissors version on page 12.

Has it been played at a real party?

Not yet, and we would rather say so. Every page was checked at print size by our own check script, which measures every page and makes sure no text runs off a card or the page, and the logic grid was checked by a computer solver. If anything does not work at your table, write to support@arling.sk and we fix it or send the money back.

What happens after I pay?

Stripe sends you back to this page, the page checks that the payment went through, and download buttons for the A4 and US Letter PDF appear in place of the buy button. The same links are sent by e-mail to the address you paid with. The links on this page work for 7 days and the ones in the e-mail for 30 days; after that, write to support@arling.sk and we send fresh ones.

The unlock is remembered in this browser, so you can come back to this page later on the same device. Keep the files once you have downloaded them.

How many copies may I print?

As many as you need for your own family, party, classroom or club. Please do not share or resell the files.

Who made it?

ARLing s. r. o. in Bratislava wrote the story and the puzzle and drew the pictures, with the help of AI tools. The drawings are simple shapes drawn in code, and our own program typesets every page.

Do I get a receipt?

Yes. Stripe handles the sale through Managed Payments as the merchant of record, and ARLing supplies the files. At checkout you will see “Sold through Link”, your bank statement will show LINK.COM* ARLING, and Link sends you the receipt.

Can I get my money back?

By law, no, and we would rather say it before you pay. Digital content delivered immediately with your consent carries no right of withdrawal: in Slovakia that is section 19 paragraph 1 letter m) of Act No. 108/2024 Coll. on consumer protection, and across the EU Article 16 letter m) of Directive 2011/83/EU. Clicking the payment button is that consent, because the files are shown at once.

That is the law, not our customer service. All fifteen pages are shown above so you can decide before paying. If a file is broken or never arrived, write to support@arling.sk and we will fix it or send the money back. Terms and withdrawal form.
