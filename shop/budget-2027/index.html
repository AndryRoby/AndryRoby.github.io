2027 Budget Spreadsheet.

Type what you spend in one plain table. The dashboard, the monthly plan, the charts and the Data check update on their own. Debt payoff shows snowball and avalanche side by side, with real interest, and tells you the month you are debt free.

What you get

Two Excel files and a guide: a blank file ready for your numbers, the same file with an example household filled in from January to September so you can see every sheet with real numbers first, and a 3 page quick start guide (PDF). Open them in Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer, or in Google Sheets. Nothing is shipped.

9.90 €one payment, VAT included

VAT included, nothing added at checkout. You buy the files once and keep them; next year change the year in Settings and start again.

How it works.

Set it up once, then only type your payments. Everything else follows.

  1. Settings, once. Your year, the first month, your currency symbol, how often you are paid and up to 4 people.
  2. Plan and bills, once. Your categories and how much you plan for each, and your bills and subscriptions, which repeat on their own.
  3. Type what you spend. One row per payment in Transactions, the category from a drop down list. A refund is a minus amount.
  4. Read the result. The Dashboard, Monthly, Annual and the charts update on their own, and the Data check says All good or tells you what to fix.

Snowball or avalanche, with interest.

Up to 10 debts with their interest rate and minimum payment. Both plans pay every minimum and put the rest on one debt at a time; the sheet shows the month you are debt free and the total interest for each plan.

Thirteen sheets.

Only Transactions is for daily typing. The rest you set up once, update monthly, or it is calculated.

Before you buy.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Import the file (File, Import, Upload) or use the Make a copy link in the quick start guide. We imported it into Google Sheets and compared every calculated number with Excel: 34,720 number cells matched. We have not tested Excel for Mac, Excel for the web, phone and tablet apps or Apple Numbers yet.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. Nothing is connected. You type each payment in Transactions, or paste rows from your bank's export into its columns. The file never leaves your computer or your Google account.

What happens after I pay?

Stripe sends you back to this page, the page checks that the payment went through, and download buttons for the two Excel files and the guide appear in place of the buy button. The same links are sent by e-mail to the address you paid with. The links on the page work for 7 days and the ones in the e-mail for 30 days.

Can I get my money back?

As a consumer you can withdraw within 14 days without giving a reason (our terms, section 5), and we refund you within 14 days. If a file is broken or does not do what this page says, write to support@arling.sk and we fix it or send the money back.

Can I use it next year?

Yes. Change the year in Settings and clear Transactions; the guide lists every step. Any first month works too, so the year can start in July if yours does.

Is it financial advice?

No. The file is a tool for your own records. It adds up what you type and shows the result; decisions stay yours.

Do I get a receipt?

Yes. Stripe handles the sale through Managed Payments as the merchant of record, and ARLing supplies the files. At checkout you will see “Sold through Link”, your bank statement will show LINK.COM* ARLING, and Link sends you the receipt.
