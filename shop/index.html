Good to know

Before you buy.

Questions about a product?
andrej@arling.sk

What do I receive?

These are digital products, so nothing is shipped. One-time book purchases unlock download links on the product page after payment. Puzzle Post and Bulletin deliver new puzzles by e-mail. Memory Post sends a weekly question and a monthly book. Puzzle Studio is a browser tool with a free allowance and paid plans.

Can I try before buying?

The book pages linked above offer free PDF samples. Puzzle Studio lets you make two puzzles a day for free. Try an e-ink sample on your own device before buying. The daily browser puzzles are free too.

Is this a one-time purchase or a subscription?

Each offer is labelled. The downloadable books and Puzzle Planner are one-time purchases. Puzzle Post, Memory Post, Puzzle Studio and Bulletin offer monthly or yearly subscriptions. See the product page for its plans; existing subscriptions can be managed from Account.

Will the PDFs work on my device?

The e-ink PDFs are sized 157 x 210 mm. We have not tested them on physical e-ink devices. Download a free sample and check the links and writing on your own tablet first. Most titles also include A4 and US Letter files; the format is shown beside each offer.

Is VAT included?

Yes. Prices on this page include VAT. Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. is the merchant of record: it issues the receipt and settles the VAT, and ARLing supplies the files. If buying for a company, enter your VAT number at checkout.

What if I need help with a purchase?

If a download is missing or a file is broken, contact andrej@arling.sk with your order details. We will help fix the problem or arrange a refund where appropriate. Withdrawal and refund conditions are set out in our terms and the information shown at checkout. Withdrawal form.
