Questions

Before you buy.

Is it cheaper here than on Etsy?

No, the price is the same in both places. The difference is on our side: Stripe keeps 1.5 percent plus 0.25 € of a payment, Etsy about 10 percent, so buying here leaves more of your money with the people who made the book.

How is a file delivered?

On arling.sk the download links appear on the title page as soon as Stripe confirms the payment, with no account and no e-mail address. The two subscriptions are the exception: Puzzle Post issues arrive by e-mail every month and Puzzle Post Bulletin sheets every Monday, because a new file is sent each time. On Etsy the files are in your Etsy downloads.

The unlock is remembered in the browser you paid in, so you can come back to the page later on the same device. Keep the files once you have downloaded them.

Is VAT included?

Yes. Every price on this page is the final price, VAT included, and nothing is added at checkout. Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. is the merchant of record: it issues the receipt and settles the VAT, and ARLing supplies the files. If you are buying for a company, enter your VAT number in the checkout.

Can I try before paying?

Yes. Every title has a free sample: five puzzles from each printable puzzle book, ten pages of one of the e-ink puzzle books, days 1 to 7 of Morning Quiet, the first three chapters of each novel, ten puzzles from Puzzle Post and a whole real week of Puzzle Post Bulletin. The daily logic games are free as well.

Which devices are these made for?

The e-ink files are 157 x 210 mm, the 3:4 proportion of the reMarkable 2, reMarkable Paper Pro, Kindle Scribe, Boox Note Air and Supernote A5 X2. The puzzle books are made for paper, A4 and US Letter. We have not opened any of the files on a physical e-ink device, which is why every title has a free sample.

Can I get my money back?

By law, no. Digital content delivered immediately with your consent carries no right of withdrawal: in Slovakia that is section 19 paragraph 1 letter m) of Act No. 108/2024 Coll. on consumer protection, across the EU Article 16 letter m) of Directive 2011/83/EU. That is why every title has a free sample.

That is the law, not our customer service. If a file is broken or never arrived, write to andrej@arling.sk and we will fix it or send the money back, because that is a different thing from changing your mind.
