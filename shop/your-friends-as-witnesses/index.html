How your case is made.

Two boxes on the Etsy order page. After that, your case is built automatically as soon as the payment goes through.

  1. Type 3 to 14 names. In the first box, type your guests, each as First Last, separated by commas. For example: Emma Walsh, Liam Turner, Olivia Brooks.
  2. Name the detective, if you like. The second box is optional: a detective name for the cover, for example Sophie.
  3. We build your case. Your friends become the witnesses, named above the clues they gave, and they appear in the register among 5,000 guests. The solution page shows which clue cleared each friend.
  4. Download it a few minutes later. A few minutes after your payment goes through, your case is ready at arling.sk/case. Enter your Etsy order number and the surname of the first guest on your list (or your own), and download three PDF files: A4, US Letter and E-ink.

Find your friends. Clear them.

Every friend you name stands somewhere in the register of 5,000 guests, sorted by surname. Nobody you name is ever the killer, so sooner or later a clue clears each of them.

Every case checked before you get it.

A computer program builds each case. Before your files are released, a second, separate program reads the printed clues and the printed register and checks that exactly one guest fits all the clues, that every clue removes at least 5 percent of the guests still in play, and that every checkpoint number is right. Names and privacy.

You type the names in the first box on Etsy. This is how we read them.

Each name
First name and surname, separated by commas, up to 20 letters in each.
Two surnames
Join them with a hyphen (Garcia-Lopez), or we use the last one.
Left out
Titles, initials and middle names. Accents are written without marks: Zoë becomes Zoe.
Fine as typed
Apostrophes, hyphens and surnames with van or de, like O’Brien, Smith-Jones or Van Dijk.
Unreadable
If we cannot read at least 3 names, the download page asks you to type them again, and your case is built right away.
Privacy
We use the names only to build your case. Your files contain them and are deleted automatically 30 days after they are ready, so please save your files. Please only use the names of people who will be happy to appear in a game. More in section 8b of our privacy policy.

Before you buy.

Is one of my friends the killer?

No. The killer is always a stranger: nobody you name is ever the killer, so the puzzle stays fair and nobody gets accused. Your friends are the witnesses. Each of them signs at least one clue and stands in the register among 5,000 guests, and the solution page shows which clue cleared each friend.

How do I type the names?

When you order on Etsy, type 3 to 14 guests in the first box, each as First Last, separated by commas, for example Emma Walsh, Liam Turner, Olivia Brooks. The second box is optional: a detective name for the cover. If a name comes out wrong, there is a link next to your files to fix the names once; your current files keep working until the new ones are ready.

When and where do I get my files?

Right after you pay, your Etsy download has a Start here page and Case No. 1, Murder at the Lantern Ball, in A4, US Letter and E-ink, so you can start tonight. A few minutes after your payment goes through, your own case is ready at arling.sk/case: enter your Etsy order number and the surname of the first guest on your list (or your own), and download three PDF files. The order number is in Purchases and reviews on Etsy and in the receipt email from Etsy. If your case is not there within 30 minutes, message us on Etsy (ARLingPuzzles); we reply within 24 hours.

Is it a party game with roles?

No. It is a pencil and paper logic puzzle for one solver or a team, not a role-play party game with characters and scripts. To play as a group, split the register pages between friends: everyone strikes out guests on their own pages, and at each checkpoint you add up your counts. Or play alone over several evenings.

How long does a case take?

We have not timed how long a case takes to solve, so we do not promise a playing time. It is a long puzzle: the early clues mean going through thousands of rows of the register.

What happens to my friends’ names?

We use the names only to build your case. Your files contain them and are deleted automatically 30 days after they are ready, so please save your files. Please only use the names of people who will be happy to appear in a game. The details are in section 8b of our privacy policy.

How many copies may I print?

As many as you need for yourself, your family or your friends. Please do not share or resell the files. It is a digital download; nothing is shipped.
