A crossword made from your own words.

Type 10 to 25 words with your own clues: the places, names and jokes only the two of you, or your whole family, know. Our puzzle software turns them into a printable crossword, ready a few minutes after your payment goes through.

How your crossword is made.

Four boxes on the Etsy order page, and only the first one is required. After that, your crossword is built automatically as soon as the payment goes through.

  1. Type your words and clues. One entry per line: the word, a colon, then the clue. You need 10 to 25 entries. The first box holds about 13, and the second box is for the rest.
  2. Add a title, a date and a dedication, if you like. For example Our Story / est. 2019 for the cover and the keepsake page, and For Daniel, on our 7th anniversary under the title. Leave the title empty and we use Our Story.
  3. Our software builds it. It places every word and numbers the grid, and a second program checks the result before your files are released.
  4. Download it a few minutes later. A few minutes after your payment goes through, your crossword is ready at arling.sk/case. Choose Custom crossword, enter your Etsy order number and the first word on your list (or your own surname from the order), and download two PDF files: A4 and US Letter.

You type, one per line

  • LISBON: City where we had our honeymoon
  • BISCUIT: Name of our first dog
  • COFFEE: How we met: you spilled it on me

Five pages, two paper sizes.

You get one PDF in A4 and one in US Letter, with the same five pages in each. These are the real pages of our sample file.

Open any page to see it larger. It is a digital download: nothing is shipped and no frame is included. You may print as many copies as you need for yourself, your family or your friends; please do not resell the files.

Writing your words and clues.

You type them in the boxes on the Etsy order page. This is how our software reads them.

Each entry
One per line: the word, a colon, then the clue. For example PARIS: Where we got engaged.
How many
10 to 25 entries. Each word can be used once.
Word length
Up to 15 letters. Spaces, hyphens and apostrophes are fine: New York goes into the grid as NEWYORK, and the clue shows (3,4).
Letters
English letters, A to Z. Accents are written without marks: Zoë becomes ZOE. Numbers and symbols cannot go into the grid, so write 2019 as a clue, not as a word. Clues can be in any language that uses these letters.
Clues
Keep them short, up to about 60 characters. With more than 20 words, long clues may not fit on one page. Your clues are printed exactly as you type them.
If a word does not fit
An entry we cannot use, for example a word with no clue or a word that shares no letters with the others, is left out, and the download page tells you which one and why. You can then correct your list once on the same page, and your crossword is built again right away.
Too few words
If fewer than 10 entries can be used, or the clues are too long to fit on one page, no file is made. The download page tells you what to change, and you send the list again there.
Privacy
We use your words and clues only to build your crossword. Your files contain them and are deleted automatically 30 days after they are ready, so please save your files. More in section 8b of our privacy policy.

Who it is for, and who it is not for.

A good pick if

  • you want a gift for an anniversary, a birthday, a wedding, Christmas or Valentine’s Day: for a husband or wife, parents, grandparents or a best friend
  • the two of you, or your family, share words nobody else would guess
  • you need it soon: it is ready a few minutes after you pay, and you print it at home

Not the right pick if

  • you want a large, dense crossword like the ones in a newspaper; this is a free-form crossword, where your words cross each other like on the pages above
  • you want someone to write or proofread the clues for you; they are printed exactly as you type them
  • you want something printed, framed or sent in the mail; it is a digital download
  • your words need letters other than A to Z in the grid
  • you have fewer than 10 words

You need a printer and plain A4 or US Letter paper, printed at 100% scale. A pencil makes changes easy.

Before you buy.

How many words do I need?

10 to 25. Each word can have up to 15 letters and can be used once. The first box on the Etsy order page holds about 13 entries, and the second box is for the rest.

How do I write the words and clues?

One entry per line: the word, a colon, then the clue, for example PARIS: Where we got engaged. Keep clues short, up to about 60 characters; with more than 20 words, long clues may not fit on one page. Spaces, hyphens and apostrophes in a word are fine: New York goes into the grid as NEWYORK and the clue shows (3,4). Accents are written without marks, so Zoë becomes ZOE. Numbers and symbols cannot go into the grid, so write 2019 as a clue, not as a word. Nobody edits or proofreads your clues: they are printed exactly as you type them.

When and where do I get the PDF?

Right after you pay, your Etsy download has a one-page Start here file with the link and the steps. A few minutes after your payment goes through, your crossword is ready at arling.sk/case: choose Custom crossword, enter your Etsy order number and the first word on your list (or your own surname from the order), and download two PDF files. The files are on our page, not in an Etsy message. The order number is in Purchases and reviews on Etsy and in the receipt email from Etsy. If your crossword is not there within 30 minutes, message us on Etsy (ARLingPuzzles); we reply within 24 hours.

Do I get A4 or US Letter?

Both. Every order has one PDF in A4 and one in US Letter, 5 pages each: the cover, how to play, the puzzle, the solution and the keepsake page. Print at 100% scale on plain paper.

What if a word does not fit?

An entry we cannot use, for example a word with no clue or a word that shares no letters with the others, is left out, and the download page tells you which one and why. You can then correct your list once on the same page, and your crossword is built again right away. If fewer than 10 entries can be used, or the clues are too long to fit on one page, no file is made: the download page tells you what to change, and you send the list again there.

What happens to my words and clues?

We use your words and clues only to build your crossword. Your files contain them and are deleted automatically 30 days after they are ready, so please save your files. The details are in section 8b of our privacy policy.

Is it like a newspaper crossword?

No. It is a free-form crossword, where your words cross each other like on the pages shown here. It is not a dense newspaper-style grid, and it is built by our puzzle software, not drawn by hand.

How many copies may I print?

As many as you need for yourself, your family or your friends. Please do not resell the files. It is a digital download; nothing is shipped, and no frame is included.
