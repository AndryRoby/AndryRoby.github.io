Before you buy

Is my data safe?

This page itself does not process any payments or statements, it only manages the licence. Processing IBANs, amounts and statements runs entirely in your browser inside each tool; none of it is uploaded.

What if Pro is not for me, or a tool does not work?

Write to support@arling.sk. A bug in any of the tools gets fixed with priority; if Pro simply is not for you, we refund the payment within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked.

How do I cancel the subscription?

You can cancel or change the subscription at any time in the Stripe customer portal (log in with the e-mail you paid with); it stays active until the end of the paid period. You do not need to tell us anything.

What exactly is free?

SEPA pain.001 Doctor and SEPA pain.001 Generator: checking and building a file are free, with no limit on uses (the generator takes up to 5,000 payments per file). camt.053 to Excel and Payment matcher: conversion and matching run free on the whole file; without a licence the download holds the first 20 rows as a sample. The full downloaded file, the MT940 and DATEV export and convenience for repeated monthly processing are part of Pro.

What Pro adds in three tools.

The full downloaded file in statement conversion and matching, plus convenience for repeated monthly processing. Checking and building pain.001 stays free, up to 5,000 payments per generated file.

  1. Saved payer profiles.

    Company name, IBAN, BIC and bank in one click in both the Generator and the matcher, no retyping every time.

  2. Several files and accounts at once.

    Several statements, batch payment files or bank accounts in one batch instead of processing them one by one.

  3. Presets for Pohoda, Omega and Money S3.

    Default column mapping for exports from these three Slovak/Czech accounting programs, in one click instead of setting it up by hand. camt.053 to Excel separately offers DATEV, Lexware and sevDesk presets.

    Convert a camt.053 statement to MT940 (.sta) or straight to DATEV Buchungsstapel (EXTF CSV) in the camt.053 to Excel tool. More on MT940 and DATEV.

  5. History.

    The most recent runs in the Generator, camt.053 to Excel and Payment matcher, stored in your browser, downloadable again anytime.

  6. Priority email support.

    A question, or a case one of the tools got wrong? A priority reply, directly from the tools’ author.

Questions people actually search for.

Direct answers about the bundle and one licence for three tools.

What exactly do I get in the Banking tools bundle?

One Pro licence valid in three tools at once: SEPA pain.001 Generator, camt.053 to Excel and Payment matcher. SEPA pain.001 Doctor is free without a licence; its automatic file fix for €29 is a separate purchase. Checking and building pain.001 files is free, up to 5,000 payments per generated file; statement conversion and matching are free too, but without a licence the downloaded file holds the first 20 rows. Pro adds the full downloaded file, the MT940 and DATEV export, and convenience: saved payer profiles, several files and accounts at once, mapping presets for Pohoda, Omega (KROS) and Money S3, a processing history, and priority email support.

How does one licence for three tools work?

After payment, one signed licence key is generated and stored in your browser. The three tools with Pro run on the same domain, arling.sk, so a key stored on one of their pages automatically works on the rest. If that does not happen by itself for some reason (a different browser, cleared site data), the key can always be pasted in manually in the Licence section above, or directly in any of the three tools with Pro.

Is my data sent anywhere?

This page itself does not process any payments or statements, it only manages the licence. Processing IBANs, amounts and statements runs entirely in your browser inside each tool; none of it is uploaded. The only network activity on this page is verifying the licence key after payment and an anonymous usage counter via self-hosted Umami.

What if I only want one of the tools, not all four?

Each of the four tools works free without this page: checking and building pain.001 free (up to 5,000 payments per generated file), statement conversion and matching on the whole file with a download of the first 20 rows. The Banking tools bundle pays off once you repeatedly use at least two of them; if the free version is enough for occasional use of one tool, that is exactly what it is for, and you do not need Pro.

How do I get an invoice?

The licence is sold through Stripe Managed Payments. The merchant of record there is Link (Sold through Link, LLC), which sends you the receipt and the invoice as a PDF; Stripe calculates and remits the VAT. ARLing s. r. o. delivers the tools and the licence key.

Can I cancel the licence or get a refund?

You can cancel or change the subscription at any time in the Stripe customer portal (log in with the e-mail you paid with); it stays active until the end of the paid period. If Pro is not for you, write within 14 days of purchase to support@arling.sk and we will refund the payment, no questions asked.
