Where to start

Sorted by how much each one adds to the index. Every finding is a prompt to edit, not an error.

  1. Stock phrases

    11 occurrences

    Set phrases repeated in thousands of texts, such as “in today’s fast-paced world” or “plays a crucial role”.

    What to do: Delete the phrase or replace it with a concrete fact from your topic.

    Show in text

  2. Moral at the end

    2 occurrences

    The last sentence gives advice or repeats what the reader already knows, such as “Ultimately” or “Remember”.

    What to do: Delete the closing lesson or replace it with one concrete next step.

    Show in text

  3. Lists of three

    3 occurrences

    Three items in a row, such as “speed, accuracy and transparency”. When it happens in every paragraph, the text sounds rehearsed.

    What to do: If they are real things (flour, yeast, salt), leave it. If they are vague words, keep the most important one and support it with an example.

    Show in text

  4. Nouns instead of verbs

    4 occurrences

    Long abstract words ending in -tion, -ment, -ness or -ity, such as “implementation”, “optimisation” or “effectiveness”. They hide who does what and make sentences heavy. Plain words that share an ending are counted too and are fine.

    What to do: If a sentence reads heavy, say it with a verb: instead of “carry out an assessment”, write “we assess”. Otherwise leave it.

    Show in text

Reading your results

Start with a highlighted passage. Each suggestion is an editing prompt, not a rule to remove every phrase. Texts of 30 to 79 words receive only the stock phrase, dash, nouns instead of verbs and lists of three checks. The same text, language and version produce the same result.

Voice profiles, rewriting and version comparisons are in preparation. The writing check is available now, with no account or payment.

Questions

Can I paste text from a PDF or Word file?

Yes. Table of contents lines, page numbers, the title page, numbered headings and formulas are left out of the check, and the result tells you how many lines that was. Words stuck together at a page break are split when it is unambiguous.

Can a score identify the author?

No. People use stock phrases too. The index is a provisional editing aid, not evidence of where a text came from.

Which languages can I check?

Slovak, Czech, English and German. You can override automatic detection at any time. Choose manually for short or mixed-language writing.

What happens to my text?

It is processed only in this browser. We do not save it to a history or send it to a server. Anonymous usage events contain only language, score band and length bucket. Copying a report requires your click.

Does Rukopis remove Claude’s watermark?

No. Rukopis neither verifies nor removes Claude’s watermark. It does not promise to bypass checks. It helps you revise the style in your own words.

Evidence and limits

StoryScope, Reinhart et al. and LAMP inform working hypotheses; they do not validate our index. Lists of three are our own hypothesis. A phrase match says nothing about authorship.
