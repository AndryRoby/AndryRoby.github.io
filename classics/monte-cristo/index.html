What a page looks like

A reading page, a running head and room for the pen.

The picture is a real page of the e-ink file, not a mockup. The running head names the chapter you are in, and the bottom of the page carries the Contents link and the page number.

The text block is 139 x 194 mm on a page of 157 x 210 mm, set in 13 pt serif with 22 lines to a page and no hyphenation, so a line break never falls inside a word. Italics are kept wherever the source transcription marks them, which in this novel is mostly ship names and French words.

Under the text there is a 33 mm band left empty on every page, for a note with the pen. Under the band sit the Contents link and the page number, so from any page you are two taps from any chapter of that volume.

The A4 and US Letter volumes hold the same text block in the middle of the sheet, so the pages, the page order and the links are the same in all three sets.

Text source

Where the words come from.

The anonymous English translation of The Count of Monte Cristo, first published by Chapman and Hall, London, in 1846, as reprinted in a later nineteenth century edition with a short set of editorial footnotes. The printed edition behind the transcription is not identified. The text is in the public domain and is reproduced unchanged; the editorial footnotes are kept at the end of volume 5. Only the line breaks were reflowed for the page, placeholders left by the illustrations of an earlier edition removed, and italics restored where the transcription marks them.

The same translation is free as an EPUB from Project Gutenberg and from Standard Ebooks. What you pay for here is the typesetting for an e-ink page, the notes band, the page numbers and the linked contents in PDFs that behave like PDFs on the tablet. If an EPUB reader suits you better, take the free one.

Typeset and published by ARLing s. r. o., Slovakia, by our own program.

The novel is 2287 pages. Split the way the source text is divided, into five volumes, each file stays between 2.2 and 3.1 MB, which an e-ink reader opens and turns without waiting. Every volume has its own linked table of contents.

The e-ink files are 157 x 210 mm, the 3:4 proportion of the reMarkable 2, reMarkable Paper Pro, Kindle Scribe, Boox Note Air and Supernote A5 X2, so the page fills the screen without zooming.
