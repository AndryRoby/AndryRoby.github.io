E-invoice

XRechnung, Peppol BIS 3.0 and EN 16931

What it finds or makes

ErrorBR-CO-15

The invoice total with VAT (BT-112) should be the total without VAT 100.00 plus the VAT 19.00, that is 119.00. The file says 120.00. Correct the total in the invoice header, not the individual lines.
What you get
Check and preview free. Create the XML for Germany or the Peppol network from a form.
Price
