Questions

Including the awkward ones. If something you want to ask is missing, write to us and it gets added here.

Is a square really ten by ten kilometres?

Almost, and we would rather say so than round it up. A row of the grid is 0.09 degrees of latitude tall, which is 9.95 km at the equator and 10.05 km at the pole. The number of columns in a row follows the cosine of the latitude, so the width stays near ten kilometres everywhere. Over Bratislava a square is 10.04 × 10.01 km. The real size of your square is printed on your certificate, and we never write "exactly 10 × 10 km" anywhere.

You are selling air.

That is a fair thing to think, so here is the plain answer. We sell a place on our own map and a certificate with a number on it, and nothing more than that. It is a small, cheap, honest piece of entertainment, like having a star named after you, except we say what it is on the buy button instead of hiding it in the terms. If you buy one and decide it was silly, write to us and we send the money back, beyond what the law asks of us.

Am I buying land?

No. You are buying a non-transferable entertainment licence to show a name, a link and a drawing on one cell of our grid, for the time written in the terms. It gives you no ownership, no deed and no right to any real ground.

Can someone overwrite my drawing?

No. This is the line we will not cross. A paid square cannot be taken or repainted by another player, by a weekly event or by a season. The only thing that can change it is moderation, if the drawing breaks the content rules, and then you get a written reason and can appeal.

What if you stop running this?

The licence runs for as long as we run the service and at least until 31 December 2029. "Forever" is a promise a one person company cannot keep, so we do not make it. If we ever stop, we tell every owner by e-mail at least 60 days ahead and the certificates stay valid as what they are, a keepsake.

Why can I only buy in Slovakia and Czechia?

Because a million and a half free squares is not scarcity, it is an empty board. Stage one is 1 265 squares, which fits on one screen and means something. The next country opens when 60 % of the open stage is sold. The whole world is on the map from the first day and you can look anywhere.

Does my link help my search ranking?

No, and we would rather lose the sale than pretend otherwise. Every outgoing link carries rel="nofollow ugc". We also remove links used for spam, malware or unrelated advertising, and links that pretend to be somebody else.

Is this gambling?

No. Slovak gambling law needs chance and a prize at the same time. You choose the square yourself, at a fixed price, and nothing is drawn and nothing is won. That is also why there will never be a random pack of parcels or a paid raffle for territory.

Can I sell my square to somebody else?

No. The licence is not transferable and there is no marketplace. The moment parcels become something people speculate on, the project turns into the thing we are trying not to be.

Can a child buy a square?

Checkout asks you to confirm that you are 18 or that you have the cardholder’s consent. If a parent writes to us because a child paid without asking, we refund it. We do not argue about that.

What do you keep about me?

Your e-mail, your billing country and the two pieces of location evidence that EU VAT rules require, plus the name, link and drawing you choose. No real name, no address, no phone number. There is no password and no account: the only way in is a signed link we e-mail you.

Where does the map come from?

We draw it ourselves from Natural Earth, which is public domain, and we host every image. There is no third party tile server, so nothing outside our control can take the map down. The list of 24 336 towns used for search comes from Natural Earth and from GeoNames, licensed CC BY 4.0. The projection is Mercator, which is not a fashion choice: it is conformal, so a square on the ground is a square on your screen at every zoom.

Can I get my money back?

Yes. Digital content that you agreed to receive immediately has no 14 day cooling off period in law, and you will confirm that at checkout. We give the money back anyway if you write to us and say you changed your mind. It costs us less than an unhappy customer.
