Terms

The short version is on the buy button: an entertainment licence on our map, not land, not an investment, not crypto. The long version is below and it says the same thing.

Who you are dealing with

ARLing s. r. o., Ivanská cesta 32E, 821 04 Bratislava, Slovensko, company ID 56583486, VAT ID SK2122352100, registered in the Commercial Register of the City Court Bratislava III. Written contact: andrej@arling.sk. The same address is the contact point for notices under the Digital Services Act.

What you buy

You buy a non-transferable entertainment licence to display a name you choose, one link and one drawing of 32 × 32 pixels on one cell of the grid on the map at arling.sk, for the period set out below.

The grid is ours. It divides the Earth into 5 092 954 cells, each one row of 0.09 degrees of latitude tall and one column wide, and it exists only on our map. Your parcel number is the position of your cell in that grid.

What you do not buy

  • Not land. No title deed, no right to any real ground, anywhere on Earth.
  • Not an investment. We will never tell you a square can go up in value, because it cannot.
  • Not crypto, not an NFT, not a token. There is no chain and there is nothing to trade.
  • Not gambling. You pick the square yourself at a fixed price. Nothing is random and nothing is won.
  • Not an SEO service. Every outgoing link carries rel="nofollow ugc" and we promise no benefit to your ranking.

How long the licence runs

The licence runs for as long as we operate the service and at least until 31 December 2029. EU rules on digital content require a clear duration, and "forever" is not one.

If we decide to stop operating the map, we will tell every owner by e-mail at least 60 days in advance. Any part of a subscription you have already paid for and cannot use will be refunded pro rata. One off purchases of a square are not refunded after that minimum period has passed, because the licence will have been delivered in full.

Price and VAT

Prices are shown including VAT. Selling a parcel is an electronically supplied service, so the VAT rate is the rate of your country, not ours. The rate is calculated and collected by Stripe Tax at checkout and declared by us through the Union One Stop Shop.

If you are a VAT registered business in another EU member state, tell us your VAT ID before you pay; that sale falls under the reverse charge, which is a different regime.

Delivery and withdrawal

The parcel is delivered immediately after payment. Before you pay you must tick two boxes that are not ticked for you: one agreeing to immediate supply and acknowledging that you therefore lose the 14 day right of withdrawal, and one confirming your age. Both are stored with a timestamp and repeated in your confirmation e-mail. The full text is on the withdrawal page.

Separately from the law: if you change your mind, write to us and we will send the money back. We do not argue about it.

Your content and our moderation

The name, link and drawing are yours. You give us a licence to show them on the map, on the parcel page and in previews of shared links, for as long as the licence runs. You promise that you have the right to use what you upload and that it follows the content rules.

In the first weeks every new name, link and drawing is checked before it appears. Until it is checked, the square shows a neutral colour and the words "waiting for approval", because silence there looks like a bug. If we hide or restrict anything, you get the reason in writing and you can appeal by replying to that e-mail.

Links

A link must use https. Every outgoing link carries rel="nofollow ugc". We promise no benefit to your search ranking and we may disable any link used for spam, malware, unrelated advertising or impersonation. You can switch your own link off at any time from your owner panel.

Business parcels

A business parcel is a subscription, yearly or monthly. You can cancel it with one click in your owner panel, without writing to anyone, from the first day. When it ends, the square and the drawing stay visible; what switches off is the clickable link, the verified mark and the monthly report of views and clicks. We charge for what actually costs us work and risk, and we say so here rather than hiding it.

The promise we will not break

A paid square is never overwritten. Not by another player, not by an event, not by a season and not by us. If a later game layer is ever added, it can only be played over unsold territory, and nothing in it can be bought.

Availability and liability

The map is a small service run by one company on modest hardware. We do not promise uninterrupted availability. When the part that handles buying is down, the map still loads and says so plainly instead of spinning. Our liability is limited to what the law allows us to limit; your rights as a consumer are not affected by anything on this page.

Complaints

Write to andrej@arling.sk. We answer every complaint, at the latest within 30 days. If we cannot agree, you can turn to the Slovak Trade Inspection (Slovenská obchodná inšpekcia) as the body for alternative dispute resolution, or use the European online dispute resolution platform.

Data protection

We keep your e-mail, your billing country and the two pieces of location evidence that EU VAT rules require, plus the name, link and drawing you choose and the two timestamped consents from checkout. We do not ask for your real name, address or telephone number, and there is no password. Payment card details never reach us; Stripe handles them. Visit counts come from our own Umami, which sets no cookies. The general privacy policy applies; write to us to have your data deleted.

Changes and governing law

If we change these terms, owners are told by e-mail and the version below changes. A change never shortens a licence you have already paid for. Slovak law applies, and the mandatory consumer protection rules of your country of residence apply where they give you more.
