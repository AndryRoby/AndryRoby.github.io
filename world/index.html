The World in Squares

Find your town, pick one square of about ten kilometres and make it yours: a name, one link, a drawing. Five euros once, with a certificate. Looking is free and needs no account.

The map needs JavaScript, because it is drawn in a canvas in your browser rather than sent as pictures from a server. Everything else on this page works without it: the price, the terms and the rules are plain text below.

    What five euros buys

    • The square

      Yours on the public map, with the name you choose.

    • One link

      https only, rel="nofollow ugc", and you can switch it off at any time.

    • A drawing

      Thirty two by thirty two pixels in sixteen colours. No uploads, which is what keeps moderation possible for one person.

    • A certificate

      A PDF with the parcel number, the coordinates and the real size of that one square in kilometres.

    • A promise

      Nobody overwrites a paid square. Not another player, not an event, not us.

    What this is not

    • Not land. No title deed, no right to any real ground, anywhere on Earth.
    • Not an investment. We will never tell you a square can go up in value, because it cannot.
    • Not crypto, not an NFT, not a token. There is no chain and there is nothing to trade.
    • Not gambling. You pick the square yourself at a fixed price. Nothing is random and nothing is won.
    • Not an SEO service. Every outgoing link carries rel="nofollow ugc" and we promise no benefit to your ranking.

    • A giftsame price

    These are final prices: nothing is added at checkout. A pack is credit you spend on the map one square at a time; to buy one, pick any free square on the map and choose the pack there. We do not sell random packs, premium colours, extra turns, resale or anything that would let one player buy an advantage over another.

    The whole world is on sale

    The grid covers the Earth with 5 092 954 squares. 1 481 468 of them are land, and 1 356 435 are on sale, in 234 countries and territories. Our build script counted them from the Natural Earth 1:10m boundaries: a square belongs to the country its centre falls in, so these are computed numbers and not estimates.

    Three things cannot be bought. Open sea, because there is nothing there to name. Antarctica, where the Antarctic Treaty puts every territorial claim on hold, so we leave it alone. And 13 territories that carry no country code in the public map data, Somaliland and Northern Cyprus among them: a certificate has to name a country, and choosing one for them is not our decision to make. On the map these places are drawn darker, and a click on them says that they are not for sale.

    Questions people ask

    Is a square really ten by ten kilometres?

    Almost, and we would rather say so than round it up. A row of the grid is 0.09 degrees of latitude tall, which is 9.95 km at the equator and 10.05 km at the pole. The number of columns in a row follows the cosine of the latitude, so the width stays near ten kilometres everywhere. Over Bratislava a square is 10.04 × 10.01 km. The real size of your square is printed on your certificate, and we never write "exactly 10 × 10 km" anywhere.

    You are selling air.

    That is a fair thing to think, so here is the plain answer. We sell a place on our own map and a certificate with a number on it, and nothing more than that. It is a small, cheap, honest piece of entertainment, like having a star named after you, except we say what it is on the buy button instead of hiding it in the terms. If you buy one and decide it was silly, write to us and we send the money back, beyond what the law asks of us.

    Am I buying land?

    No. You are buying a non-transferable entertainment licence to show a name, a link and a drawing on one cell of our grid, for the time written in the terms. It gives you no ownership, no deed and no right to any real ground.
