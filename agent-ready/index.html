Shopify's agentic storefronts bring products to AI channels through Shopify Catalog, and a product that misses a catalog requirement does not enter the catalog automatically. The app checks your products against the requirements it can read, the fields Shopify recommends and a few thresholds of our own, and labels every check with its origin.

Shopify Help Center: agentic storefronts
  1. 6

    Catalog requirement

    Conditions a product must meet to enter the product catalog that AI shopping channels read. A product that misses one does not enter the catalog automatically.

    Source: Shopify Catalog requirements (Shopify Help Center)

    6 checks: product title, product image, price above zero, published to the online store, not unlisted, not hidden from search engines
    • Product title. The product has a title.
    • Product image. At least one product image. The app counts only images with an https address.
    • Price above zero. Every variant has a price greater than zero.
    • Published to the online store. Published to the online store sales channel. Shopify also accepts Hydrogen and headless channels, which the app cannot see, so a product published only there fails this check.
    • Not unlisted. Unlisted products open only from a direct link and do not meet the catalog requirements.
    • Not hidden from search engines. The seo.hidden metafield does not hide the product from search engines.
  2. 1

    Catalog risk

    A signal that may keep a product out of the catalog. It is a risk, not a certainty, because the catalog classifies products with its own data.

    Source: Shopify Catalog requirements (Shopify Help Center)

    1 check: category set
    • Category set. Without a category, Shopify may classify the product as Uncategorized, which the catalog excludes. Shopify can still classify it differently from the category you set.
  3. 5

    Recommended field

    Fields the platform recommends filling in. A missing recommended field is a gap, not a block.

    Source: Optimizing your products for AI platforms (Shopify Help Center)

    5 checks: product type, vendor or brand, named variant options, description, valid barcode
    • Product type. The product type field is filled in.
    • Vendor or brand. The vendor field is filled in. Use the real brand or manufacturer; the app cannot verify it.
    • Named variant options. A product with several variants has option names, such as Size or Color, and values for each option.
    • Description. The product has description text.
    • Valid barcode. A barcode, if there is one, has a valid GTIN check digit. A missing barcode is not an error.
  4. 4

    ARLing heuristic

    Our own checks with our own thresholds. They are not platform requirements and every threshold is shown.

    Related Shopify guidance for each check is listed on How we count.

    4 checks: title of 3 characters or more, description of 80 characters or more, image alt text, in a collection
    • Title of 3 characters or more. Our threshold for a descriptive title, not just a code.
    • Description of 80 characters or more. Counted as plain text, after HTML is removed.
    • Image alt text. The first product image has alt text.
    • In a collection. The product is in at least one collection.

What a result means

Pass
The data is there and meets the rule.
Fail
The data is missing or does not meet the rule. The product is listed under the rule, and its name opens it in your admin.
Unknown
The app could not read the data, for example more variants or media than it reads for one product. Unknown is never counted as a pass and never as a fail.

The overview shows a readiness score of the checks that could be evaluated, and lists the rest under Could not be checked. How we count explains the score, the coverage and every rule with its weight, threshold and source.

How Pro checks changes

Shopify tells the app when a product is created or updated, and the app checks it in a run every 10 minutes. A product that changes again within an hour is checked after that hour.

What the app does not do.

  • It does not change your store.Its only permission is to read products. You make every fix in your admin.
  • It does not promise sales or a place in AI channels.Shopify and each channel decide which stores and products appear. The checks look at product data only and do not predict sales or visibility.
  • It does not use AI for the checks.Every check is a fixed rule, the same for every store. Support replies come from an AI assistant run by ARLing.
  • It does not read your whole store.No customers, orders, payments or theme code, and at most 20 variants, 3 media and 10 collections per product.
  • It does not send emails or reports.Results are in the app when you open it.

Your data.

  • One permission: read products.The app does not read or keep customer data.
  • Results, not your content.It keeps each product's ID, title, status, check results and up to 10 collections, not your descriptions or images.
  • No cookies, no analytics in the app.Inside your Shopify admin it loads only Shopify's scripts and its own.
  • Deleted after uninstall.Access ends when the uninstall is processed. Store data is deleted when Shopify sends its erasure request 48 hours later.

Questions before you install.

Will AI channels show my products after I fix the issues?

Nobody can promise that. The app shows which products miss Shopify Catalog requirements and recommended fields, and why. Which stores and products appear in an AI channel is decided by Shopify and by each channel.

Can the app change or break my products?

No. It can only read products. You make every fix yourself in your Shopify admin, and the app links each product there.

What do I get on the Free plan?

The full first check of every active and unlisted product with every issue, the score, issues by rule with every product and results by collection. You get 25 re-checks a calendar month, and a full check again 30 days after the last one.

How much is Pro?

$9.99 a month or $99 a year, with a 7-day free trial, billed by Shopify in US dollars.

How do I change plan or cancel?

Open Plan in the app and select Change plan, which opens the Shopify plan page. When a change to Free is scheduled, the app shows the date, and Pro features stay on until then. According to the Shopify Help Center, uninstalling a paid app cancels future recurring charges, but you might still be billed for the current billing cycle.

Why does a check say unknown?

The app could not read that data, for example more variants or media than it reads for one product. Unknown is never counted as a pass or as a fail. The app lists those products under Could not be checked, and the score counts only the checks it could evaluate.

Does the app use AI?

Not for the checks: every check is a fixed rule with a published source. Support replies come from an AI assistant run by ARLing.

How is the free web check different from the app?

The web check needs no install and works for any public store, on Shopify or not. It reads a public sample: up to 20 products and 3 product pages. The app reads every active and unlisted product from your Shopify admin, including fields a public feed may leave out, such as barcodes and image alt text.
