Privacy policy

What the app reads, keeps and deletes

ARLing: Agentic Product Check only reads your products. It keeps the check results it needs to show you, deletes your access when you uninstall and deletes the rest when Shopify asks.

02

What the app can read

The app asks Shopify for one permission: read products (read_products). It cannot see customers, orders, payments or theme code, and it changes nothing in your store.

During a check it reads the product description, images and their alt text, prices, barcodes, options and variants, category, product type, vendor and online store publication. It does not store them. Only the rule result and a short explanation are kept.

The app does not read or store data about your customers. It therefore answers Shopify's customers/data_request and customers/redact requests with no customer data.

The app sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics tracking. Inside the Shopify admin it loads only Shopify's scripts (App Bridge and Polaris) and its own script.

04

Uninstalling and erasure

  1. When you uninstall the app, we delete the access tokens as soon as we process the uninstall event. The app can no longer read your store.
  2. 48 hours after you uninstall, Shopify sends us a request to erase the store's data (shop/redact). When we process it, we delete the store record, tokens, check results, history, limits, queue, subscription status, stored answers and locks for the store.
  3. If erasure fails, the request stays to be retried and nothing is marked as done.
  4. If you reinstall the app before the erasure request arrives, erasure deletes all check results for the store, including those made after reinstalling; you can then run a new check. Access for the new installation stays.
  5. After erasure only the record of the handled request and the event delivery records remain, for the periods in the table above.

05

Legal basis

Providing the app you installed, and the Pro plan: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Technical logs, protection against double processing and proof of handled requests: our legitimate interest in a secure and demonstrably correct service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Support e-mails: our legitimate interest in answering your questions about the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Store data is mostly business data. It can be personal data if the store is run by an individual or a product title contains a person's name.

The app makes no decisions with legal effect. The score is an automatic sum of the rules described on How we count.

Čo appka smie čítať

Appka žiada od Shopify jediné oprávnenie: čítanie produktov (read_products). Nevidí zákazníkov, objednávky, platby ani kód témy a nič v obchode nemení.

Pri kontrole prečíta popis produktu, obrázky a ich alternatívny text, ceny, čiarové kódy, možnosti a varianty, kategóriu, typ, výrobcu a zverejnenie v online obchode. Neukladá ich, uloží len výsledok pravidla a krátke vysvetlenie.

Údaje o zákazníkoch obchodu appka nečíta ani neukladá. Na žiadosti Shopify customers/data_request a customers/redact preto odpovedá, že o zákazníkovi nemá žiadne údaje.

Appka nenastavuje cookies a nepoužíva reklamné ani analytické sledovanie. V administrácii Shopify načítava len skripty Shopify (App Bridge a Polaris) a vlastný skript.

04

Odinštalovanie a výmaz

  1. Keď appku odinštalujete, prístupové tokeny zmažeme hneď, ako spracujeme udalosť odinštalovania. Appka potom už nemôže čítať váš obchod.
  2. Shopify nám 48 hodín po odinštalovaní pošle žiadosť o výmaz údajov obchodu (shop/redact). Keď ju spracujeme, zmažeme záznam obchodu, tokeny, výsledky kontrol, históriu, limity, frontu, stav predplatného, uložené odpovede a zámky obchodu.
  3. Ak výmaz zlyhá, žiadosť ostane na zopakovanie a nič sa neoznačí ako vybavené.
  4. Ak appku nainštalujete znova skôr, ako príde žiadosť o výmaz, výmaz zmaže všetky výsledky kontrol obchodu, aj tie po novej inštalácii; novú kontrolu potom môžete spustiť znova. Prístup novej inštalácie ostane.
  5. Po výmaze ostáva len záznam o vybavenej žiadosti a záznamy o doručení udalostí do ich lehoty (tabuľka vyššie).
