From the listing

How to use it.

Print pages 7 to 54 double sided (flip on the long edge) and you get 24 sheets. Fold each sheet, number it and hide it in an envelope, a box or a stocking. You can also print one sided; every day is then two pages in a row.

From the listing

How the puzzles are made.

Our program builds every puzzle, and a solver checks that exactly one answer fits before the puzzle goes into the calendar. Each Christmas picture can be solved line by line, without guessing. The puzzles are new: none of them is in our other puzzle books or in our free daily puzzles.
