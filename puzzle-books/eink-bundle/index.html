What is not verified

No physical device, and no form fields.

Every number on this page is measured in the finished PDF files by our check script: puzzles, pages, links, file sizes, the sha256 of every archive. What we have not done is open one of these files on a reMarkable, a Kindle Scribe, a Boox or a Supernote and solve a puzzle on it with a pen. Nobody here owns one. That is what the free sample is for: same page size, same grids, same kind of links.

The second thing worth saying plainly: these ten books carry no PDF form fields. The script counts 0 in all ten files. On an e-ink tablet that changes nothing, because you write over the page in the reader's own pen layer, which is how people solve puzzles on these devices anyway. On a computer it means there is no box to type a number into. Our reading titles do have form fields; these puzzle books, which were built earlier, do not.

Questions

Before you buy.

Which devices are these made for?

The pages are 157 x 210 mm, the 3:4 proportion of the reMarkable 2, reMarkable Paper Pro, Kindle Scribe, Boox Note Air and Supernote A5 X2, so a page fills the screen without zooming. We have not opened the files on a physical device: the sample of ten pages is free, so you can try it on yours before you buy.

Can I write in the files with the pen?

You write on the page with your reader's own pen layer, the way you write on any PDF. These ten books carry no PDF form fields: the check script counts 0 of them in all ten files, and we would rather say so than let you find out. Solving with the pen is what they are made for, the cells are large and the lines are high contrast, but nothing in them is a typed box on a computer.

The ten PDF files come to 68.29 MB. They are split into four archives of 18.81, 19.02, 19.61 and 10.84 MB, each under the 20 MB that a single file may have on Etsy, where this bundle is going next, and so that a download that drops does not cost you all ten books.
