The Halloween Logic Puzzle Book, in twenty seconds.

Nonogram 2 from page 6 of the book solves itself, one row or column at a time, until the light goes out and a pumpkin glows. Then the four chapters: 80 logic puzzles to print at home, 4.90 € on Etsy.

Sound starts only after you tap. It pauses when you scroll away.

What the film shows.

  1. Page 6 of the book, Nonogram 2 (easy, 10 by 10), already solved: in a dark grid a pumpkin glows and its face is lit like a candle. Halloween, Logic Puzzle Book. 80 printable puzzles, PDF, and the address of our Etsy shop, arlingpuzzles.etsy.com.
  2. The picture drains away from the middle and the empty puzzle is left: numbers beside every row and above every column. The numbers hide a picture.
  3. The first step, slowly: row 3 has the number 6. A bar of 6 cells slides from the left end to the right end, and the middle two cells are covered either way, so they are certain.
  4. The rest of the puzzle solves itself quickly, 22 steps in all. Each step looks at one row or one column and fills only the cells that are certain, without guessing. New cells are orange and the older ones black, the way the book's "Your first moves" pages mark each step; empty cells get a dot. Every puzzle in the book has exactly one solution.
  5. The light goes out from the middle of the grid and the picture glows: it was a pumpkin all along. The eyes and the mouth are the empty cells inside it.
  6. The grid shrinks into the table of contents: Nonograms, Hashi, Kakuro, Slitherlink. Four kinds, 20 puzzles each.
  7. The page comes back with the glowing pumpkin and the title, the same picture the film started with.

The puzzle in the film is Nonogram 2 exactly as printed on page 6, with its real numbers, and the steps come from a solver that reads one line at a time. The film shows the picture of this one puzzle; the other 19 pictures in the book stay hidden until you solve them. The chapter names and icons are the ones printed in the book.

About the book.

The Halloween Logic Puzzle Book has 80 logic puzzles in four chapters, easy to hard, with every solution at the back: 20 Nonograms that hide Halloween pictures drawn for this book, plus 20 Hashi, 20 Kakuro and 20 Slitherlink. It is 91 pages, as two PDF files, A4 and US Letter, to print at home. It costs 4.90 € on Etsy as a digital download; nothing is shipped.

See the pages and what is inside or get it on Etsy.

How the film is made.

There is no video file on this page. Every frame is drawn on a canvas by a small script, using the numbers and the picture of the puzzle from the book, and every sound is synthesised by Web Audio in your browser after you tap. No photo, no recording, no picture generator. The same script renders the film frame by frame into a video for YouTube and Instagram.

It is built to be light: what does not move is drawn once and reused, the film stops when it ends, when you scroll away or switch tabs, and it shows a still picture if your device asks for reduced motion. Questions: andrej@arling.sk.

Our anonymous statistics (self-hosted Umami) count the page visit, whether the film was started or replayed, and which link was clicked. Nothing else.
