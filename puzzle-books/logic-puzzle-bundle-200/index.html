What is inside

Four books, fifty puzzles each.

The bundle is four printable logic puzzle books, one for each kind of puzzle. Every book holds 50 puzzles in four levels, 13 easy, 15 medium, 15 hard and 7 challenge, with the rules in English and an example puzzle with its answer at the front and all 50 solutions at the back.

You download two ZIP files, one with the four books in A4 and one with the same four books in US Letter. The puzzles are the same in both, only the page size differs.

Inside, each book has a bird or animal name: Cranes (Hashi), Squirrels (Kakuro), Magpies (Nonogram) and Otters (Slitherlink). The rules are told as a small story, so in Cranes the islands are sandbanks and the bridges are walkways.

These are not our 100 puzzle books of the same names sold on this site: none of the 200 puzzles is in those books, and none of them has appeared in our free daily games.

The four puzzles

One line of rules for each.

  1. Hashi. Connect the numbered islands with bridges.
  2. Kakuro. Fill the runs with digits that match the printed sums.
  3. Nonogram. Use the row and column clues to decide which squares to fill.
  4. Slitherlink. Use the numbered clues to draw a single closed loop.

Page 2 of every book holds the full rules, one worked example and its only solution, and says which puzzle numbers are easy, medium, hard and challenge: 1 to 13, 14 to 28, 29 to 43 and 44 to 50. On the right is that page from the Hashi book, as it prints.

One solution

Checked by program.

Our program generates every puzzle and checks each one for a single solution, so a puzzle never has two answers that both fit the clues.

The four books are the same files as our four single book listings on Etsy. If you already own one of them, the bundle repeats it. The other three bought one by one come to 8.70 € before discounts, so the bundle is still the cheaper way to get them.

Questions

Before you buy.

How do I download it?

After Etsy confirms your payment, download the files from Purchases and reviews on Etsy.com in a web browser. Downloads are not available in the Etsy app. If you bought as a guest, use the download link in your Etsy receipt e-mail. It is a digital download only, so nothing is shipped.

Which files do I get?

Two ZIP files: ARLing-Logic-Puzzle-Bundle-A4.zip and ARLing-Logic-Puzzle-Bundle-US-Letter.zip. Each holds the same four PDF books, one for Hashi, Kakuro, Nonogram and Slitherlink, so you get 200 puzzles in both paper sizes. Extract the ZIP, then open a PDF.

How do I print it?

Choose A4 or US Letter to match your paper and print at 100 percent or Actual size, in black and white. Try one page before printing a whole book.

I already bought one of the single books. Is it in the bundle?

Yes. The four books in the bundle are the same files as our four single book listings on Etsy, so if you already own one of them, the bundle repeats it. Our 100 puzzle books with the same names, sold on this site, are different books: none of their puzzles is in the bundle.

Does every puzzle have a solution in the book?

Yes. Each book has the rules in English, an example puzzle with its answer, and all 50 solutions at the back. Our program generates every puzzle and checks each one for a single solution.
