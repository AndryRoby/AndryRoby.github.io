The Advent Puzzle Calendar, in twenty seconds.

A printable Advent calendar of logic puzzles: one sheet for every day from December 1 to 24, 48 puzzles in all. The film shows the printed sheet for December 1, solves its real Nonogram line by line and turns the sheet over to the puzzle on the back.

Sound starts only after you tap. It pauses when you scroll away.

What the film shows.

  1. The printed sheet for December 1 on a dark green night, snow falling, two more sheets under it. Its Nonogram is filled in as a red bell. A red label: printable PDF. Puzzle Calendar, Advent 2026: 24 days, 48 logic puzzles. arlingpuzzles.etsy.com.
  2. The red cells clear and the sheet comes closer, with its row and column numbers. Caption: December 1, find the picture.
  3. The Nonogram solves itself line by line, without guessing. Each row or column lights up in turn, and the cells it proves turn red or get a dot.
  4. The grid fades and the picture is a bell. It swings. 23 more pictures wait on the other sheets.
  5. The sheet turns over. On the back is the Hashi puzzle for December 1; other days have Kakuro or Slitherlink.
  6. The sheet turns back to the bell, the grid and the numbers come back, and the film ends where it started.

The Nonogram in the film is the real December 1 puzzle from the calendar, and the film solves it with a line solver: it only fills a cell when that line allows no other choice. It is the only solution the film shows. The sheets under it show empty grids.

What you buy is a PDF: one sheet for every day, which you print, fold and hide in an envelope, a box or a stocking.

About the calendar.

Advent Puzzle Calendar 2026 has one sheet for every day from December 1 to 24. The front of each sheet is a Nonogram that hides a Christmas picture; the back is a Hashi, Kakuro or Slitherlink puzzle, taking turns. December 1 to 8 are easy, 9 to 16 medium and 17 to 24 hard. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and all 48 solutions are at the back. Two PDF files, A4 and US Letter, 63 pages each. It costs 4.90 € on Etsy, a digital download; nothing is shipped.

See the calendar on Etsy. In German: Rätsel-Adventskalender 2026. All our printables: arlingpuzzles.etsy.com.

How the film is made.

There is no video file on this page. Every frame is drawn on a canvas by a small script and every sound is synthesised by Web Audio in your browser after you tap. No photo, no recording, no picture generator. The same script renders the film frame by frame into a video for YouTube and Instagram.

It is built to be light: what does not move is drawn once and reused, the film stops when it ends, when you scroll away or switch tabs, and it shows a still picture if your device asks for reduced motion. Questions: andrej@arling.sk.

Our anonymous statistics (self-hosted Umami) count the page visit and whether the film was started, replayed or its Etsy link was clicked. Nothing else.
