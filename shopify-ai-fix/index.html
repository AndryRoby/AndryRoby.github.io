What the fix covers

Six parts, one price, one store. The free plan tells you which of them your store needs and how many products each one touches.

  1. A check of your whole catalog

    We read every product in your public product export, not a sample, and several product pages, and list each gap with the products it affects. Our free public check reads 20 products; the plan reads all of them.

  2. Product types filled in

    Shopify names Type among the product details that AI platforms consider. We propose a type for every product without one, you approve the list, and we fill it in.

    In our study, 390 of 2,098 sampled products had no type, in 49 of 113 stores. Source: Shopify Help Center, Optimizing your products for AI platforms

  3. Categories from Shopify's Standard Product Taxonomy

    A product without a category is saved as uncategorized. Shopify classifies products for Shopify Catalog itself and leaves out those it classifies as Uncategorized; complete product information helps it classify them correctly. We propose a standard category for each product without one, and nothing changes until you approve.

    Sources: Shopify's Standard Product Taxonomy · Requirements for Shopify Catalog

  4. Return policy and shipping details in your product markup

    Google lists both as recommended properties of a product offer. We add them directly to each product offer in the structured data (JSON-LD) of your product pages, with your real terms and on a copy of your theme that you preview and publish yourself. If your store already declares them store-wide and they pass our check, the plan says so and we add nothing.

    In our study, a valid return policy in product markup in 4 of 88 stores, valid shipping details in 4 of 87. Source: Google Search Central, merchant listing structured data

  5. Products hidden from search engines

    The seo.hidden setting keeps a product out of search engines, your sitemap and Shopify Catalog while its page still opens, and apps can set it too. We list the products that have it and ask which should be visible; we change only those you name. To list them, we may add a product metafield definition for seo.hidden, which changes no product.

    Sources: Shopify Help Center, Hiding a page from search engines · Our guide to seo.hidden

  6. A report before and after

    We run our public check before and after the work and send you both results with a list of every change we made. The score is the weighted share of the check's rules, among those we could evaluate, that your store passes. It is not a ranking.

What a fix cannot promise

It does not guarantee a place in ChatGPT, Shopify Catalog or Google results. Shopify decides catalog eligibility from your plan, your account standing and its own classification of each product, and every AI assistant and search engine weighs factors of its own. The fix removes known gaps in your product data: that is what we promise, and what we re-check.

Sources: Requirements for Shopify Catalog · Optimizing your products for AI platforms · Google merchant listing structured data

Not a fit if

  • Your store is headless (Hydrogen or a custom storefront) or not on Shopify: we work on Online Store themes.
  • You want more sales, or a place in ChatGPT, Shopify Catalog or Google, guaranteed: nobody can promise that.
  • An app writes your product markup and you want to keep it exactly as it is: we would first have to change that app's settings with you.
  • Our free check finds none of these gaps: then you probably do not need the fix, and the free plan says so.

Questions before you ask

Will this get my products into ChatGPT, Shopify Catalog or Google?

We cannot promise that. Shopify decides whether your store is in Shopify Catalog, from your plan, your account standing and its own classification of each product, and every AI assistant and search engine ranks products by factors of its own. The fix removes known gaps in your product data, and that is what we re-check.

Do you need my password?

No. You accept a collaborator request from our Shopify Partner account, with the permissions listed on this page, in Settings > Users. We remove our access as soon as we deliver, and you can change or remove it there at any time.

What exactly changes in my theme?

Only the product structured data (JSON-LD) in a copy of your theme. You preview the copy and publish it when you are happy; we never publish a theme. If you edit your live theme before you publish the copy, tell us and we make a fresh copy.

What if I never publish the theme copy?

We check the product pages of the copy with the same rules before you publish it, so the money-back promise does not depend on when you publish.

What if the plan finds nothing worth fixing?

Then we tell you so, and there is nothing to pay.

Is there a limit on catalog size?

No fixed limit. The plan gives the time; if the work does not fit into 3 working days, we agree a schedule with you before you pay.
