How these numbers are made

Visits come from Umami, an analytics tool we run ourselves. It sets no cookies and does not keep IP addresses. Only visits to arling.sk count, not our own tests on other addresses. One person who opens two of our sites counts as two visitors.

We leave out the bots we can recognise: all visits from Singapore and China (almost all were bots), data centres such as Ashburn or Des Moines, screen sizes no person uses (for example 800x600 or 2000x2000), Linux computers outside Slovakia, and visits whose location has no city. We also leave out bursts that look automated: three or more visits from the same country in the same minute, with the same browser, system and screen, each opening one page. We also leave out our own computers we can recognise. This removes some real people too, mostly on mobile networks, and some of our own phone visits may still be counted.

From 27 to 30 September 2026 our analytics recorded the location of our own proxy server instead of the visitor (mostly Vienna and Prague). Without the location we could not tell people from bots, so visits from those days are left out of all numbers.

To protect visitors, numbers below 5 are shown as "under 5". Places appear only as dots rounded to about 50 km, and only where we counted at least 5 different devices (browser, system, screen and language), so one person who opens several of our sites cannot make a dot alone. Today is never shown; the newest day is yesterday. Etsy orders are shown as a count, without countries or amounts. The numbers are refreshed automatically every morning; the list of experiments is written by hand.

Money: revenue from Etsy's order records and from Stripe; costs from the company's bank statements, supplier invoices and Etsy's own payment records, plus our estimates, which are marked. The AdMob figure is an estimate and is not counted until Google pays it.

Map data: Natural Earth, public domain. The globe is drawn with three.js (MIT licence), served from this site. Apart from our own analytics, the page loads nothing from other servers.
