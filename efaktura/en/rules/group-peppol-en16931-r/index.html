PEPPOL-EN16931-R: Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 rules

What the Peppol network asks for on top of EN 16931: data that must not be missing and calculations on lines, allowances and charges.

The group has 28 rules. Source: Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 (OpenPEPPOL, peppol-bis-invoice-3).

What our tool does about this error

The checker finds these codes in your file and shows where they sit in the XML. If you build the invoice in our generator, the finished XML runs through the same checker before the download and will not download while an error remains.

The checker and the preview cost 0 € and need no account. Downloading the finished XML costs 2.90 € excl. VAT, unlocked in the browser for 24 hours, or 9.90 € excl. VAT for 30 days including the CSV batch.
