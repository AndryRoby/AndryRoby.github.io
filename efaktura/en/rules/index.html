Rules 332 codes

E-invoice rules and error codes

Each of the 332 codes our checker reports has a page here: what the rule says, why a validator reports it and how to fix the error. The codes are grouped the way validators write them.

Check my invoice Create an e-invoice

Rule groups

  • BR Mandatory data of the document: number and date, seller, buyer, addresses, lines, allowances, charges, the VAT breakdown and payment details. 58 codes.
  • BR-CO Totals that have to add up and data that depend on each other: for example the amount due, the VAT breakdown or the reason of an allowance. 20 codes.
  • BR-DEC How many decimals the amounts in the document may have. 21 codes.
  • BR-CL The value has to come from the prescribed code list: document type, currency, country, unit, VAT category, payment means and more. 23 codes.
  • BR-S Rules for documents where a line, an allowance, a charge or the VAT breakdown uses category S, that is standard rated. 10 codes.
  • BR-Z Rules for documents where a line, an allowance, a charge or the VAT breakdown uses category Z, that is zero rated. 10 codes.
  • BR-E Rules for documents where a line, an allowance, a charge or the VAT breakdown uses category E, that is exempt from VAT. 10 codes.
  • BR-AE Rules for documents where a line, an allowance, a charge or the VAT breakdown uses category AE, that is VAT reverse charge. 10 codes.
  • BR-G Rules for documents where a line, an allowance, a charge or the VAT breakdown uses category G, that is export outside the EU. 10 codes.
  • BR-IC Rules for documents where a line, an allowance, a charge or the VAT breakdown uses category K, that is intra-community supply. 12 codes.
  • BR-O Rules for documents where a line, an allowance, a charge or the VAT breakdown uses category O, that is not subject to VAT. 14 codes.
  • BR-AF Rules for documents where a line, an allowance, a charge or the VAT breakdown uses category L, that is IGIC (Canary Islands). 10 codes.
  • BR-AG Rules for documents where a line, an allowance, a charge or the VAT breakdown uses category M, that is IPSI (Ceuta and Melilla). 10 codes.
  • BR-B Rules for documents where a line, an allowance, a charge or the VAT breakdown uses category B, that is split payment (Italy). 2 codes.
  • PEPPOL-EN16931-R What the Peppol network asks for on top of EN 16931: data that must not be missing and calculations on lines, allowances and charges. 28 codes.
  • PEPPOL-EN16931-P Which document type codes Peppol accepts and which VATEX exemption code belongs to which VAT category. 11 codes.
  • PEPPOL-EN16931-CL, F Code lists that Peppol narrows down (attachment types, allowance and charge reasons, currencies, electronic address schemes) and the way a date is written. 7 codes.
  • PEPPOL-COMMON-R Length and check digit of identifiers: GLN and national organisation numbers from Norway, Denmark, Belgium, Italy, Sweden and Australia. 13 codes.
  • BR-DE, BR-TMP What the German XRechnung asks for on top of EN 16931: seller contact, the Leitweg-ID in BT-10, payment details, and city and post code in the addresses. The BR-TMP rules come from the same schematron. 34 codes.
  • BR-DEX Rules for documents that declare the XRechnung extension in cbc:CustomizationID: attachments and the schemeID attribute of identifiers. 6 codes.
  • ARL, XML, CII Checks we added ourselves, not taken from a schematron: the shape of the XML file, CII syntax, IBAN, VAT number, Leitweg-ID, dates and currency. 13 codes.
