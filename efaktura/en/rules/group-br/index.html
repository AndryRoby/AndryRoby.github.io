E-invoice / Rules / BR

Rule group 58 codes

BR: EN 16931 core rules

Mandatory data of the document: number and date, seller, buyer, addresses, lines, allowances, charges, the VAT breakdown and payment details.

The group has 58 rules. Source: EN 16931 schematron (ConnectingEurope/eInvoicing-EN16931, licence EUPL 1.2).
