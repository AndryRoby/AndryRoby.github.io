Slovak e-invoicing from 1 January 2027

Does the e-invoice mandate apply to me?

From 1 January 2027, VAT payers in Slovakia must issue invoices to businesses and public offices in Slovakia as XML e-invoices, and every business must be able to receive them. At most 6 questions, with a source for every paragraph of the result.

Source: Financial Administration FAQ 9/DPH/2025/IM of 15 September 2026, in Slovak (introduction, questions 7 and 17), and Act No. 385/2025 Coll.
