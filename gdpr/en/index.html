Data protectionGDPR, BratislavaVersion 1.1 of 10 September 2026

What personal data we process, why, and for how long.

This document describes every processing of personal data at ARLing s. r. o., its legal basis and how long the data is kept.

It complements the Privacy page and our Terms of use. In force since 6 September 2026.

ARLing s. r. o., Bratislava, company ID 56583486.
Data protection officer

Cookies

Access, rectification, erasure and portability; we answer within a month.

1Controller

The controller of the personal data is ARLing s. r. o., Company ID 56583486, VAT ID SK2122352100, registered office at Ivanská cesta 19112/32E, 821 04 Bratislava, Slovak Republic. Contact for data protection matters: andrej@arling.sk. We have not appointed a data protection officer (DPO); for the scope of our processing the law does not require one.

2What we process and what we do not

We process only the data described in this document: your e-mail address when you sign up for news, technical data when a licence key is issued, the content of a SEPA file uploaded for the paid check (section 5), and ordinary e-mail correspondence. We do not sell data, we do no profiling and no automated decision-making, we use no advertising networks and we set no cookies.

3News e-mail list

If you sign up for news, we store your e-mail address, the page you signed up from, the language and the time of sign-up. The data is on our own server in Slovakia. We use it only to send news about the tools. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time by replying to any of our e-mails or by writing to andrej@arling.sk, and we then delete the address within 30 days.

4Issuing licence keys

When you buy a Pro licence we store the Stripe Checkout payment session identifier, the plan type, the expiry date and the time the key was issued. We do not store your e-mail address; the licence key contains only a shortened hash of the e-mail. Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). We keep the data for the term of the licence and for a further 3 years in case of a complaint.

5SEPA file check

For the paid SEPA file check you upload, after paying, a pain.001 file on our page, that is a bulk payment order. It contains the names of the payer and the payees, their IBANs and the amounts, so as a rule also personal data of third parties (for example your suppliers) who are not a party to the contract and with whom we do not communicate directly. You, the customer, are the controller of that data; ARLing s. r. o. processes it solely to perform the check you ordered and for nothing else. We never use the file to train any model and we do not pass it on. Legal basis: performance of the contract with you (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).

Together with the file we store a short order record: the Stripe Checkout session identifier, the e-mail address given at payment, the amount and currency, the time of payment and upload, the file size and whether you ticked the box on the upload page consenting to the service starting before the withdrawal period ends. File and record are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV with a 30-day expiry from upload; when it runs out, the platform deletes them automatically. On request we delete them earlier. One file may be at most 5 MB and at most 5 files can be uploaded per payment. On upload the server only checks that the body is XML; the content is then read exclusively by Andrej Lauko, the managing director, manually.

Sub-processors for this service: Cloudflare (storage and processing; Cloudflare has data centres in the EU as well and the transfer is covered by standard contractual clauses) and Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (the payment and the check that the checkout session is paid before we accept a file). The notification that a new file has arrived is sent by our own server through ntfy and carries only the last 8 characters of the checkout session identifier, never the file or its content. Send deletion requests to andrej@arling.sk.

6Payments and invoicing

Payments, proofs of purchase and VAT are handled by Stripe as the merchant of record and a separate controller for the payment data: Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., registered office in Dublin, Ireland; card details are seen only by Stripe. ARLing s. r. o. delivers the service. The order data needed to deliver the service and for our accounting we process on the basis of performance of a contract and a legal obligation (GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (c)). Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

7E-mail correspondence

If you send us an e-mail, we handle the correspondence through Zoho Mail (Zoho Corporation B.V., the Netherlands), in data centres in the EU. We keep it for as long as it is needed to deal with your question; we keep accounting records for 10 years under Slovak law.

8Website hosting

The arling.sk website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub keeps server logs with IP addresses according to its own privacy statement. The transfer to the USA takes place under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

9Visit analytics

We measure visits with our own Umami tool on our server. It uses no cookies and no fingerprinting. The IP address is hashed with a salt that changes daily and is not stored; only aggregate counts of page views and events (for example "generate", "buy_click") are stored. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). Because we store no cookies and no identifiers on your device, a cookie banner is not needed.

10Connecting to hosted tools

News sign-ups and licence checks go through an encrypted TLS connection (Tailscale Funnel, Tailscale Inc.), which sees only the metadata of the encrypted traffic, not its content.

11Data in your browser

Tool settings and the licence key are stored only in your browser (localStorage), on your device. This storage is necessary for the tools to work and is not tracking.

12Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restriction of processing, to data portability, to object and to withdraw consent. Send your request to andrej@arling.sk, we reply within one month.

13Complaint

If you believe we process your data contrary to the GDPR, you can lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic, Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava 27, dataprotection.gov.sk.

14Changes to this document

We may amend this document. We announce a change on this page in advance, at least 30 days before it takes effect.

15Contact and related pages

Send questions to andrej@arling.sk. Related pages: Privacy and Terms of use.
