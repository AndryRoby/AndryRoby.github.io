ARLing Word Search: privacy policy · Prism Night: privacy policy

PrivacyARLing s. r. o., BratislavaVersion of 18 September 2026

What we collect, and what we never collect.

ARLing s. r. o. publishes books and runs the tools on arling.sk. This page says plainly what happens to your data.

The two Android apps have policies of their own: ARLing Word Search and Prism Night.

1The tools

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. What you paste into a form (URLs, configuration, XML files) is processed on your device and never sent to us or to anyone else. You can verify this in the page source on GitHub. Two opt-in exceptions, Feed Doctor Monitor and the paid SEPA file check, are described in sections 4 and 5. Session recording is disabled; section 6 describes its current status.

2Visit counts

We count page views and button clicks with self-hosted Umami on our own server. Umami sets no cookies and stores no personal data: no IP addresses, no names, no content of what you type. It records the page, the country, the browser type and which buttons were used. That is why there is no cookie banner: there are no cookies.

3The e-mail list

If you enter your e-mail in a "Tell me when a new tool lands" form, we store the address, the tool where you signed up, the language and the date. Nothing else. We use it only to tell you when a new tool is live. We do not send newsletters, we do not share or sell the list, and we do not track opens.

To be removed, reply to any mail we send or write to andrej@arling.sk. We delete the address within 7 days.

4Feed Doctor Monitor

This is the first of two opt-in exceptions to section 1: if you sign up for Feed Doctor Monitor (a paid add-on to the free Feed Doctor checker), it stores your e-mail, the feed URL and each check's results (score and issue counts); feed content is not stored. Its server fetches only the feed URL you gave it, on the schedule you chose (weekly on the free plan, daily on Pro), to alert you when something got worse. Delete it any time from the monitor page or by replying to an alert e-mail; deleting removes the e-mail, feed URL and history.

5SEPA file check

The second exception to section 1 is the paid SEPA file check. After paying, you upload a pain.001 file, a bulk payment order, on our page. It contains the names of the payer and the payees, their IBANs and the amounts, so as a rule also personal data of third parties, for example your suppliers. You, the customer, are the controller of that data; ARLing s. r. o. processes it solely to perform the check you ordered and for nothing else. We never use the file to train any model and we do not pass it on.

The file and a short order record (the Stripe checkout session id, the e-mail given at payment, the amount and currency, the time of payment and upload, the file size and whether you ticked the box on the upload page consenting to the service starting before the withdrawal period ends) are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV with a 30-day expiry from upload: when it runs out, the platform deletes them itself, whether or not anyone remembered. On request we delete them earlier. One file may be at most 5 MB and at most 5 files can be uploaded per payment. On upload the server only checks that the body is XML; the content is then read exclusively by Andrej Lauko, the managing director, manually.

Sub-processors for this service: Cloudflare (storage and processing; Cloudflare has data centres in the EU as well and the transfer is covered by standard contractual clauses) and Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (the payment and the check that the checkout session is paid before we accept a file). The notification that a new file has arrived is sent by our own server through ntfy and carries only the last 8 characters of the checkout session id, never the file or its content. Send deletion requests to andrej@arling.sk.

6Session recording is disabled

Our session recording tool has been disabled since 19 September 2026. It does not record or send screen content, scrolling, mouse movement or click sequences, and it does not create a visit identifier.

The previous text described an unfinished feature as an operational service. We have corrected that description. The feature is unfinished and is not in operation. This does not affect the aggregate visitor measurement described in section 2.

7E-mail and GitHub

If you write to us or open a GitHub issue, we keep that correspondence to answer you. Legal basis for all of the above: our legitimate interest in running the tools, your consent for the e-mail list and opt-in add-ons, and performance of a contract for paid services (GDPR art. 6(1)(a), (b) and (f)).

What we collect and what we never collect.

Tools run entirely in your browser. Whatever you paste into a form (addresses, configuration, XML) is processed on your own device and is never sent anywhere.

Traffic we count with our own Umami on our own server. Umami sets no cookies and stores no personal data: no IP addresses, no names, no form content. That is why we have no cookie banner: we have no cookies.

The e-mail list: if you enter your e-mail in the "Tell me when a new tool lands" form, we store the address, the tool, the language and the date. We use it only to send word of a new tool. No newsletter, no sharing, no open tracking. To unsubscribe: reply to any mail or write to andrej@arling.sk; we delete the address within 7 days.

Feed Doctor Monitor: the first of two opt-in exceptions to the section on tools. If you sign up for Feed Doctor Monitor (a paid add-on to the free Feed Doctor checker), we store your e-mail, the feed URL and the results of the checks (score and issue counts); feed content is not stored. The server fetches only the feed URL you gave it, on the schedule you chose (weekly for free, daily on Pro), and alerts you if something got worse. Cancel any time from the monitor page or by replying to an alert; the e-mail, feed URL and history are deleted.

SEPA file check: the second exception. The pain.001 file you upload after paying is kept at Cloudflare for at most 30 days and then deletes itself; it is read only by Andrej Lauko, is not used to train models and is not passed on anywhere. The push notification about a new file carries only the last 8 characters of the payment session id.

Session recording: disabled since 19 September 2026. This unfinished feature does not record or send the course of your visit and does not create a visit identifier.
