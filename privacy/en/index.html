PrivacyARLing s. r. o., BratislavaVersion of 30 September 2026

What we collect, and what we never collect.

ARLing s. r. o. publishes books and runs the tools on arling.sk. This page says plainly what happens to your data.

The Android apps have policies of their own: Word Search, Quiet Grids, Duel, Prism 5, Stop the Frame, One Peg, Stitch, Lanti: Lantern Run, Cast Off and Lantern Ferries.

Change of 8 October 2026: section 8b now covers the personalized advent treasure hunt (children's first names and ages, hiding spots, what we store, for how long and who processes it), and the Cloudflare row in section 10 names the crossword and the advent hunt.

Change of 3 October 2026: new section 8b on the personalized mystery case and the personalized crossword (what we store, for how long and who processes it).

Change of 30 September 2026: new section 10 with a table of who processes data with us and which data goes to the USA, and new section 11 on the arling.sk account. Section 2 now says exactly what Umami measurement receives and what it stores, and gives its legal basis; the previous version said Umami does not receive IP addresses, which was not true. Section 11 no longer says that Slovak accounting law makes us keep the purchases in the account for 10 years: it describes what the account actually does.

Change of 26 September 2026: section 5 describes the SEPA file check the way it is ordered today, with a form, without payment in advance and without uploading on the page. The previous version described a Stripe payment and a file upload that are no longer used.

1The tools

The tools that work on a file or text you put into them (for example SEPA pain.001 Doctor, SEPA pain.001 Generator, camt.053 to Excel, Payment matcher, the e-invoice, the GDPR documents, the contract templates and Feed Doctor) run in your browser. What you paste into them (URLs, configuration, XML files) is processed on your device and never sent to us or to anyone else; for a payment or a licence only the payment identifier goes to a server. You can verify this in the page source on GitHub. Two opt-in exceptions, Feed Doctor Monitor and the paid SEPA file check, are described in sections 4 and 5. Session recording is disabled; section 6 describes its current status.

Services that cannot work without a server are different: Proof, Renewals, Asistent, Stacklog, The World in Squares, Kontrola e-shopu, Memory Post, Puzzle Post, Puzzle Post Bulletin, the arling.sk account and payments. What you enter into them is processed on a server, not only in your browser. Which data each of them stores is described on its own page or in its FAQ; payments are processed by Stripe. For questions or deletion requests write to support@arling.sk.

Puzzle Post, Puzzle Post Bulletin and the e-mail after buying a digital title. These purchases are delivered by e-mail, so after payment we store your e-mail address on our server. For a Puzzle Post or Bulletin subscription it comes with the plan, the expiry date and the Stripe subscription and payment identifiers; for every issue or title sent, a record that it went out, so it does not arrive twice. We use the address only to deliver what you bought. The e-mails are sent through Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., USA) as our processor; section 10 describes the transfer to the USA. There is no automatic deletion period set yet: we delete the address and the sending records on request at support@arling.sk. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

2Visit counts

We count page views and button clicks with self-hosted Umami on our own server. Your browser sends the measurement data through our address api.arling.workers.dev, which runs on Cloudflare (section 10). We pass your IP address and the country Cloudflare works out from it on to Umami. Umami also estimates the region and city from the IP address and stores them with the visit; it does not store the IP address itself. Because of a mistake in our setup, records from 27 to 30 September 2026 often show the location of a Cloudflare server instead of yours.

What Umami stores: the page address and title, where you came from (the referrer and campaign tags in the address), the country, device type, browser, operating system, language, screen size, the time and which buttons you used. What it does not store: your IP address, name, e-mail or the content of what you type. From the IP address and browser, together with a regularly changed key, it computes a visit identifier so that one visit is not counted twice, and then discards the IP address. That identifier is pseudonymous personal data, which is why we mention it. Umami sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner. We have not yet set a fixed automatic deletion period for Umami records, including the older ones with the city and region.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing which pages and tools people use and in fixing what does not work (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object to this processing at any time (Art. 21 GDPR) by writing to support@arling.sk. You can also switch measurement off in your browser by blocking the script from api.arling.workers.dev.

3The e-mail list

If you enter your e-mail in a "Tell me when a new tool lands" form, we store the address, the tool where you signed up, the language and the date. Nothing else. We use it only to tell you when a new tool, or news about the tool where you signed up (for example a new ARLing Shopping Assistant feature), is ready. We do not send newsletters, we do not share or sell the list, and we do not track opens.

To be removed, reply to any mail we send or write to support@arling.sk. We delete the address within 7 days. There is no one-click unsubscribe yet; until we finish it, a reply to any of our e-mails is the way out. The list is stored on our own server. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

Alerts on new versions of the e-invoice FAQ. If you enter your e-mail in the "Notify me" box on /efaktura/ or /efaktura/zmeny-faq/, we store the address, where you signed up, the language and the date. We send you one e-mail with a confirmation link; an unconfirmed address is deleted after 7 days. A confirmed address is used only to e-mail you when the Slovak Financial Directorate publishes a new version of document 9/DPH/2025/IM, and kept until you unsubscribe. Every e-mail has an unsubscribe link; the address is then deleted from this list, usually within 10 minutes and at the latest within 7 days. E-mails are sent through Zoho Mail (EU data centre) as our processor. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

The "Get in touch" enquiry form. We store the e-mail, website and message you write, with the page it came from and the language, and also record them in our CRM (Twenty, running on our own server) so that we can answer you. No IP address and no cookies. We delete them on request. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Withdrawal from a contract on /odstupenie/en/. Your name, the contract details and your e-mail are sent by e-mail to us, and the confirmation of receipt to you; nothing is stored in a database. Legal basis: legal obligation (sec. 20a of Slovak Act No. 108/2024 Coll.).

4Feed Doctor Monitor

This is the first of two opt-in exceptions to section 1: if you sign up for Feed Doctor Monitor (a paid add-on to the free Feed Doctor checker), it stores your e-mail, the feed URL and each check's results (score and issue counts); feed content is not stored. Its server fetches only the feed URL you gave it, on the schedule you chose (weekly on the free plan, daily on Pro), to alert you when something got worse. Delete it any time from the monitor page or by replying to an alert e-mail; deleting removes the e-mail, feed URL and history.

5SEPA file check

The second exception to section 1 is the paid SEPA file check. You order it with the form on the service page; you pay nothing in advance and upload nothing on the page. From the order we store on our own server the e-mail address and the text you write into the form, a label of the page and language the order came from, and the time it was sent. We store no IP address, no browser data and no cookies. A notification about a new order is sent by our own server through ntfy and contains the page label, the e-mail address with the part before the @ hidden, and the beginning of your text. We also record the e-mail address, page label, language and date in our CRM (Twenty, running on our own server) so that we can handle the order. We have not yet set an automatic deletion period for this data; we delete it on request. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract and performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

You send us the pain.001 file, a bulk payment order, only after ordering, following the instructions we send you by e-mail. Until you send it, we do not have it. It contains the names of the payer and the payees, their IBANs and the amounts, so as a rule also personal data of third parties, for example your suppliers. You, the customer, are the controller of that data; ARLing s. r. o. processes it solely to perform the check you ordered and for nothing else. We do not use the file to train any model. We delete it at the latest 30 days after delivering the result, earlier on request.

Where the file will be stored, with which tools it will be checked and which processors will have access to it, we tell you in the e-mail with the instructions before you send the file, and we will add it here as well. We exchange e-mails with you through Zoho Mail (EU data centre), which acts as our processor for this correspondence. Stripe is not used for this service: ARLing s. r. o. issues the invoice only after delivering the result and it is paid by bank transfer; we keep the invoice data for as long as accounting law requires. Send deletion requests to support@arling.sk.

6Session recording is disabled

Our session recording tool has been disabled since 19 September 2026. It does not record or send screen content, scrolling, mouse movement or click sequences, and it does not create a visit identifier.

The previous text described an unfinished feature as an operational service. We have corrected that description. The feature is unfinished and is not in operation. This does not affect the aggregate visitor measurement described in section 2.

7E-mail and GitHub

If you write to us or open a GitHub issue, we keep that correspondence to answer you. Legal basis for all of the above: our legitimate interest in running the tools, your consent for the e-mail list and opt-in add-ons, and performance of a contract for paid services (GDPR art. 6(1)(a), (b) and (f)).

8Puzzle video maker and TikTok

At https://arling.sk/puzzle-video/ you can send a video you made to your own TikTok account as a draft. You sign in on TikTok's page; we never see your password. With your permission TikTok gives our server access with two scopes: user.info.basic, from which we read your display name and account id, and video.upload, the right to upload a draft to your inbox. We show you the display name so that you know which account receives the video; the account id is neither used nor kept. What TikTok gets from us: the video file and nothing else, no caption, no hashtags, no name. The video passes through our server only on its way to TikTok's upload address and is not saved. What we do not keep: no video, no TikTok token, no account id and no display name. The permission is kept encrypted in your browser tab, readable only by our server; it can upload for 15 minutes and can be revoked for 24 hours. If your browser blocks TikTok's sign-in window, the tab keeps a one-time sign-in check and your video settings in its session storage for at most 10 minutes. Ending the access: we ask TikTok to revoke the permission as soon as the draft is in your inbox or the upload failed, when you cancel, when our limits refuse the upload, when you do not send within 15 minutes, and when you close or leave the page. While TikTok is still processing the video we wait, because revoking then can stop the draft. If TikTok does not confirm a revoke, the permission expires at TikTok within 24 hours, and you can remove ARLing from the apps allowed in your TikTok settings at any time. Limits: to prevent abuse we count requests per connection under a pseudonymised fingerprint of your IP address (a keyed hash whose key changes every day and is held only by our server); the counters delete themselves within two days. We also count uploads per day as a single number without any address or account. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in preventing abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs: our server runs on Cloudflare Workers with Cloudflare's per-request logs switched off. When a call to TikTok fails, our server writes a short error line (HTTP status, TikTok's error code and TikTok's log number), never the video, the token or your name; Cloudflare adds its own technical details of the request to such a line and keeps it for at most 7 days. Processor: Cloudflare, Inc. TikTok processes the video under its own privacy policy.

8aCompanies we write to first

Sometimes we write first to a Slovak company that our service could help, for example to show an online shop a demo of Asistent built on its own products. We only write to a contact address the company published on its own website (for example info@), to each company individually and to only a few per day, never in bulk. We store the company name, its website, the published address and the page where we found it, the date of our message and your reply. If we use your shop's public product list for a demo, we delete the demo and its products within 30 days unless you switch Asistent on. We keep the other data for at most 12 months after the last message. If you do not want further messages, just reply “no”: we add your address and domain to a list we never write to again (we keep that list precisely so we do not write to you again) and delete the rest. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in offering a service to businesses (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, for e-mail, § 116 of Slovak Act No. 452/2021 Coll. on electronic communications, which allows writing to the published contact details of a legal person or entrepreneur if every message offers a way to refuse further ones. We send e-mail through Zoho Mail (EU data centre) as our processor and keep the record in our CRM (Twenty, running on our own server). Your rights: you have the right to access this data, to have it corrected or erased and to restrict its processing. You can object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR); since this is an offer of a service, after an objection we will not write to you again and we delete the data except the entry on the list we never write to. Just reply to our message or write to andrej@arling.sk. You also have the right to complain to the Slovak Data Protection Office (dataprotection.gov.sk).

8bPersonalized mystery case, crossword and advent treasure hunt

When you buy the personalized mystery case on Etsy, you type the names of your guests and, if you want, a name for the detective. Etsy passes these answers to us with the order. We use them only to build the files of your case. Etsy's own policy covers the purchase and the payment; building and delivering your case is done by us as described here.

What we store and for how long: the three PDF files of your case, which contain the names, and a record of the order's status with its order number. Both are deleted automatically 30 days after your case is built. Your surname from the Etsy order and the surname of the first guest on your list (and of the first guest we could use, if we had to leave the first one out) are stored only as fingerprints (one-way hashes) together with the order number, never as text, so that the download page can find your case. If you type the names again on the download page, the surname you enter there is stored the same way. We do not keep the names anywhere else and do not use them for anything else.

Who processes it: Cloudflare (section 10) builds the files in its browser service and keeps them in its storage for those 30 days. Legal basis: for your own surname, performance of the contract you made with your order (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for your guests' names, our legitimate interest in delivering the case you ordered (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You give us your friends' names yourself; please only use the names of people who will be happy to appear in a game.

Personalized crossword: for a crossword you type words and clues on Etsy, and optionally a title with a date and a name for the dedication. We use them only to build your crossword. We do not store them as text: they exist only in your two crossword PDF files, which are deleted automatically 30 days after they are built. The first word of your list and your surname from the order are stored only as fingerprints together with the order number, so that the download page can find your crossword. Who processes them, for how long and on what legal basis is the same as for the case above.

Personalized advent treasure hunt: for the advent hunt you type on Etsy the first names and ages of 1 to 4 children, optionally numbers of hiding spots from our menu, who the Day 24 letter is from and how siblings play. We use these answers only to build your hunt. What we store and for how long: the four PDF files of your hunt (clue cards and Grown-ups Guide, each in A4 and US Letter), which contain the names and ages, and a record of the order's status with the order number and only the number of children and of hiding spots. We do not store the names, the ages or the spot numbers as text anywhere else. The first name of the first child on your list and your surname from the Etsy order are stored only as fingerprints (one-way hashes) together with the order number, so that the download page can find your hunt; if you type the answers again on the download page, the surname you enter there is stored the same way. The files, the record and the fingerprints are deleted automatically 30 days after the hunt is built; for a hunt ordered from September to November, they stay until the end of December 28 if that is later, so that the links work through Christmas. The notifications we receive about orders contain the order number and status codes, never names. Who processes it: Cloudflare (section 10) builds the files in its browser service and keeps them in its storage for that time. Legal basis: for your own surname, performance of the contract you made with your order (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for the children's first names and ages, our legitimate interest in delivering the hunt you ordered (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Because these are children's data, we ask only for first names and ages, use them for nothing else and keep them only as long as described here. If you want them deleted sooner, send us a message on Etsy or write to support@arling.sk with your order number. Etsy keeps your order under its own policy.

11The arling.sk account

The arling.sk account has no password. You enter your e-mail, we send you a 6-digit code, and entering it signs you in.

What we store: your e-mail, the date the account was created, the list of purchases (product, amount, currency, date and Stripe payment identifier), subscriptions and your progress in the daily games if a game saves it to the account. When you sign in, we look up paid purchases with the same e-mail in Stripe and add them to the list. The sign-in code is valid for 15 minutes and then deleted. To prevent abuse we count code requests per e-mail, per IP address and per network, and wrong code attempts per e-mail; the hourly counters are deleted after an hour, the lock after wrong attempts and the daily network counters after 48 hours at the latest. The network counters hold no IP address, only a bucket number. Your browser remembers the sign-in in localStorage (not a cookie) for 90 days.

Why: so that you can reach what you bought from another device too. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for the abuse counters, our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How long, and how to delete it: we keep the account and the list of purchases in it for as long as the account exists; no automatic deletion period is set yet. The “Forget me” button on the account page removes your game progress and signs out every device; it does not cancel a subscription, which you cancel in the Stripe customer portal. It keeps the e-mail, the date the account was created and the list of purchases, so that you can still reach the files you paid for. To have those deleted too, write to support@arling.sk; we delete them to the extent the law does not require us to keep them. If you sign in again with the same e-mail, we look up your paid purchases in Stripe again. The payment records kept by Link as the seller and by Stripe are not deleted by this; their own policies apply (section 10). The account is stored at Cloudflare (section 10); the code e-mails are sent by Resend.

File tools run in your browser. Whatever you paste into them (addresses, configuration, XML) is processed on your own device and is never sent anywhere. Services such as Proof, Renewals, Asistent and Stacklog run on a server; each describes its data on its own page.

The e-mail list: if you enter your e-mail in the "Tell me when a new tool lands" form, we store the address, the tool, the language and the date. We use it only to send word of a new tool or news about the tool where you signed up. No newsletter, no sharing, no open tracking. To unsubscribe: reply to any mail or write to support@arling.sk; we delete the address within 7 days.

Feed Doctor Monitor: the first of two opt-in exceptions to the section on tools. SEPA file check: the second exception. You order it with a form and send the pain.001 file only afterwards, following the instructions in our e-mail. It is used only for the check you ordered, not to train models, and is deleted at the latest 30 days after the result is delivered.

Session recording: disabled since 19 September 2026. This unfinished feature does not record or send the course of your visit and does not create a visit identifier.
