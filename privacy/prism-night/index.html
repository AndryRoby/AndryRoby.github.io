Prism Night privacy policy

Updated 20 September 2026. This policy covers Prism Night, including its match-3 journey and 5×5 logic game. Earlier builds were named Daily Grid. The website's separate privacy policy does not replace this app policy.

Who is responsible

ARLing s. r. o., Ivanská cesta 19112/32E, 821 04 Bratislava, Slovakia, company ID 56583486. Contact andrej@arling.sk about privacy or support.

Your game stays on your device

The app stores puzzle positions, notes, move history, match-3 progress, results, streaks, preferences and advertising reward credits locally. It does not send this saved gameplay to an ARLing server. There is no player account, cloud sync, online leaderboard, chat, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics in this version. Android backup is disabled for the app. Clearing its storage or uninstalling it removes local progress; ARLing cannot restore it.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. Sharing a result opens Android's share sheet only when you choose to share; the service you select then handles the shared text under its own policy.

Google advertising services

The app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), with Google Play services components. Advertising-enabled builds may request ads when Google's consent status permits it. Internal test builds use Google's sample ad units and require an explicit test action in developer settings.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic/performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses these for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. SDK traffic is encrypted in transit. The app is therefore not a zero-data-collection app even though saved gameplay stays local.

See Google's SDK data disclosure, Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Google explains its retention practices and international processing in its policies. ARLing does not control Google's retention periods.

Your choices

Where required, Google's consent message lets you manage advertising choices. When available for your region, reopen it through Settings → Ad privacy choices. Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily prevent all advertising-related data processing. You can also manage or delete the advertising ID through your Android privacy settings. Gameplay works offline and does not require watching an advertisement.

We rely on consent where required for advertising storage, access or personalisation. Local game data is used to provide the game you request. If you contact us, we use your message and contact details to answer your request and retain them only as needed to handle it and any applicable legal obligations. Do not send passwords or identity documents to the support address.

Access, deletion and children

You can delete local game data through Android Settings → Apps → Prism Night → Storage → Clear storage. This also removes saved progress. For data you have sent directly to ARLing, contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or to object where applicable. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You can complain to the Slovak supervisory authority at dataprotection.gov.sk.

This version is not designed specifically for children. We do not ask players to submit personal profiles. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to ARLing, contact us so that we can investigate and address it.

Changes

We update this policy when the app's actual features or data handling change, and show the date above. An offline copy is available in the app's Settings → Privacy policy.
