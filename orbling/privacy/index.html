Privacy policy

What Orbling reads, writes, keeps and deletes

Orbling: AI Store Builder reads your product list, the products you choose, your automatic discounts and your store policies, of which it keeps only the shipping and refund text. It changes your store only after you prepare a preview or apply, never stores a buyer's name or contact details, deletes its access when you uninstall and deletes the rest when Shopify asks.

03

What the app writes to your store

Orbling changes nothing in your store until you prepare a preview or apply. It never changes product titles, descriptions, images, prices or variants, and it does not create collections, menus, pages or policies. It writes only these:

  • Product metafield $app:orbling, key stranka: the page content you apply. The product category, your confirmed facts, benefits, fit, box contents, questions, comparisons and audience texts, the look, the bundle tiers, and the text of your shipping and refund policies.
  • Product metafield $app:orbling, key nahlad: the same content as a draft. Your theme reads it only inside the theme editor.
  • App metafields orbling.look and orbling.look_preview: the look preset (colors, corner radius, spacing and type style), used on pages without Orbling product content, such as the home page.
  • Automatic discounts whose title starts with Orbling, followed by the product number, the quantity (2 or 3) and an operation key. Each applies only to that product from 2 or 3 items, has no end date and does not combine with other discounts.

Turning off. "Turn off bundle discounts" on the "Theme setup" screen hides the savings on the page and deletes the Orbling discounts for that product. The page content stays. If a price or an Orbling discount is changed or deleted in Shopify so that the saving no longer matches your settings, the app does the same by itself.

Cancelling the subscription stops further charges. It does not remove content or discounts that are already in your store.

Uninstalling ends the app's access, so after that it cannot change or remove anything in your store. Before you uninstall, select "Turn off bundle discounts" for each product, or afterwards delete the discounts whose title starts with Orbling in Shopify admin under Discounts. To hide the content, remove the Orbling blocks and the Orbling Look app embed in the theme editor.

04

Buyers and orders

The Orbling theme blocks set no cookies, use no browser storage and send nothing to our servers. Apart from loading their own script and style files from Shopify, the only request they can send goes to your store's own cart. Outside the theme editor preview, when a buyer submits a product form connected to the Buy box, the script adds a hidden _orbling cart attribute and line item property. Their JSON value is {"v":1,"product":"<id>","look":"<look>"}: a version, product ID and look name, with no personal data. It also sends the cart attribute to the store's own cart/update.js. The request has a four-second timeout in browsers supporting AbortSignal.timeout. If marking fails, the script still submits the purchase form; the order may then have no usable attribution.

Today the app receives no orders. The order events orders/create, orders/paid, orders/cancelled and refunds/create are switched on only after Shopify approves the app's access to protected customer data, without the name, address, phone and e-mail fields. Cart marking is already implemented. Orders can be counted only after approval and activation of those webhooks. Until then the Attributed revenue screen stays empty: no orders are counted and the usage charge is 0.00 USD.

Once they are switched on, Shopify sends each order event in full, and it can include buyer details. The app reads only these fields: order and line item IDs, product IDs, quantities, prices, discounts and taxes on marked lines, the currency, whether prices include tax, the created and updated times, whether the order is paid, a test or cancelled, the _orbling marker, and for refunds the refund ID, the refunded line items, quantities and amounts.

For each order event the app stores a record with the order ID, the times, the paid, test and cancelled flags, the marked lines (line ID, product ID, look, net amount, currency and quantity) and the refunds. The app does not store a buyer's name, e-mail, phone, address or customer ID, the IP address, the order note or the event itself.

06

Imported files and AI suggestions

CSV, TXT and PDF files you import are read in your browser. The file is never uploaded; only the text in the form is sent when you select "Build my page". PDFs are read without OCR and without any network request.

AI suggestions run on Cloudflare Workers AI (the Llama 3.3 70B model), when you select "Improve with AI" with an active trial or subscription, or automatically on return after approving the plan from Preview. The request contains our instructions, your confirmed facts with the source you entered for each, and the product title and category. It does not contain the product description, prices, your policies, your store domain, unconfirmed research, or any order or buyer data.

The app keeps only suggestions whose text appears word for word in one of those sources and discards the rest of the reply. Nothing reaches your page until you select "Accept all". Each store has 3 attempts per day (UTC), and one daily limit is shared by all stores.

Cloudflare states that it does not use customer content sent to Workers AI to train AI models (Workers AI data usage). The app sends store data to no other AI provider.

07

Uninstalling and erasure

  1. When you uninstall the app, we delete the access tokens as soon as we process the uninstall event (app/uninstalled). From then on the app runs no action, event or scheduled task for the store, other than Shopify's privacy requests. Your Orbling data stays until erasure.
  2. 48 hours after you uninstall, Shopify sends us a request to erase the store's data (shop/redact). When we process it, we delete everything Orbling stored for the store (page records, facts and research, discount, subscription, operation and order records, AI counters), and the store record, tokens, stored answers and locks.
  3. If erasure fails, the request stays to be retried and nothing is marked as done.
  4. If you reinstall the app before the erasure request arrives, erasure still deletes all Orbling data for the store, including what you created after reinstalling. Access for the new installation stays.
  5. A customers/redact request deletes the order records for the orders named in it.
  6. For a customers/data_request, the app records the request. Orbling stores no buyer's name or contact details, and today it holds no order records. If a request names orders the app holds technical records for, nothing is sent automatically: write to support@arling.sk and we send you those records by e-mail, prepared by hand.
  7. After erasure only the record of the handled request, the event delivery records, the Cloudflare technical logs and any support e-mails remain, for the periods in the table above.

Legal basis

Providing the app you installed, and the Orbling Growth subscription: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). This includes order records, which are used only to show attributed revenue and calculate the usage charge in the plan you approved.

Technical logs, operation records, protection against double processing and proof of handled requests: our legitimate interest in a secure and demonstrably correct service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Support e-mails: our legitimate interest in answering your questions about the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Store data is mostly business data. It can be personal data if the store is run by an individual, or if a product title, fact or research text contains a person's name.

The app makes no decisions with legal effect. Attributed revenue is a sum of marked order lines; it is not proof that Orbling caused a sale.

04

Kupujúci a objednávky

Bloky Orblingu v téme nenastavujú cookies, nepoužívajú úložisko prehliadača a nič neposielajú na naše servery. Okrem načítania vlastných súborov skriptov a štýlov zo Shopify môžu poslať jedinú požiadavku, a to do košíka vášho obchodu. Mimo náhľadu v editore témy pri odoslaní produktového formulára spojeného s Buy boxom skript pridá skrytý atribút košíka a vlastnosť položky _orbling. Hodnota JSON je {"v":1,"product":"<id>","look":"<look>"}: verzia, ID produktu a názov vzhľadu, bez osobných údajov. Atribút pošle aj do vlastného cart/update.js obchodu. V prehliadačoch s podporou AbortSignal.timeout má požiadavka limit štyri sekundy. Ak označenie zlyhá, skript aj tak odošle nákupný formulár; objednávka potom nemusí mať použiteľnú atribúciu.

Dnes appka nedostáva žiadne objednávky. Udalosti objednávok orders/create, orders/paid, orders/cancelled a refunds/create sa zapnú až po tom, čo Shopify schváli appke prístup k chráneným údajom zákazníkov, bez polí meno, adresa, telefón a e-mail. Značka košíka je už implementovaná. Objednávky možno započítavať až po schválení a zapnutí týchto webhookov. Dovtedy ostáva obrazovka Attributed revenue prázdna: nezapočíta sa žiadna objednávka a príplatok za používanie je 0.00 USD.

Po zapnutí Shopify pošle každú udalosť objednávky celú a môže v nej byť aj údaj o kupujúcom. Appka z nej číta len tieto polia: ID objednávky a položiek, ID produktov, množstvá, ceny, zľavy a dane na označených položkách, menu, či ceny obsahujú daň, čas vytvorenia a zmeny, či je objednávka zaplatená, testovacia alebo zrušená, značku _orbling a pri refundácii ID refundácie, refundované položky, množstvá a sumy.

Ku každej udalosti objednávky uloží záznam s ID objednávky, časmi, príznakmi zaplatená, testovacia a zrušená, označenými položkami (ID položky, ID produktu, vzhľad, čistá suma, mena a množstvo) a refundáciami. Appka neukladá meno, e-mail, telefón, adresu ani ID zákazníka, IP adresu, poznámku k objednávke ani samotnú udalosť.

Importované súbory a návrhy AI

Súbory CSV, TXT a PDF, ktoré importujete, sa čítajú vo vašom prehliadači. Súbor sa nikdy nenahráva; po stlačení „Build my page“ sa pošle len text vo formulári. PDF sa číta bez OCR a bez sieťovej požiadavky.

Návrhy AI bežia na Cloudflare Workers AI (model Llama 3.3 70B), a to keď s aktívnou skúškou alebo predplatným zvolíte „Improve with AI“, alebo automaticky po návrate zo schválenia plánu začatého v Preview. Požiadavka obsahuje naše pokyny, vaše potvrdené fakty so zdrojom, ktorý ste ku každému zadali, a názov a kategóriu produktu. Neobsahuje popis produktu, ceny, vaše zásady, doménu obchodu, nepotvrdený výskum ani údaje o objednávkach či kupujúcich.

Appka si nechá len návrhy, ktorých text sa doslova nachádza v niektorom z týchto zdrojov, a zvyšok odpovede zahodí. Na stránku sa nič nedostane, kým nezvolíte „Accept all“. Každý obchod má 3 pokusy za deň (UTC) a jeden denný limit je spoločný pre všetky obchody.

Cloudflare uvádza, že obsah zákazníkov poslaný do Workers AI nepoužíva na trénovanie modelov AI (Workers AI data usage). Žiadnemu inému poskytovateľovi AI appka údaje obchodu neposiela.

Odinštalovanie a výmaz

  1. Keď appku odinštalujete, prístupové tokeny zmažeme hneď, ako spracujeme udalosť odinštalovania (app/uninstalled). Odvtedy appka pre obchod nespustí žiadnu akciu, udalosť ani plánovanú úlohu okrem žiadostí Shopify o ochranu údajov. Údaje Orblingu ostávajú do výmazu.
  2. Shopify nám 48 hodín po odinštalovaní pošle žiadosť o výmaz údajov obchodu (shop/redact). Keď ju spracujeme, zmažeme všetko, čo Orbling o obchode uložil (záznamy stránok, fakty a výskum, záznamy zliav, predplatného, operácií a objednávok, počítadlá AI), a tiež záznam obchodu, tokeny, uložené odpovede a zámky.
  3. Ak výmaz zlyhá, žiadosť ostane na zopakovanie a nič sa neoznačí ako vybavené.
  4. Ak appku nainštalujete znova skôr, ako príde žiadosť o výmaz, výmaz aj tak zmaže všetky údaje Orblingu o obchode, aj tie vytvorené po novej inštalácii. Prístup novej inštalácie ostane.
  5. Žiadosť customers/redact zmaže záznamy objednávok, ktoré sú v nej uvedené.
  6. Pri žiadosti customers/data_request appka žiadosť zaznamená. Orbling neukladá meno ani kontakt kupujúceho a dnes nemá ani žiadne záznamy objednávok. Ak žiadosť uvádza objednávky, ku ktorým appka má technické záznamy, automaticky sa nič neodošle: napíšte na support@arling.sk a tieto záznamy vám pošleme e-mailom, pripravené ručne.
  7. Po výmaze ostáva len záznam o vybavenej žiadosti, záznamy o doručení udalostí, technické záznamy Cloudflare a prípadné e-maily podpory, každé do svojej lehoty (tabuľka vyššie).
