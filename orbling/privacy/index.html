Privacy policy

What Orbling reads, writes, keeps and deletes

Orbling: Product Page Builder reads your product list, the products you choose and your automatic discounts. Its Catalog check only reads your products. It does not read orders, customers or your store policies. It changes your store only when you build a preview or publish, never stores a buyer's name or contact details, deletes its access when you uninstall and deletes the rest when Shopify asks, with the exceptions in section 08.

02

What the app can read

Installing Orbling asks Shopify for four permissions, all required: read_products, write_products, read_discounts and write_discounts. Shopify asks you to approve them once, when you install. Before it writes anything the app checks in Shopify which access has been granted; if it is missing, nothing is changed and the app says so. It does not ask for theme write access (write_themes) or theme read access (read_themes), so it can neither see nor change your theme code. It does not ask for access to orders, customers or store policies.

The app sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics tracking. Inside the Shopify admin it loads only Shopify's scripts (App Bridge and Polaris) and its own script.

03

What the app writes to your store

The Catalog check changes nothing in your store. Orbling changes nothing either until you build a preview or publish. It never changes product titles, descriptions, images, prices or variants, and it does not create collections, menus, pages or policies. It writes only these:

  • Product metafield $app:orbling, key stranka: the page content you publish. The product category, your confirmed facts, benefits, fit, box contents, questions, comparisons and audience texts, the delivery and return days you enter, the look and the bundle tiers. Your shipping and refund policies are not copied: the Product story block reads them from your store in Shopify each time the page is shown, as a short excerpt with a "Read the full policy" link. They never reach Orbling's servers.
  • Product metafield $app:orbling, key nahlad: the same content as a draft from your last "Build my page". Your theme reads it only inside the theme editor. Publish deletes it; if that fails, the next Publish tries again.
  • App metafield orbling.look: the published look preset (colors, corner radius, spacing and type style), used on pages without Orbling product content, such as the home page. App metafield orbling.look_preview: the look from your last "Build my page", deleted when you publish. App metafield orbling.home: the handle and ID of the product you last published, so the Home block can feature it when no product is chosen in its settings.
  • Automatic discounts titled with the product name (shortened if long) and the offer, for example "Desk light: Buy 2, save 10%" or, for a fixed amount, "Desk light: Buy 3, save 5.00 USD each". If another discount already uses that title, the name gets a number, such as "Desk light #2: Buy 2, save 10%". Buyers can see this title at checkout. Each discount has a tag starting with orbling- followed by 24 letters and digits derived from the operation, applies only to that product from 2 or 3 items, has no end date and does not combine with other discounts.

Turning off. "Turn off bundle discounts" on the "Theme setup" screen hides the savings on the page and deletes the Orbling discounts for that product. "Turn off all bundle discounts" on the "Plan and billing" screen does the same for every product that still has Orbling discounts, also without a paid plan. The page content stays. If a price or an Orbling discount is changed or deleted in Shopify so that the saving no longer matches your settings, the app does the same by itself.

Changing or cancelling your plan in Shopify stops further charges. Shopify can apply a change to Free at the end of the billing cycle. Orbling checks your plan regularly. After it has confirmed twice, at least one hour apart, that no paid plan is active, it turns off the bundle discounts it created, the same way as "Turn off all bundle discounts". Your published page content stays in your store. To turn bundles back on, choose a plan and publish the page again.

Uninstalling ends the app's access, so after that it cannot change or remove anything in your store. Orbling's discounts stay in your store after you uninstall. Before you uninstall, select "Turn off all bundle discounts" on "Plan and billing", or afterwards delete Orbling's discounts in Shopify admin under Discounts: their titles are the product name followed by "Buy 2, save" or "Buy 3, save", and each has a tag starting with orbling-. Discounts that Orbling created before it used product names are titled "Orbling" followed by the product ID, 2 or 3 and a code, and have no orbling- tag. To hide the content, remove the Orbling blocks and the Orbling Look app embed in the theme editor.

04

Buyers and orders

The Orbling theme blocks set no cookies, use no browser storage and send no requests of their own, so nothing reaches our servers from your storefront. Apart from loading their own script and style files from Shopify, the Buy box only sets the quantity in your theme's product form; your theme then adds the item to the cart as usual. The blocks add nothing to the cart or to orders.

Orbling does not ask Shopify for access to orders or customers and does not subscribe to order or refund events. It does not read, count or store orders, refunds or buyer data, and it does not use protected customer data. Plans are billed by Shopify and do not depend on your sales, so billing needs no order data.

The app does not store a buyer's name, e-mail, phone, address or customer ID, or a buyer's IP address. The only buyer-related requests it handles are Shopify's mandatory privacy requests (section 08).

06

Imported files

CSV, TXT and PDF files you import are read in your browser. The file is never uploaded; only the text in the form is sent when you select "Build my page". PDFs are read without OCR and without any network request.

The app does not send your product data, facts or research to any AI model. Your page shows only your Shopify product data and the facts you confirm. The Catalog check does not use AI either.

07

Plans and billing

Orbling has two plans, Free and Growth, billed by Shopify through Shopify App Pricing. You choose and change your plan on Shopify's plan page, and Shopify charges it on your Shopify invoice. Orbling takes no payment and never sees a card number. The app screen "Plan and billing" describes the plans.

When you open the app and when you return from the plan page, Orbling asks Shopify which plan your store has, using the ID of the app and the ID of your store. For stores with active Orbling bundle discounts it also asks regularly in the background, every 10 minutes for a limited number of stores. It stores the plan, its status, whether it is a test plan and the end of the free trial, and the time of its last check and of a first confirmation that no paid plan is active (section 05). If Orbling cannot confirm your plan, paid features stay off, your saved work is kept and nothing is turned off. After two confirmations at least one hour apart, it turns off its bundle discounts as section 03 describes.

08

Uninstalling and erasure

  1. When you uninstall the app, we delete the access tokens as soon as we process the uninstall event (app/uninstalled). From then on the app runs no action, event or scheduled task for the store, other than Shopify's privacy requests. Your Orbling and Catalog check data stays until erasure.
  2. 48 hours after you uninstall, Shopify sends us a request to erase the store's data (shop/redact). If the app is still uninstalled when we process it, we delete everything the app stored for the store (Catalog check results, history, limits, queue and plan status, the plan end check, Orbling page records, facts and research, progress in the app, discount and operation records), and the store record, tokens, stored answers and locks.
  3. If you reinstall the app before we process the erasure request, that request deletes the stored answers from before the reinstall. The store record and your other data (Catalog check results, Orbling page records, facts and research, progress in the app, discount and operation records) stay with the new installation until they are erased as described in point 2. Access for the new installation stays.
  4. If erasure fails, the request stays to be retried and nothing is marked as done.
  5. For a customers/redact request, the app records the request. Orbling holds no order or buyer records, so there is nothing else to delete.
  6. For a customers/data_request, the app records the request. Orbling stores no buyer's name or contact details and holds no order records, so it has no buyer data to send. If you have a question about such a request, write to support@arling.sk.
  7. After erasure only the record of the handled request, the event delivery records, the Cloudflare technical logs and any support e-mails remain, for the periods in the table above.

Zverejnený obsah stránky vo vašom obchode ostane. Balíčky zapnete znova tak, že vyberiete plán a stránku znova zverejníte.

Odinštalovanie ukončí prístup appky, takže potom už v obchode nič nezmení ani neodstráni. Zľavy Orblingu po odinštalovaní v obchode ostanú. Pred odinštalovaním zvoľte „Turn off all bundle discounts“ na obrazovke „Plan and billing“, alebo potom zmažte zľavy Orblingu v administrácii Shopify v časti Discounts: ich názov tvorí názov produktu a za ním „Buy 2, save“ alebo „Buy 3, save“ a každá má štítok, ktorý začína orbling-. Zľavy, ktoré Orbling vytvoril skôr, než začal používať názvy produktov, sa volajú „Orbling“, za ním ID produktu, 2 alebo 3 a kód, a štítok orbling- nemajú. Obsah skryjete tak, že v editore témy odstránite bloky Orbling a vložený prvok appky Orbling Look.

Kupujúci a objednávky

Bloky Orblingu v téme nenastavujú cookies, nepoužívajú úložisko prehliadača a neposielajú vlastné požiadavky, takže z vášho obchodu sa na naše servery nič nedostane. Okrem načítania vlastných súborov skriptov a štýlov zo Shopify Buy box len nastaví množstvo v produktovom formulári vašej témy; tovar potom do košíka pridá téma ako zvyčajne. Bloky nič nepridávajú do košíka ani do objednávok.

Orbling nežiada od Shopify prístup k objednávkam ani zákazníkom a neodoberá udalosti objednávok ani refundácií. Objednávky, refundácie ani údaje kupujúcich nečíta, nepočíta ani neukladá a chránené údaje zákazníkov nepoužíva. Plány fakturuje Shopify a nezávisia od vašich predajov, takže účtovanie nepotrebuje údaje o objednávkach.

Appka neukladá meno, e-mail, telefón, adresu ani ID zákazníka, ani IP adresu kupujúceho. Jediné žiadosti týkajúce sa kupujúcich, ktoré vybavuje, sú povinné žiadosti Shopify o ochranu údajov (časť 08).

07

Plány a platby

Orbling má dva plány, Free a Growth, ktoré fakturuje Shopify cez Shopify App Pricing. Plán si vyberáte a meníte na stránke plánov Shopify a Shopify ho účtuje na vašej faktúre Shopify. Orbling neprijíma platby a číslo karty nikdy nevidí. Plány opisuje obrazovka „Plan and billing“ v appke.

Keď appku otvoríte a keď sa vrátite zo stránky plánov, Orbling sa Shopify spýta, aký plán má váš obchod, pričom použije ID appky a ID vášho obchodu. Pri obchodoch s aktívnymi zľavami balíčkov Orblingu sa pýta aj pravidelne na pozadí, každých 10 minút pre obmedzený počet obchodov. Uloží plán, jeho stav, či ide o testovací plán, koniec skúšky zdarma a čas poslednej kontroly a prvého potvrdenia, že žiadny platený plán nie je aktívny (časť 05). Ak Orbling plán nevie potvrdiť, platené funkcie ostanú vypnuté, váš uložený rozpracovaný obsah sa zachová a nič sa nevypína. Po dvoch potvrdeniach s odstupom najmenej hodinu vypne svoje zľavy balíčkov podľa časti 03.

Odinštalovanie a výmaz

  1. Keď appku odinštalujete, prístupové tokeny zmažeme hneď, ako spracujeme udalosť odinštalovania (app/uninstalled). Odvtedy appka pre obchod nespustí žiadnu akciu, udalosť ani plánovanú úlohu okrem žiadostí Shopify o ochranu údajov. Údaje Orblingu a Catalog check ostávajú do výmazu.
  2. Shopify nám 48 hodín po odinštalovaní pošle žiadosť o výmaz údajov obchodu (shop/redact). Ak appka pri jej spracovaní stále nie je nainštalovaná, zmažeme všetko, čo appka o obchode uložila (výsledky Catalog check, históriu, limity, frontu a stav plánu, kontrolu konca plánu, záznamy stránok Orblingu, fakty a výskum, postup v appke, záznamy zliav a operácií), a tiež záznam obchodu, tokeny, uložené odpovede a zámky.
  3. Ak appku nainštalujete znova skôr, ako žiadosť o výmaz spracujeme, táto žiadosť zmaže uložené odpovede spred novej inštalácie. Záznam obchodu a ostatné údaje (výsledky Catalog check, záznamy stránok Orblingu, fakty a výskum, postup v appke, záznamy zliav a operácií) ostanú novej inštalácii, kým ich nezmaže výmaz podľa bodu 2. Prístup novej inštalácie ostane.
  4. Ak výmaz zlyhá, žiadosť ostane na zopakovanie a nič sa neoznačí ako vybavené.
  5. Pri žiadosti customers/redact appka žiadosť zaznamená. Orbling nemá záznamy objednávok ani kupujúcich, takže nič iné na zmazanie nie je.
  6. Pri žiadosti customers/data_request appka žiadosť zaznamená. Orbling neukladá meno ani kontakt kupujúceho a nemá záznamy objednávok, takže nemá žiadne údaje kupujúceho na odoslanie. Ak máte k takej žiadosti otázku, napíšte na support@arling.sk.
  7. Po výmaze ostáva len záznam o vybavenej žiadosti, záznamy o doručení udalostí, technické záznamy Cloudflare a prípadné e-maily podpory, každé do svojej lehoty (tabuľka vyššie).
