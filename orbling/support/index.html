What to put in your message

  • The screen you were on. The app shows it at the top, for example ORBLING / Plan and billing. Add the button you selected.

02

Build your product page

  1. Welcome: select "Choose a product". Pick a card from your catalog.
  2. Build: select Nest, Halo or Torque. Bundles default to 2 items at 10% off and 3 at 15% off. Expand "Change bundle settings" to check costs, change discounts or turn bundles off.
  3. Select "Build my page". Your catalog is enough for a preview. "Add your own facts (optional)" accepts verified research or a merchant CSV; it is not required.
  4. Preview: check the content, then "Open in theme editor". Enable Orbling Look for the look. Use "Theme setup checklist" and "Add Product story" to place the block and see draft content. A link alone does not activate a block.
  5. To request AI suggestions, select "Improve with AI, 7-day free trial". Read the pricing and usage terms, then "Approve the plan in Shopify". The request resumes on return. If the trial was already used, the button says "Improve with AI, review subscription".
  6. Check the source beside each suggestion, select "Accept all", then "Publish". If no sourced suggestions are available, Publish lets you use verified catalog content.
  7. On "Theme setup", enable Orbling Look, add Buy box near the product form, add Product story and optionally Home. Select a product in Home. Save in the theme editor. Publish activates verified discounts when bundles are on.

Common questions

I selected Publish and saw "Action not completed". What now?

"Publish" and "Improve with AI" need an active trial or subscription. Open "Plan", select "Review free trial in Shopify" (or "Review subscription in Shopify" if your trial was already used), then "Approve the plan in Shopify", and try again.

If the plan is active, check the following, because the app shows these cases only as "Action not completed":

  • A product is selected, and it has at most 100 variants.
  • For every variant, the price per item after the bundle discount stays above the "Cost plus shipping per item" you entered.
  • The saving per item for 3 items is larger than the saving per item for 2.
  • Your store has fewer than 24 active automatic discounts, so there is room for the two Orbling discounts.

If it still fails, write to us with the screen and the product title.

Does the free preview change my live store?

No. "Build my page" saves a draft that your theme shows only inside the theme editor. Your live product page stays unchanged until you select "Publish". Bundle cards appear only after you publish. The Home block never shows the draft: your facts and benefits appear there only after you publish.

One thing to know: if you turn on the Orbling Look app embed or add an Orbling block and save the theme, that change is live in your published theme right away, as with any theme change.

Why is no saving shown on my product page?

A saving appears only after Orbling has created its automatic discounts and checked them. That happens when you select "Publish" with bundle offers on, which needs an active trial or subscription.

If the discount is changed or deleted in Shopify, or a price no longer stays above the "Cost plus shipping per item" you entered, Orbling turns the saving off by itself. A fixed amount saving is hidden when the buyer shops in a currency other than your store currency. Discounts apply from 2 or from 3 items of the same product and do not combine with other discounts.

Why is Attributed revenue empty?

The Buy box now adds a hidden _orbling cart attribute and line item property containing only a version, product ID and look name, with no personal data. It sends the attribute to your store's own cart/update.js; if marking fails, the purchase form still submits. Today the app receives no orders. Order and refund webhooks remain disabled pending Shopify's approval of protected customer data access, without name, e-mail, phone or address fields. Until approval and activation of those webhooks, no orders are counted, "Revenue" stays empty and the usage charge is 0.00 USD. In live billing, the base subscription applies after the trial.

Test orders, cancelled orders, tax and shipping are never counted. Attributed revenue is not proof that Orbling caused a sale.

How is the usage charge calculated?

The base price is 29.00 USD every 30 days, after a 7-day free trial (one trial while Orbling holds your store's data). On top of it, a usage charge depends on the attributed revenue in the 30-day cycle, after discounts and without tax, shipping, test orders, cancellations and refunds:

  • Up to 1000.00 USD0.00 USD
  • Up to 2500.00 USD10.00 USD
  • Up to 5000.00 USD20.00 USD
  • Up to 10000.00 USD35.00 USD
  • Up to 20000.00 USD65.00 USD
  • Up to 35000.00 USD110.00 USD
  • Up to 50000.00 USD150.00 USD
  • Above 50000.00 USD195.00 USD

Tiers are not cumulative: you pay the charge of one tier, not a sum. There is no usage charge during the trial. The usage cap is 500.00 USD per cycle and the current maximum total is 224.00 USD per cycle. Only USD revenue is billable. The same terms are in the app on "Plan", under "All revenue tiers and terms".

Why did no AI suggestions appear?

Suggestions need an active trial or subscription. Orbling keeps only text that appears word for word in your confirmed facts, the product title or the category, so with few confirmed facts there may be nothing to suggest. Unconfirmed research is not used.

After you approve the plan from Preview, the queued AI request resumes automatically. Existing subscribers select "Improve with AI". If the request fails, select "Retry AI improvement". If no sourced suggestions are available, you can still publish verified catalog content. Each store has 3 attempts per day (UTC), and one daily limit is shared by all stores, so suggestions can be unavailable until the next day. Nothing reaches your page until you select "Accept all".

Which files can I import?

Merchant CSV: up to 12,000 characters and 80 rows, with exactly this header:

product_id,section,label,value,source,confirmed

The product_id is the product's ID in the form gid://shopify/Product/ followed by its number. Use true only for facts you have checked.

Research: a TXT or PDF file up to 2 MB, with up to 10,000 characters of text. You can also type lines as section | label | value, which needs no product ID. PDFs are read in your browser; scanned or unsupported PDFs need pasted text. Files are never uploaded, only the text you save. What you save at once must stay under 16 KB in total.

Does Orbling work with my theme?

Your theme must support app blocks. Orbling does not edit theme files: you place the blocks yourself from the links on "Theme setup" and save in Shopify. Links alone do not activate anything.

By default the Orbling Look app embed gives your theme's color schemes and corner radius the look you chose, while the header, footer and layout stay. You can turn this off in the embed settings "Apply look colors to the whole theme" and "Apply look shape and type to the whole theme".

Does Orbling build my whole store?

No. For the products you choose, it builds page blocks from your catalog and confirmed facts, a look and quantity discounts for 2 and 3 items. Its revenue view stays empty until order access is approved and webhooks are enabled. It does not create your header, footer, menus, collections, pages, policies or domain, it does not import products or text from links or other websites, and it does not change your product titles, descriptions, images or prices.

What does "Cancel subscription" do?

It stops further charges for that subscription; charges already submitted may remain on your Shopify invoice. Publish and AI suggestions lock again. Bundle discounts already created stay in your store until you select "Turn off bundle discounts", and applied content stays visible until you remove the Orbling blocks in the theme editor. A new subscription starts without another free trial.

What should I do before I uninstall?

After uninstalling, Orbling can no longer change your store, so select "Turn off bundle discounts" on "Theme setup" for each product first. If you have already uninstalled, delete the discounts whose title starts with Orbling in Shopify admin under Discounts. Remove the Orbling blocks and the Orbling Look app embed in the theme editor. Uninstalling stops future charges.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app. Access is removed as soon as we process the uninstall event, and store data is deleted when Shopify sends its erasure request 48 hours later, except the few records the privacy policy lists with their own retention.

Otázky a riešenia

Publish hlási „Action not completed“

Publish a AI potrebujú aktívnu skúšku alebo predplatné. V „Plan“ použite „Review free trial in Shopify“, prípadne „Review subscription in Shopify“, a potom „Approve the plan in Shopify“.

Produkt môže mať najviac 100 variantov. Cena každého variantu po zľave na kus musí zostať nad zadaným nákladom s dopravou. Úspora na kus pri 3 kusoch musí byť väčšia než pri 2 kusoch. Obchod musí mať menej než 24 aktívnych automatických zliav. Tieto prípady appka hlási všeobecne. Ak chyba trvá, napíšte podpore.

Mení náhľad živý obchod?

„Build my page“ ukladá koncept pre editor témy. Zverejnený obsah produktu sa nemení do „Publish“. Karty balíčkov sa objavia až po zverejnení. Home nečíta koncept; názov, fotku a tlačidlo môže ukázať už z nastavenia produktu, vaše fakty a výhody až po zverejnení. Ak v publikovanej téme uložíte zapnutý embed alebo pridaný blok, táto zmena témy je okamžite živá.

Prečo sa neukazuje úspora?

Úspora sa ukáže až po vytvorení a overení automatických zliav pri Publish. Pri zmene alebo zmazaní zľavy či nevyhovujúcej cene ju Orbling vypne. Pevná úspora sa skryje pri inej mene kupujúceho. Zľavy platia od 2 alebo od 3 kusov rovnakého produktu a nekombinujú sa s inými zľavami.

Prečo je Attributed revenue prázdne?

Buy box už pridáva skrytý atribút košíka a vlastnosť položky _orbling: len verziu, ID produktu a vzhľad, bez osobných údajov. Atribút posiela do vlastného cart/update.js obchodu. Pri zlyhaní označenia sa nákupný formulár aj tak odošle.

Dnes appka nedostáva žiadne objednávky. Webhooky objednávok a refundácií ostávajú vypnuté do schválenia prístupu k chráneným údajom zákazníkov bez mena, e-mailu, telefónu a adresy. Do schválenia a zapnutia webhookov sa nezapočíta žiadna objednávka a usage je 0.00 USD. Pri ostrej fakturácii platí základné predplatné po skúške. Testy, zrušené objednávky, dane a doprava sa nerátajú, refundácie sa odpočítajú. Pripísané tržby nedokazujú, že predaj spôsobil Orbling.

Koľko stojí plán?

Základ je 29.00 USD za 30 dní po 7-dňovej skúške. Skúška sa neopakuje, kým Orbling uchováva údaje obchodu. Príplatok závisí od pripísaných tržieb za cyklus po zľavách, bez dane, dopravy, testov, zrušení a refundácií.

Tržby do 1000.00 USD: príplatok 0.00 USD; do 2500.00: 10.00; do 5000.00: 20.00; do 10000.00: 35.00; do 20000.00: 65.00; do 35000.00: 110.00; do 50000.00: 150.00; nad 50000.00: 195.00 USD. Stupne sa nesčítavajú. Počas skúšky nie je usage. Strop je 500.00 USD za cyklus, aktuálne maximum základu a príplatku spolu 224.00 USD. Účtovateľné sú len tržby v USD. Presné podmienky sú v „Plan“, „All revenue tiers and terms“.

Prečo AI nič nenavrhla?

AI potrebuje aktívny plán a ponechá len text, ktorý sa doslova nachádza v potvrdených faktoch, názve alebo kategórii. Nepotvrdený výskum nepoužíva. Každý obchod má 3 pokusy denne podľa UTC; platí aj spoločný denný limit. Návrhy prijmete cez „Accept all“ a zverejníte cez „Publish“.

Aké súbory možno pridať?

Voliteľný CSV má najviac 12 000 znakov a 80 riadkov, hlavičku product_id,section,label,value,source,confirmed a ID produktu vo formáte gid://shopify/Product/ s číslom. true použite len pri overených faktoch. Výskum TXT alebo PDF môže mať 2 MB a najviac 10 000 znakov textu. Formát section | label | value nepotrebuje ID. Súbory sa čítajú v prehliadači, nenahrávajú sa; po „Build my page“ sa posiela text. Skenované či nepodporované PDF nahraďte vloženým textom. Jeden zápis musí zostať pod 16 KB.

Téma musí podporovať app bloky. Orbling neupravuje súbory témy. Embed predvolene mení farby a polomer rohov, hlavička, päta a rozloženie ostávajú. V nastaveniach možno vypnúť „Apply look colors to the whole theme“ a „Apply look shape and type to the whole theme“.

Orbling nestavia celý obchod. Dopĺňa bloky stránky produktu z katalógu a potvrdených faktov, vzhľad a množstevné zľavy. Nevytvára menu, kolekcie, zásady ani doménu, neimportuje obsah z odkazov a nemení názvy, popisy, obrázky ani ceny produktov.
