02

Set up Orbling, step by step

  1. Install Orbling from the Shopify App Store. Installing asks Shopify for four permissions: read_products, write_products, read_discounts and write_discounts. Orbling opens on its own page, "Orbling". If you used the app when it was previously called ARLing: Agentic Product Check, your catalog check is still here, under "Catalog check" in the app menu.
  2. Optional first look: in the app menu select "Catalog check", then "Run catalog check". It reads your products and lists those with missing or incomplete data, such as images, descriptions or product type. Complete data gives your page more to show. Catalog check does not change your store, and Orbling's page builder does not use its results. Home and the Build step also show the card "Check your product data" with the link "Open Catalog check".
  3. On the Orbling page: select "Choose a product". Pick a card. Orbling lists only active products on the Online Store sales channel. If a product is not on your online store, Orbling says so: in Shopify set it to Active and add it to the Online Store sales channel, then choose it again.
  4. Build your page, offers: in "Bundle offers", bundles default to 2 items at 10% off and 3 at 15% off. Select Edit to enter "Cost plus shipping per item", change the discounts or turn bundles off. The defaults do not guarantee a profit. The cost field is empty for a new product, and Orbling keeps bundle prices above your cost only after you enter your cost. Until then, "Bundle offers" and the Summary say "Cost not entered. Orbling cannot keep bundle prices above your cost." and the preview's next step says "Enter your cost per item in Build so bundle prices stay above it." You can still publish.
  5. Build your page, delivery and returns (optional): open "Delivery and returns" and fill in "Delivery time in business days, from", "Delivery time in business days, to" and "Returns accepted within (days)". The app says: "Shown to shoppers exactly as you enter it. Leave empty to hide." Orbling never fills in or guesses these days. After you publish, the Buy box shows them (theme editor setting "Show delivery and returns"), or the block "Orbling: Delivery info" does when you place it below Buy buttons.
  6. Build your page, style: "Style in your store" shows "Theme style": Orbling blocks use the colors and fonts of your theme, with nothing to set up. "Orbling Look (optional)" offers Nest, Halo or Torque, and the app says: "Used in your store only when the Orbling Look embed is on."
  7. Select "Build my page". Your catalog is enough for a preview. Expand "Your facts" to import facts from a CSV file or paste research lines in the form section | label | value; it is not required. Orbling shows the facts you confirm as you wrote them, except facts that match our lists of links, ratings or reviews, environmental, health or compliance claims, safety or manufacturer details, superlatives or urgency. Build and Preview name each confirmed fact your page leaves out and why, so you can rewrite it.
  8. Preview: check the content. To see your draft in your theme, add Buy box once: on "Theme setup" select "Open in theme editor" next to "Add Buy box" and save. Buy box then shows your draft: the bundle cards with the prices they will have after you publish and the note "Draft. Bundle prices apply after you publish in Orbling." Only you see the draft, in the theme editor; shoppers do not. "See your draft in the theme editor" opens your product in the theme editor without adding another block; the preview says: "If you have not added Buy box yet, add it in Theme setup." "Edit offers and facts" takes you back to Build, and "Theme setup" lists every block.
  9. Plans: Free covers building a page, the preview and the Catalog check. "Publish" needs Growth. Select "Choose a plan in Shopify" in the preview or on "Plan and billing" in the app menu. Shopify opens its plan page: pick Growth, monthly or yearly, and approve it there. A store that has not used it gets a 7-day free trial on Growth. Shopify then returns you to Orbling, which reads your plan from Shopify. The answer about cost below has the details.
  10. With Growth, open the preview again from the Orbling page. "Next step" shows "Ready to publish": select "Publish". Orbling writes the page content to your product and, with bundle offers on, creates the bundle discounts. If you have not entered your cost, the preview first says "Enter your cost per item in Build so bundle prices stay above it." You can still publish.
  11. On "Theme setup", "Open in theme editor" next to each step adds that block: put Buy box right above Buy buttons, add Product story and Home the same way and save the theme in Shopify. Each of these links adds its block, so use it once; "Open theme editor" only opens the theme editor at your product. Saving the theme makes the blocks live right away on every product page that uses this template. Until you publish, they show your Shopify product details. Your page content goes live when you publish. Before that, the Home step shows "After you publish". "Theme tips" explains where each block lands in Dawn and Horizon. "Optional: Orbling Look embed" is off until you turn it on. Choosing a product in Home is optional: with none chosen, Home features the product you last published with Orbling. Publish with bundle offers on creates and checks the automatic discounts.

Common questions

Can I try Orbling before I pay?

Yes, on the Free plan: build a page, see the draft in your theme editor, where shoppers do not see it, and run Catalog check with full details for up to 3 products. "Publish" and bundle offers need Growth, which starts with a 7-day free trial if this store has not used one. To cancel, choose Free on Shopify's plan page (Plan and billing, Change plan in Shopify); cancelling stops future charges, and charges already submitted may remain on your Shopify invoice.

Two steps change your live store, even during a trial: saving Orbling blocks in your theme makes them live on every product page that uses the template you added them to, and "Publish" puts your page content live and, with bundle offers on, creates the automatic discounts in Shopify. You can turn Orbling's bundle discounts off at any time on every plan, including Free.

How much does Orbling cost?

Orbling has two plans, billed by Shopify, with no fee on your sales. Free: build product pages, preview them in your theme and check your catalog. Growth: 19 USD a month or 190 USD a year. Publish pages, bundle offers and all catalog check features. Growth starts with a 7-day free trial if this store has not used one.

The sentences from "Free:" to "has not used one." are the exact sentences the app shows on "Plan and billing", together with "Features by plan" and the button to choose or change your plan in Shopify. On Free, Catalog check shows full details for up to 3 products and counts for the rest; Growth shows full details for every product. The answer about the limits of Catalog check below has the details.

How do I change or cancel my plan?

Open "Plan and billing" in the app menu, select "Change plan in Shopify" and make the change on Shopify's plan page. Orbling cannot change or cancel a plan for you. Cancelling stops future charges; charges already submitted may remain on your Shopify invoice.

Shopify can apply a change to Free at the end of the billing cycle. Until then "Plan and billing" shows the date and paid features stay on. After that, Publish needs Growth again and the preview stays free. Orbling checks your plan regularly. After it has confirmed twice, at least one hour apart, that no paid plan is active, it turns off the bundle discounts it created. Your published page content stays in your store. To turn bundles back on, choose a plan and publish the page again.

Does Orbling build my whole store?

No. For the products you choose, it builds page blocks from your catalog and confirmed facts, quantity discounts for 2 and 3 items and, if you enter them, delivery and return days. It does not track orders or sales. It does not create your header, footer, menus, collections, pages, policies or domain, it does not import products or text from links or other websites, and it does not change your product titles, descriptions, images or prices. Orbling writes only to its own product and app metafields (the draft from "Build my page", the content you publish, the look and the Home block) and, when you publish with bundle offers on, creates two automatic discounts for that product. It does not edit theme files: you place its blocks yourself in the theme editor.

Does Orbling work with my theme?

Your theme must support app blocks. Buy box, Product story and Home need an Online Store 2.0 theme (for example Dawn or Horizon). The Orbling Look embed can be turned on in any theme that supports app embeds. Orbling does not edit theme files: you place the blocks yourself from the links on "Theme setup" and save in Shopify. Links alone do not activate anything.

Put the Buy box right above Buy buttons. If you leave it lower, it still works and tells shoppers that their choice sets the quantity above. "Theme tips" on "Theme setup" says where each block lands in Dawn and Horizon. To move a block, drag it in the theme editor sidebar. To remove it, select the block, remove it and select Save. Our example pages use only Dawn and Horizon, so if a block looks wrong in your theme, write to us with the theme name.

How long does setup take?

We have not measured a typical setup time, so we do not promise one. Setup has four parts, and the steps above give every button name: choose an active product on your online store; set your bundle offers and "Cost plus shipping per item"; select "Build my page" for a free preview, for which your catalog is enough; then, with Growth, select "Publish", place the blocks from "Theme setup" in the theme editor and save the theme.

How are bundle prices worked out?

For each product you set two offers, 2 items and 3 items, both as a percentage or both as a fixed amount off each item, starting from the defaults in "Bundle offers" (step 4 above). For example, on a 48.00 USD product, 5.00 USD off each of 2 items makes 43.00 USD each, 86.00 USD in total. When you select "Publish", Orbling checks for every variant that the price per item after the discount stays above the "Cost plus shipping per item" you entered, and that 3 items save more per item than 2. If not, it publishes your page without bundle offers, and the message says what is wrong.

The cost field is empty for a new product and an empty cost counts as 0, so until you enter your cost the app says "Cost not entered. Orbling cannot keep bundle prices above your cost." The defaults do not guarantee a profit. The discounts are Shopify automatic discounts for that one product and do not combine with other discounts.

Which permissions does Orbling ask for?

Installing Orbling asks Shopify for four permissions: read_products, write_products, read_discounts and write_discounts. read_products lets Orbling and the Catalog check read your products; write_products saves page content to your products; read_discounts and write_discounts create and check the bundle discounts. Orbling asks for all four permissions at install, so there is no Grant access step. You approve once, in the Shopify approval window.

Before every change the app checks in Shopify which access is granted. If something is missing, for example before you approve an update of its permissions, nothing is changed and you see the message about access. Orbling never asks for access to orders, customers, store policies or your theme code.

Where is my data stored?

Orbling runs on Cloudflare Workers with a Cloudflare D1 database. Cloudflare's servers are worldwide, with transfers to the USA under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework certification and standard contractual clauses. Orbling stores a store record, page records with your product details and bundle settings, the facts you enter, records of its own discounts and Catalog check results; access tokens are encrypted. It does not read orders or customers and stores no buyer data.

The privacy policy lists every record and how long it is kept, and the answer about deleting your data explains deletion. The operator is ARLing s. r. o. in Bratislava, Slovakia.

What happens when I uninstall, and what should I do first?

Orbling's bundle discounts stay in your store after you uninstall, and after uninstalling Orbling can no longer change your store. So first select "Turn off all bundle discounts" on "Plan and billing" (or "Turn off bundle discounts" on "Theme setup" for each product). If you have already uninstalled, delete Orbling's discounts in Shopify admin under Discounts: their titles are the product name followed by "Buy 2, save" or "Buy 3, save", and each has a tag starting with orbling-. Discounts that Orbling created before it used product names are titled "Orbling" followed by the product ID, 2 or 3 and a code, and have no orbling- tag. Remove the Orbling blocks and the Orbling Look app embed in the theme editor. Uninstalling stops future charges. Your store data is deleted later, as the answer about deleting your data explains.

Does Orbling write my product text with AI?

No. Orbling does not use AI to write or suggest product text. Your page shows your Shopify product details and the facts you confirm, from a CSV file or research lines you paste, and only facts you mark as checked become page content. Orbling does not check whether your facts are true. Your page content goes live when you select "Publish".

What does Catalog check do?

Catalog check reads your products and lists those with missing or incomplete data, with the rule and its source for each issue. It does not change your store and does not predict sales or visibility. Larger catalogs are read in parts: if you close the app, the check continues in the background and the result is waiting when you come back. Select "Run catalog check" if the check does not start by itself.

A rule says unknown when the app could not read that data, for example a metafield the store did not allow it to read, or more variants or media than one request reads. Unknown is never counted as a pass; it lowers coverage instead. Draft and archived products are counted as out of scope; only active and unlisted products are checked. How we count explains each rule.

I selected Publish and saw "Action not completed". What now?

Orbling then shows a message with the reason. For the first four reasons nothing was changed; for the last one Orbling publishes your page without bundle offers. The most common reasons:

  • Your plan. Publishing needs the Growth plan. The message says: Publishing and bundle offers need the Growth plan. Nothing was changed. Open Plan and billing to choose it in Shopify.
  • Your access. The message says: Orbling does not have access to edit products and discounts yet. Select Grant access and approve it in Shopify, then try again. Nothing was changed. Open Orbling from your Shopify admin and approve the permission update Shopify shows, then select Publish again.
  • The product. A product is selected, it is on your online store and it has at most 100 variants. For a larger product the message says "Orbling supports products with up to 100 variants. Choose another product." If the product was deleted in Shopify, Orbling says so; choose another product.
  • Delivery and return days, when you entered them: whole numbers, delivery from 1 to 90 business days with "from" not above "to", returns from 1 to 365 days. The message appears next to the field.
  • Bundle offers, when they are on: for every variant, the price per item after the bundle discount stays above the "Cost plus shipping per item" you entered (an empty cost counts as 0, so then this check only keeps prices above zero); the saving per item for 3 items is larger than the saving per item for 2; and your store has fewer than 24 active automatic discounts, so there is room for the two Orbling discounts.

For the last point the message says what is wrong, and Orbling publishes your page without bundle offers. Change the bundle offer or end an automatic discount you no longer need, then select Publish again. If it still fails, write to us with the screen and the product title.

Does the free preview change my live store?

The preview does not, but saving Orbling blocks in your theme does. "Build my page" saves a draft that your theme shows only inside the theme editor, with a note that it is a draft. There the Buy box shows your bundle cards with the prices they will have after you publish, marked "Draft. Bundle prices apply after you publish in Orbling." The saving itself starts only when you publish, because Orbling creates the bundle discounts in Shopify then. Shoppers never see the draft: your Orbling content reaches your live product page only when you select "Publish". The Home block never shows the draft: your facts and benefits appear there only after you publish.

Saving the theme makes the blocks live right away on every product page that uses this template. Until you publish, they show your Shopify product details. This is the same as any theme change: a saved Product story or Buy box block shows your product's own details from Shopify and your store settings to shoppers: Product story shows key specs and your store policies when set, plus the description when you turn on its setting "Show product description", and Buy box shows your store's payment methods or, when nothing else applies, availability and policy links. The theme editor shows the same unless a draft from "Build my page" exists; then these blocks show the latest draft with a note. "Publish" deletes the draft, so after you publish the theme editor shows the same content as your store.

Shipping and returns in Product story always come from your store policies in Shopify, as a short excerpt with a "Read the full policy" link. Orbling does not copy them, so a policy change shows without publishing again. The delivery and return days from "Delivery and returns" are separate: they show exactly as you entered them.

Why is no saving shown on my product page?

A saving appears on your store page only after Orbling has created its automatic discounts and checked them. That happens when you select "Publish" with bundle offers on, which needs the Growth plan. Before that, only the theme editor shows the bundle prices, in your draft and marked as a draft.

If the discount is changed or deleted in Shopify, or a price no longer stays above the "Cost plus shipping per item" you entered, Orbling turns the saving off by itself. It also turns its bundle discounts off after your paid plan has ended (see the answer about changing your plan). A fixed amount saving is hidden when the buyer shops in a currency other than your store currency. Discounts apply from 2 or from 3 items of the same product and do not combine with other discounts.

Does Orbling track my orders or sales?

No. Orbling does not ask for access to orders or customers and receives no order or refund events, so it does not count orders or sales and stores no buyer data.

The Buy box only sets the quantity in your theme's product form. It sends no requests of its own and adds nothing to the cart or to orders. Plan prices do not depend on your sales.

Which files can I import?

Merchant CSV: up to 12,000 characters and 80 rows, with exactly this header (Build shows an example under "CSV format"):

product_id,section,label,value,source,confirmed

The product_id is the product's ID in the form gid://shopify/Product/ followed by its number. Use true only for facts you have checked.

Research lines: type them, or import a TXT or PDF file up to 2 MB with up to 10,000 characters of text. Each line is one fact in the form section | label | value, which needs no product ID. Orbling does not turn other text into facts: if a line has another form, Build my page says "Use one line per fact: section | label | value. Nothing was saved." PDFs are read in your browser; scanned or unsupported PDFs need pasted text. Files are never uploaded, only the text you save. What you save at once must stay under 16 KB in total.

What does the Orbling Look embed do?

Orbling blocks use the colors and fonts of your theme; that is the "Theme style" card in Build. The Orbling Look embed adds optional extras, and every one of them is off until you turn it on: a sticky buy bar on mobile for themes without their own, and the colors and corners of your look for themes with color schemes, such as Dawn. If you leave the embed off, your theme looks the same as before and the look you picked in "Orbling Look (optional)" is not used. You can turn it on or off in the theme editor under app embeds.

I fixed a product, but the issue is still there. What are the limits of Catalog check?

Select "Re-check" next to the product. On the Free plan, "Re-check" is shown for the up to 3 products with full details, 3 re-checks each calendar month, and Orbling runs the next full check by itself 30 days after the last one, which updates the counts for every product. On Growth, every product is shown in full, new and changed products (including imported ones) are checked automatically, there is a weekly check and a history, you can download all findings as a CSV file and there is no re-check limit.

The app was previously called ARLing: Agentic Product Check. What changed?

Orbling was previously called ARLing: Agentic Product Check. It is the same app with the same store: your catalog check is now "Catalog check" in the app menu, and it still only reads your products. Orbling adds the page builder, which is the only part that writes to your store. For it, Shopify asks you once to approve access to edit products and discounts.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app. Access is removed as soon as we process the uninstall event, and store data is deleted when Shopify sends its erasure request 48 hours later, except the few records the privacy policy lists with their own retention. If you reinstall the app before we process that request, your data, such as page records, facts and Catalog check results, stays with the new installation.

Otázky a riešenia

Dá sa Orbling vyskúšať pred platbou?

Áno, v pláne Free: postavíte stránku, koncept uvidíte v editore témy, kde ho kupujúci nevidia, a spustíte Catalog check s plným detailom najviac 3 produktov. „Publish“ a balíčky potrebujú Growth, ktorý začína 7-dňovou skúškou zdarma, ak ju obchod ešte nevyužil. Zrušíte ho tak, že na stránke plánov Shopify (Plan and billing, Change plan in Shopify) zvolíte Free; zrušenie zastaví ďalšie poplatky a už odoslané môžu zostať na faktúre Shopify.

Aké oprávnenia Orbling žiada?

Pri inštalácii Orbling žiada od Shopify štyri oprávnenia: read_products, write_products, read_discounts a write_discounts. read_products dovolí Orblingu a Catalog check čítať vaše produkty; write_products ukladá obsah stránky k produktom; read_discounts a write_discounts vytvárajú a overujú zľavy balíčkov. Orbling žiada všetky štyri oprávnenia pri inštalácii, preto krok Grant access nie je. Schvaľuje sa raz, vo vlastnom okne Shopify.

Čo robí Catalog check?

Catalog check prečíta vaše produkty a vypíše tie, ktorým chýbajú alebo sú neúplné údaje, pri každom probléme s pravidlom a jeho zdrojom. Váš obchod nemení a nepredpovedá predaje ani viditeľnosť. Aké súbory možno pridať?

Voliteľný CSV má najviac 12 000 znakov a 80 riadkov, hlavičku product_id,section,label,value,source,confirmed (príklad ukáže Build pod „CSV format“) a ID produktu vo formáte gid://shopify/Product/ s číslom. true použite len pri overených faktoch. Riadky výskumu napíšte alebo importujte zo súboru TXT či PDF do 2 MB a najviac 10 000 znakov textu. Každý riadok je jeden fakt vo formáte section | label | value a nepotrebuje ID. Iný text Orbling na fakty neprevádza: ak má riadok iný tvar, Build my page povie „Use one line per fact: section | label | value. Nothing was saved.“ Súbory sa čítajú v prehliadači, nenahrávajú sa; po „Build my page“ sa posiela text. Skenované či nepodporované PDF nahraďte vloženým textom. Jeden zápis musí zostať pod 16 KB.

Vložený prvok Orbling Look pridáva voliteľné doplnky a každý z nich je vypnutý, kým ho nezapnete: lepkavú lištu na nákup na mobile pre témy, ktoré ju nemajú, a farby a rohy vášho vzhľadu pre témy s farebnými schémami, napríklad Dawn. Ak ho necháte vypnutý, vaša téma vyzerá ako predtým a vzhľad vybraný v „Orbling Look (optional)“ sa nepoužije. Zapnete ho alebo vypnete v editore témy v časti app embeds.
