Orbling vs EComposer

Similar price, different scope. Plans, page limits, conversion tools and bundle offers side by side, from public pages read on 13 November 2026.

Status on 13 November 2026. Orbling: Product Page Builder is in the Shopify App Store with the Free and Growth plans described here. It is the new name of ARLing: Agentic Product Check, one app with one listing. When you install it, Shopify asks you to let Orbling read and edit your products and discounts, which it needs to save page drafts, publish page content and create bundle offers; the Catalog check only reads.

At a glance

Choose EComposer if
you want to build many page types from templates with AI help, and add conversion tools such as upsells, bundles, wishlist and compare in the same app (popups and A/B tests from the Pro plan).
Choose Orbling if
you want the same three blocks on your product pages (Buy box, Product story, Delivery info), filled from your own product data and the facts you confirm, with 2- and 3-item offers checked against the cost you enter and a plan that lists no page limit.
Price
EComposer Free; Standard 25 USD a month or 228 USD a year on its App Store listing. Orbling Free (no publishing); Growth 19 USD a month or 190 USD a year.

Where EComposer is the better choice.

  • You want many kinds of pages.Landing, home, collection, blog, cart and 404 pages from templates in an editor. Orbling only fills product page blocks and a Home block for one product.
  • You want more conversion tools in one app.EComposer lists upsells, cross-sells, bundles, volume discounts, wishlist and compare, and a popup builder from Pro. Orbling has only its 2- and 3-item bundle offers.
  • You want AI help with pages.EComposer lists an AI page builder and AI Page Audit. Orbling does not offer this.
  • You want A/B tests and page analytics.EComposer lists page analytics from Standard and A/B tests from Pro. Orbling has neither.
  • You want something live on the free plan.EComposer Free allows 1 live page or section. Orbling needs Growth to publish.
  • You want chat support and an app with many reviews.EComposer lists 24/7 live chat from Standard, has the Built for Shopify badge and 3,459 reviews. Orbling has no reviews yet.

Where Orbling fits.

  • No design work.You want the same structure on your product pages (Buy box, Product story with key specs and questions, Delivery info), built from your product data and the facts you confirm.
  • Offers checked against your cost.For 2 and 3 items, Orbling checks for every variant that the price per item after the discount stays above the cost you enter. If you leave the cost empty, the check only keeps prices above zero, and the defaults do not guarantee a profit.
  • Find gaps in product data.The catalog check lists products with missing data, such as images, descriptions or product type, with the rule and its source.
  • A plan not counted in pages.Growth is one plan, billed by Shopify, with no fee on your sales.
  • Your theme code stays as it is.Orbling does not edit theme files and does not ask for theme access. Its blocks use your theme's colors and fonts.

What Orbling does not do.

Orbling builds product page blocks and bundle offers for 2 and 3 items, and checks your catalog data. This is what it leaves out.

Other limits, such as theme requirements and what to do before uninstalling, are in the Orbling support answers: scope, themes and uninstalling.

  • It has no page editor and no templates. It does not build your whole store or free-form pages: no header, menus, collections, landing pages or policies.

  • It has no popups, wishlist, compare or cross-sell. It adds no reviews, ratings or countdowns.

  • It does not run A/B tests. It does not track orders or sales.

  • It does not write or suggest text with AI.

  • Bundles are only 2 and 3 items of the same product. Each product with bundles uses two Shopify automatic discounts, and Orbling publishes bundles only while the store has fewer than 24 active automatic discounts (at most 12 products with bundles if there are no other automatic discounts).

Questions.

Which one costs less?

It depends on billing and what you need. Monthly, Orbling Growth is 19 USD and EComposer Standard is 25 USD. Yearly, Orbling Growth is 190 USD and EComposer Standard is 228 USD (19 USD a month on its pricing page). EComposer Free can publish 1 live page or section; Orbling Free cannot publish.

Does EComposer have bundles like Orbling?

EComposer's listing names bundles and volume discounts among its conversion tools. We did not find 2- and 3-item offers with a check against your own cost described in its listing or pricing page as read on 13 November 2026. That does not prove it is missing; ask EComposer if this matters to you.
