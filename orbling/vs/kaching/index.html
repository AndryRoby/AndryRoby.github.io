Quantity offers on your Shopify product page. Plans, bundle types, cost checks and limits side by side, from public pages read on 13 November 2026.

Status on 13 November 2026. Orbling: Product Page Builder is in the Shopify App Store with the Free and Growth plans described here. It is the new name of ARLing: Agentic Product Check, one app with one listing. When you install it, Shopify asks you to let Orbling read and edit your products and discounts, which it needs to save page drafts, publish page content and create bundle offers; the Catalog check only reads.

Where Kaching is the better choice.

  • You want more than buy 2, buy 3.Mix and match, build a box, BOGO, free shipping offers, subscription boxes or bundles across different products. Orbling does none of these.
  • You want bundles on many products.Kaching paid plans list unlimited bundle deals. Orbling publishes bundles on at most 12 products if the store has no other automatic discounts.
  • You want upsells, gifts and add-ons.Kaching lists product page upsells, free gifts and one-click add-ons such as shipping protection or gift wrap.
  • You want to A/B test offers and see conversions.Orbling does not test offers or track sales.
  • Your extra revenue through the app stays within 1,000 USD a month.Then Kaching Starter at 14.99 USD costs less than Orbling Growth at 19 USD.
  • You need integrations or other languages.Kaching lists Shopify POS, Flow and page builders such as PageFly, GemPages and EComposer, and its listing is in 10 languages. Orbling lists no integrations and is in English only.
  • You prefer an app with many reviews.Kaching has the Built for Shopify badge and 6,721 reviews, and has been in the App Store since August 2022. Orbling has no reviews yet.

Where Orbling fits.

  • Offers and the page around them in one app.A Buy box right above the Buy buttons, Product story with key specs and answers, and delivery and return days exactly as you enter them.
  • Each offer is checked before it goes live.For every variant, the price per item after the discount must stay above the "Cost plus shipping per item" you entered, and 3 items must save more per item than 2. If you leave the cost empty, the check only keeps prices above zero, and the defaults do not guarantee a profit.
  • A price that does not change with your sales.Growth is 19 USD a month or 190 USD a year, billed by Shopify, with no fee on your sales.
  • Find gaps in product data.The catalog check lists products with missing data, such as images, descriptions or product type, with the rule and its source.
  • No access to orders or customers.Orbling asks for four permissions: read and edit products and discounts. It does not ask for access to orders, customers or theme code.

What Orbling does not do.

Orbling builds product page blocks and bundle offers for 2 and 3 items, and checks your catalog data. This is what it leaves out.

Other limits, such as theme requirements and what to do before uninstalling, are in the Orbling support answers: scope, themes and uninstalling.

  • It offers only 2 and 3 items of the same product. No mix and match, BOGO, gifts, free shipping offers or bundles across products.

  • Its bundles use Shopify automatic discounts. Each product with bundles uses two, they do not combine with other discounts, and Orbling publishes bundles only while the store has fewer than 24 active automatic discounts (at most 12 products with bundles if there are no other automatic discounts).

  • It does not run upsells, cross-sells or A/B tests.

  • It does not track orders or sales. That is why it shows no bundle analytics.

  • It does not write or suggest text with AI.

Questions.

Which one costs less?

It depends on your sales through the app. Kaching Starter is 14.99 USD a month for up to 1,000 USD of additional revenue through the app, and its price rises in tiers above that. Orbling Growth is 19 USD a month or 190 USD a year whatever you sell.

Does Kaching check bundle prices against my cost?

We did not find a check against your own cost described in Kaching's listing or pricing page as read on 13 November 2026. That does not prove it is missing; ask Kaching if this matters to you. In Orbling the check is part of Publish.
