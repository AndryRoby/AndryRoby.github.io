Orbling and other Shopify apps

Orbling builds product page blocks and 2- and 3-item bundle offers checked against your cost. Here it is next to 10 other Shopify apps, from their public App Store listings and their makers' pages, read on 13 November 2026.

Status on 13 November 2026. Orbling: Product Page Builder is in the Shopify App Store with the Free and Growth plans described here. It is the new name of ARLing: Agentic Product Check, one app with one listing. When you install it, Shopify asks you to let Orbling read and edit your products and discounts, which it needs to save page drafts, publish page content and create bundle offers; the Catalog check only reads.

At a glance

Choose a page builder if
you want to design landing pages, home pages or free layouts yourself, from templates in an editor.
Choose a bundle app if
you want many bundle types, such as mix and match, gifts or bundles across products, upsells or A/B tests of offers.
Choose Orbling if
you want the same three blocks on your product pages (Buy box, Product story, Delivery info), filled from your own product data and the facts you confirm, with 2- and 3-item offers checked against the cost you enter.

Page builders.

Apps for designing many kinds of pages in an editor. Choose one of them if you want free layout, templates or landing pages; Orbling has no page editor.

  • Orbling vs GemPagesA drag-and-drop page builder with 400+ templates, sales funnels, A/B tests of offers and AI help. Its Free plan publishes 1 page; paid plans from 29 USD a month on its listing.
  • Orbling vs PageFlyA page builder with 320+ templates, AI page creation, and A/B tests and heatmaps on higher plans. Its Free plan publishes 1 page or section; Builder from 24 USD a month.
  • Orbling vs EComposerA page builder with 400+ templates, AI tools and built-in conversion extensions such as upsells, bundles and wishlist. Its Free plan publishes 1 page or section; Standard 25 USD a month.
  • Orbling vs ShogunA visual page builder for many page types, with reusable sections and the Shopify Plus Certified badge. Draft Mode is free without publishing; Build 39 USD a month for 25 published pages.
  • Orbling vs ReploA landing page builder for ecommerce teams, with 1000+ templates, A/B tests and analytics. Its App Store free plan is for previews; Starter 119 USD a month for 15 published items.
  • Orbling vs Zipify PagesA page builder for funnels, landing and product pages, with split tests and AI copy. Starter 19 USD a month for 5 landing pages and 1 product page; its free plan is for development and partner stores.

Bundle and quantity offer apps.

Apps for bundles, quantity breaks and upsells. Choose one of them if you need many bundle types; Orbling offers only 2 and 3 items of the same product.

  • Orbling vs Kaching BundlesA bundle and upsell app with many bundle types, gifts, add-ons, A/B tests and bundles suggested by AI, priced by the extra revenue it brings in, from 14.99 USD a month.
  • Orbling vs BundlerA bundle app whose Free plan includes quantity discounts and Buy X get Y with no revenue or order limit; Premium 9.99 USD and Executive 19.99 USD a month.
  • Orbling vs Fast BundleA bundle and upsell app with many bundle types, free gifts and AI recommendations, priced by monthly bundle sales from 19 USD a month; free for eligible merchants up to 500 USD of bundle sales.
  • Orbling vs Bundle BearA volume discount app for products, collections or the whole store, with mix and match, X+Y bundles and stacking with discount codes. From 29.99 USD a month on its listing.
