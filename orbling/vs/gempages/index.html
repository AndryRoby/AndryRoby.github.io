Orbling vs GemPages

Which one fits your Shopify product pages? Plans, page building, bundle offers and limits side by side, from public pages read on 13 November 2026.

Status on 13 November 2026. Orbling: Product Page Builder is in the Shopify App Store with the Free and Growth plans described here. It is the new name of ARLing: Agentic Product Check, one app with one listing. When you install it, Shopify asks you to let Orbling read and edit your products and discounts, which it needs to save page drafts, publish page content and create bundle offers; the Catalog check only reads.

At a glance

Choose GemPages if
you want to design many kinds of pages yourself in a drag-and-drop editor, with funnels, A/B tests of offers and AI help.
Choose Orbling if
you want the same three blocks on your product pages (Buy box, Product story, Delivery info), filled from your own product data and the facts you confirm, with 2- and 3-item offers checked against the cost you enter.

Where GemPages is the better choice.

  • You want to design pages freely.Landing pages, product pages and theme sections from 400+ templates in a drag-and-drop editor. Orbling has no page editor and no templates.
  • You need sales funnels or post-purchase upsells.GemPages lists pre-sell advertorials, post-purchase upsells and downsells, plus countdowns and stock counters. Orbling has no funnels or upsells and adds no countdowns.
  • You want to A/B test offers.GemPages lists A/B tests of funnel offers with analytics. Orbling does not test offers or track sales.
  • You want to publish a page without paying.GemPages Free publishes 1 page. Orbling needs Growth to publish.
  • You want AI help to build pages.GemPages MCP lets you build and refine pages in an AI chat, from the Free plan. Orbling does not offer this.
  • You rely on other apps or languages.GemPages says it connects with 160+ apps, for example Judge.me, Klaviyo and Omnisend, and its listing is also in Spanish, German and Chinese. Orbling lists no integrations and is in English only.
  • You prefer an app with many reviews.GemPages has 4,265 reviews with an average of 4.9 and has been in the App Store since 2017. Orbling has no reviews yet.

Where Orbling fits.

  • No design work.Your products are already in Shopify and you want each product page to show the same blocks (Buy box, Product story, Delivery info), built from your own product data and the facts you confirm.
  • Bundle offers checked against your cost.For 2 and 3 items, Orbling checks for every variant that the price per item after the discount stays above the cost you enter. If you leave the cost empty, the check only keeps prices above zero, and the defaults do not guarantee a profit.
  • A catalog check in the same app.It lists products with missing or incomplete data, for example images, descriptions or product type, with the rule and its source.
  • One flat paid plan.Growth is billed by Shopify, with no fee on your sales.
  • Your theme code stays as it is.Orbling does not edit theme files and does not ask for theme access. Its blocks use your theme's colors and fonts.

What Orbling does not do.

Orbling builds product page blocks and bundle offers for 2 and 3 items, and checks your catalog data. This is what it leaves out.

Other limits, such as theme requirements and what to do before uninstalling, are in the Orbling support answers: scope, themes and uninstalling.

  • It has no page editor and no templates. It does not build your whole store: no header, menus, collections, landing pages or policies.

  • It has no funnels or post-purchase upsells. It adds no reviews, ratings or countdowns.

  • It does not run A/B tests. It does not track orders or sales.

  • It does not write or suggest text with AI.

  • Bundles are only 2 and 3 items of the same product. Each product with bundles uses two Shopify automatic discounts, and Orbling publishes bundles only while the store has fewer than 24 active automatic discounts (at most 12 products with bundles if there are no other automatic discounts).

Questions.

Does Orbling use AI to build my pages, like GemPages MCP?

No. Your page shows your Shopify product details and the facts you confirm. If you want AI help with page building, GemPages offers it and Orbling does not.

GemPages also lists quantity breaks and bundles. What is different in Orbling?

Orbling's offers are narrower: exactly 2 and 3 items of the same product. Before it publishes, it checks for every variant that the price per item after the discount stays above the "Cost plus shipping per item" you entered, and that 3 items save more per item than 2. We did not find a cost check like this described in GemPages' listing or pricing page as read on 13 November 2026; ask GemPages if this matters to you.
