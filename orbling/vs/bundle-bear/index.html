Orbling vs Bundle Bear

Volume discounts and quantity offers on Shopify. Plans, offer types, cost checks and limits side by side, from public pages read on 13 November 2026.

Status on 13 November 2026. Orbling: Product Page Builder is in the Shopify App Store with the Free and Growth plans described here. It is the new name of ARLing: Agentic Product Check, one app with one listing. When you install it, Shopify asks you to let Orbling read and edit your products and discounts, which it needs to save page drafts, publish page content and create bundle offers; the Catalog check only reads.

Where Bundle Bear is the better choice.

  • You want discounts across collections or the whole store.Bundle Bear lets you set discounts on specific products, collections or your entire store. Orbling makes offers per product, on at most 12 products if the store has no other automatic discounts.
  • You want more offer types.Mix and match, X+Y bundles, cart value goals, free gifts and free shipping incentives. Orbling offers only 2 and 3 items of the same product.
  • Your customers also use discount codes.Bundle Bear says its offers can be stacked with manual discount codes. Orbling bundle discounts do not combine with other discounts.
  • You want to see how offers perform.Bundle Bear lists analytics on every plan. Orbling does not track sales.
  • You want a longer trial.Its App Store listing shows a 14-day free trial on every plan; its website says 7 days. Orbling Growth has 7 days.
  • You prefer an app with reviews.Bundle Bear has 151 reviews with an average of 4.6 and has been in the App Store since 2021. Orbling has no reviews yet.

Where Orbling fits.

  • The offer and the page around it from one app.A Buy box right above the Buy buttons with 1-, 2- and 3-item choices and the price each, Product story with key specs and answers, and delivery and return days exactly as you enter them.
  • Each offer is checked before it goes live.For every variant, the price per item after the discount must stay above the "Cost plus shipping per item" you entered, and 3 items must save more per item than 2. If you leave the cost empty, the check only keeps prices above zero, and the defaults do not guarantee a profit.
  • A lower monthly price.Growth is 19 USD a month or 190 USD a year, billed by Shopify, with no fee on your sales. Bundle Bear starts at 29.99 USD a month.
  • Find gaps in product data.The catalog check lists products with missing data, such as images, descriptions or product type, with the rule and its source.
  • No access to orders or customers.Orbling asks for four permissions: read and edit products and discounts. It does not ask for access to orders, customers or theme code.

What Orbling does not do.

Orbling builds product page blocks and bundle offers for 2 and 3 items, and checks your catalog data. This is what it leaves out.

Other limits, such as theme requirements and what to do before uninstalling, are in the Orbling support answers: scope, themes and uninstalling.

  • It offers only 2 and 3 items of the same product. No mix and match, X+Y bundles, cart value discounts, gifts or free shipping offers.

  • Its bundles use Shopify automatic discounts. Each product with bundles uses two, they do not combine with other discounts, and Orbling publishes bundles only while the store has fewer than 24 active automatic discounts (at most 12 products with bundles if there are no other automatic discounts).

  • It does not set discounts on collections or the whole store.

  • It does not track orders or sales. That is why it shows no bundle analytics.

  • It does not write or suggest text with AI.

Questions.

Which one costs less?

Orbling Growth is 19 USD a month or 190 USD a year. Bundle Bear's App Store listing shows plans from 29.99 USD a month, named after Shopify plans, up to 74.99 USD for Shopify Plus; its own website shows one plan at 29.99 USD a month. Neither runs offers on a free plan: Orbling Free builds and previews pages, and Bundle Bear has a free trial.

Does Bundle Bear check bundle prices against my cost?

We did not find a check against your own cost described in Bundle Bear's listing or website as read on 13 November 2026. That does not prove it is missing; ask Bundle Bear if this matters to you. In Orbling the check is part of Publish.
