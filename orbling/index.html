Turn the products you import into product pages with bundle offers.

Already imported your products? Orbling turns each one into a product page in your own theme: a Buy box with bundle offers that stay above the cost you enter, and Key specs from your product data and the facts you confirm. From your catalog to a published page.

  1. Choose a productAny active product on your online store, also one you imported. Up to 100 variants.
  2. Set your offersBundle offers for 2 and 3 items with your cost plus shipping per item, and the delivery and return days you enter, if you want them shown.
  3. Build a free previewBuild my page makes a draft from your catalog and the facts you confirm, and shows it only in your theme editor. In the theme editor, your draft shows bundle prices marked as a draft until you publish.
  4. PublishSelect Publish: Orbling writes the page to your product and turns on your bundle discounts. Then place the blocks in the theme editor from Theme setup. Publish needs the Growth plan.

Your page shows what you give it.

Orbling builds each page from your Shopify product data and the facts you confirm. It does not write product text of its own, and only facts you mark as checked become page content.

Where each part of the page comes from

Key specs
The facts you confirm. Until you add them, your Shopify product details, such as the product type.
Story
Why it works, who it suits, what is in the box and questions come from the facts you confirm, from a CSV file or research lines you paste (section | label | value).
Delivery
Delivery and return days exactly as you enter them in Build. Empty fields stay hidden.
Bundles
Your discount for 2 and 3 items, kept above the cost plus shipping per item you enter.

Orbling does not check whether your facts are true. Use true only for facts you have checked. Your page content goes live when you publish.

Bundle offers for 2 and 3 items.

When you publish, Orbling creates Shopify automatic discounts for one product: a percentage or a fixed amount off each item. The example takes 5 USD off each of 2 items and 9 USD off each of 3.

  • Above your costsEach variant's price after the discount must stay above the cost plus shipping per item you enter. If you leave the cost empty, Orbling tells you so in Build and before you publish: Cost not entered. Orbling cannot keep bundle prices above your cost.
  • No stackingBundle discounts do not combine with other discounts.
  • Turned off by itselfIf a price or discount changes in Shopify so the saving no longer holds, Orbling turns the saving off, as long as it has access to edit discounts.
  • Off on every planTurn off bundle discounts removes the product's discounts and keeps your content. Turning off Orbling discounts works on every plan, including Free. Do it before you uninstall: after that, Orbling can no longer change your store.

How we count, every rule with its source

What Orbling does not do.

  • It does not build your whole store. No header, menus, collections, pages or policies.

  • It does not edit your product details. Titles, descriptions, images and prices stay as they are.

  • It adds no reviews, ratings, countdowns or compare‑at prices.

  • It does not track orders or sales. It has no access to orders or customers.

  • It is in English only. The app and support are in English.

Questions about Orbling.

How do I get Orbling?

Orbling: Product Page Builder is in the Shopify App Store. It is the new name of ARLing: Agentic Product Check, one app with one listing. Shopify reviews app updates before a listing changes, so the App Store listing may still show the earlier name, ARLing: Agentic Product Check.

Does the free preview change my live store?

The draft does not. Build my page saves a draft that your theme shows only inside the theme editor, with a note that it is a draft. Saving the theme makes the blocks live right away on every product page that uses this template. Until you publish, they show your Shopify product details. Your page content goes live when you publish.

Does Orbling build my whole store?

No. For the products you choose, it builds product page blocks from your catalog and confirmed facts, and bundle offers for 2 and 3 items. It does not create your header, footer, menus, collections, pages, policies or domain.

Does Orbling work with my theme?

Your theme must support app blocks. Buy box, Product story and Home need an Online Store 2.0 theme, for example Dawn or Horizon. Orbling does not edit theme files: you place the blocks from the links on Theme setup and save in Shopify. In your store, Orbling blocks use the colors and fonts of your theme. The Orbling Look embed adds optional extras, all off until you turn them on.

Which permissions does Orbling ask for?

Four, all required at install: read_products, write_products, read_discounts and write_discounts. Orbling needs them to save page content to your products and to create bundle discounts, and Shopify asks you to approve them once. It asks for no access to orders, customers or your theme code.

How much does it cost, and how do I cancel?

Free: build product pages, preview them in your theme and check your catalog. Growth: 19 USD a month or 190 USD a year, with a 7-day free trial on Growth if this store has not used one. Shopify bills the plan. Change or cancel on Shopify's plan page, which Orbling opens from Plan and billing. Cancelling stops future charges; charges already submitted may remain on your Shopify invoice.

What is Catalog check?

A separate screen in the app that reads your products and lists those with missing or incomplete data, with the rule and its source for each issue. It does not change your store. The rules are on How we count.

Who answers support questions?

Write to support@arling.sk in English. Replies come from an AI assistant run by ARLing, and support cannot change anything in your store. We do not promise a response time yet.
