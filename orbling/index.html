From your catalog to a published page.

Orbling builds one product page at a time. Your theme keeps its header, footer and layout; you place the blocks and save in the theme editor.

  1. Choose a productFrom your Shopify catalog. Nothing to import; up to 100 variants.
  2. Pick a lookNest, Halo or Torque. Orbling recommends one for the product's category.
  3. Build my pageA free draft from your catalog alone, without AI. It shows only in your theme editor, never to shoppers.
  4. Improve with AISuggestions for key specs, who the product suits and common questions. The first time, it starts the 7‑day free trial.
  5. Accept and publishCheck each source, select Accept all, then Publish. Add the Orbling blocks in the theme editor and save.

Every suggestion shows its source.

Facts are taken word for word from your product data or from facts you confirmed. Other lines are rewritten by AI and pass an automatic check: numbers and materials must appear in the cited source, and superlatives or urgency are removed.

The rules also reject

  • certificates not in the source
  • reviews and ratings
  • health claims
  • anything about price, stock, delivery, returns or warranty
Input
The product title, category, up to 4,000 characters of plain description text and the facts you confirmed. Not prices, policies or buyer data as separate fields.
Model
Llama 3.3 70B on Cloudflare Workers AI.
Limit
Up to 20 AI runs per store per day (UTC), plus a daily capacity limit shared by all stores.

The checks are rules. They do not prove that your description is true, so read each source before you select Accept all.

Bundle offers for 2 and 3 items.

Orbling creates real Shopify automatic discounts for one product when you publish. By default 2 items get 10% off and 3 items 15% off; you can change the amounts or turn bundles off.

  • Above your costsEach variant's price after the discount must stay above the cost plus shipping per item you enter.
  • No stackingBundle discounts do not combine with other discounts.
  • Turned off by itselfIf a price or discount changes in Shopify so the saving no longer holds, Orbling turns the saving off.
  • Off at any timeTurn off bundle discounts removes the product's discounts and keeps your content. It works without a plan.
  • Before you uninstallTurn the discounts off first: after uninstalling, Orbling can no longer change your store.

What Orbling does not do.

  • It does not build your whole store. No header, footer, menus, collections, pages, policies or domain.

  • It does not edit your product details. Titles, descriptions, images, prices and variants stay as they are. Publish writes only Orbling's own product fields, plus Shopify discounts when bundles are on.

  • It does not edit theme files. It asks for no theme permissions; you place the blocks.

  • It works only with themes that support app blocks.

  • It adds no reviews, ratings, countdowns or compare‑at prices.

  • It does not count sales yet. The Revenue screen stays empty until Shopify approves access to protected customer data and order webhooks are on.

  • It is in English only. The app and support are in English, and it does not translate.

Questions about Orbling.

Can I install Orbling today?

No. Orbling is not in the Shopify App Store yet, so there is no public listing to install from. This page will link to the listing once Orbling is in the Shopify App Store. Our other app, Agentic Product Check, is in the Shopify App Store today.

Does the free preview change my live store?

No. Build my page saves a draft that your theme shows only inside the theme editor, with a note that it is a draft. Shoppers see Orbling content only after you select Publish. If you turn on the Orbling Look app embed or save an Orbling block before that, the change is live in your theme right away, as with any theme change.

Why is it called AI Store Builder if it does not build my whole store?

Orbling: AI Store Builder is the app's name in Shopify. For the products you choose, it builds page blocks from your catalog and confirmed facts, a look for those blocks and bundle offers for 2 and 3 items. It does not create your header, footer, menus, collections, pages, policies or domain.

Does Orbling work with my theme?

Your theme must support app blocks. Orbling does not edit theme files: you place the blocks from the links on Theme setup and save in Shopify. By default the Orbling Look embed applies the look's colors, shape and type to your whole theme while the header, footer and layout stay; you can turn that off in the embed settings.

What does the AI receive, and where does it run?

Cloudflare Workers AI (Llama 3.3 70B) receives our instructions, the facts you confirmed with their sources, the product title, category and up to 4,000 characters of plain text from the product description. It does not receive prices, policies, the store domain, order or buyer data as separate fields. Avoid personal data in the text you supply.

How much does it cost, and how do I cancel?

19.00 USD every 30 days after a 7-day free trial, one trial per store while Orbling holds its data, with no usage or other app charges. Cancel anytime on the Plan page. Cancelling or uninstalling stops future charges; charges already submitted may remain on your Shopify invoice.

Who answers support questions?

Write to support@arling.sk in English. Replies come from an AI assistant run by ARLing, and support cannot change anything in your store. We do not promise a response time yet.
