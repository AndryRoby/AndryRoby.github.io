Questions

Is anything stored on your server?

No. What you type stays in your browser. When you send a color or an answer, it travels inside the link itself, in the part after the # sign. Browsers do not send that part to any server, ours included, and our visit counter (self-hosted Umami, no cookies) is set to ignore it. We only count that something happened, for example “a card was opened”, never what was in it.

Can someone else read my answer?

Yes, anyone who has the link. Your answer is encoded so it fits in a link, not encrypted, and anyone with the link can turn it back into text. Send it only where you would send a normal message, and keep secrets out of it.

Why do I answer before I see theirs?

So your answer is your own and not an echo of theirs. Nothing is locked: if you would rather just read it, tap “Just show me their answer”.

Does this page remember anything?

Only the date you entered, and only in this browser on this device, so you do not have to type it again. “Forget my date on this device” removes it.

How do you count the days?

Calendar days in your own time zone, from the date you pick to today, with leap days and clock changes handled. The day you got together is day 0. Some apps count it as day 1, so they show one day more.

What does it cost?

Nothing. No ads, no account, no payment.

Is there an app?

Not yet. We are testing whether people want one. We will only build it if enough people tap “I’d use the app”, and we do not promise a date.
