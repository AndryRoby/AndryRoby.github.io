01

How it works

Three steps, no server-side install, no salesperson.

  1. You paste your feed URL and email.

    We support Heureka/Zboží.cz XML (the feed Shoptet and Upgates export), Google Shopping RSS/XML, Shopify /products.json, WooCommerce REST/Store API JSON, and a generic XML feed with item/name/price/url tags.

  2. Your feed is processed into embeddings.

    A Worker downloads the feed, shortens and splits the descriptions into chunks, and stores them in Cloudflare Vectorize (up to 5,000 products). The feed refreshes automatically once a day.

  3. You add one <script> tag.

    The assistant appears in the bottom right of the page. It answers only from your feed and your shop’s contact details, never makes anything up, and if it does not know, it points to your shop’s contact instead.

Before you start

Do you store customer conversations?

No. The content of a conversation is never stored, not by us, not in any database. We only keep counters: how many conversations and product clicks happened per day, for billing and the monthly limit.

Do you have a GDPR Article 28 agreement?

Yes. ARLing s. r. o. acts as processor for the messages your e-shop’s visitors send. Template agreement: Data Processing Agreement (DPA). The template is written in Slovak; an English translation is available on request at andrej@arling.sk.

How do I cancel if it does not work out?

The free plan up to 100 conversations a month has no commitment and no card, you simply stop using it. A paid plan above that limit runs through Stripe and can be cancelled any time, same as every other ARLing tool.

What if my feed is not in any of the supported formats?

We support Heureka/Zboží.cz XML (SHOP/SHOPITEM tags, exported by Shoptet or Upgates, for example), Google Shopping RSS/XML (g: tags), Shopify /products.json, WooCommerce REST/Store API JSON, and a generic XML feed with item/name/price/url/description/image tags. Try a different format in the form below; if processing rejects it, write to andrej@arling.sk with a sample of your feed.

FAQ




      

        How long does setup take?
        
Paste your product feed URL and email. A Worker downloads the feed, chunks it, and stores it as embeddings, usually within a few minutes depending on catalogue size (the limit is 5,000 products). Then you add one <script> tag to your e-shop.

      

      

        Which feed formats do you support?
        
Heureka/Zboží.cz XML (SHOP/SHOPITEM tags, exported by Shoptet or Upgates, for example), Google Shopping RSS/XML (g: tags), Shopify /products.json, WooCommerce REST/Store API JSON, and a generic XML feed with item/name/price/url/description/image tags.

      

      

        Do you store customer conversations?
        
No. The content of a conversation is never stored, not by us, not in any database. We only keep counters: how many conversations and product clicks happened per day, for billing and the monthly limit.

      

      

        What language does the assistant reply in?
        
Slovak, Czech, English or German, depending on the widget’s data-lang setting on the page. The answer always comes only from the products in your feed and never makes anything up.

      

      

        What if the assistant cannot answer?
        
If nothing relevant to the question is found in the feed, the assistant says so clearly and points the customer to contact the shop directly, at the email address entered during setup.

      

      

        Does this work on Shoptet, WooCommerce or Shopify?
        
The script works on any e-shop right away, you just add one <script> tag to the admin or theme. The dedicated WooCommerce plugin has been submitted to wordpress.org and is awaiting review (until then it installs from a ZIP file from GitHub); the Shopify app is in preparation. Shoptet and Upgates shops have a separate guide (Heureka XML feed from the admin, script tag through the HTML code editor). The Slovak demo shop shows the widget on a fictional Slovak store.

      

      

        What is free?
        
Up to 100 conversations a month, forever, no payment card. You can try it right on this page with your own feed, the chat appears in the bottom right.

      

      

        Where does the data run?
        
On Cloudflare’s global network (Workers, Vectorize, D1). Cloudflare does not publicly guarantee an EU-only processing location, so the Data Processing Agreement (DPA) between ARLing and the e-shop relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) and Cloudflare’s EU Cloud Code of Conduct.

      

      

        How does payment and billing work?
        
Up to 100 conversations a month, use is completely free, no card. Above that you upgrade to Starter (19 EUR a month, up to 1,000 conversations) or Pro (39 EUR a month, up to 3,000) from your account page (arling.sk/asistent/tenant/?t=your account id), linked right after the account is created. You pay by card through Stripe, Stripe e-mails the receipt and invoice, and you can cancel any time.
