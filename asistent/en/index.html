A small chat in the corner of your e-shop that helps shoppers choose from your product feed. It answers in the shopper’s language, even in the evening when nobody is at the phone, and adds cards for up to three suggested products, with price and link, below the answer.

How it answers your shoppers

Two more real screenshots from the same demo shop. The shop and its 64 products are made up; the answers are not edited, only cropped to the chat window. These two were asked in Slovak, so each has an English translation in its transcript.

Answers are written by AI over your products, so the wording can differ slightly when asked again. The name, price and link on each card come straight from your feed. Ask the demo shop yourself.

How to switch it on

The assistant takes your products from the feed your e-shop already has and refreshes them from it automatically. You put it on your site in one of three ways.

  1. WooCommerce

    A plugin from wordpress.org: in your admin go to Plugins, Add New and search for "ARLing". It loads the products from your shop by itself. WooCommerce guide

  2. Shoptet and Upgates

    The Heureka feed your shop already generates, plus one line of code added as HTML code in the admin. Shoptet and Upgates guide (Slovak)

  3. Any other e-shop

    One line before </body>. You get the exact line with your account number after the trial below. On Shopify the same way, in the theme.

    <script src="https://arling-asistent.arling.workers.dev/widget.js" data-tenant="…" data-lang="auto" defer></script>

Feed formats: Heureka and Zboží.cz XML, Google Shopping, Shopify /products.json, WooCommerce and generic XML, up to 5,000 products. The source code of the widget and the server is public on GitHub.

What shop owners ask us

      

      

        

          What if the AI says something wrong?
          
It can make mistakes, including in which product it picks, so it runs inside firm limits. It answers only from your feed and your contact details, treats product text as data, never as instructions, and when you do not sell something it says so (see the screenshot above). The name, price and link on every product card come straight from your feed, not from the text the AI writes. The best test is your own products: the trial is free and you add it to your site only once you like the answers.

        

        

          Does it stick to the shopper’s budget?
          
When asked for something "under 30 EUR", the assistant may still suggest a more expensive product. A price cap in the normal chat is not yet a reliable filter. The prices on the product cards come from your feed. Check the answers on your own products before you switch it on.

        

        

          Do you store customer conversations?
          
No. The content of a conversation is never stored, not by us, not in any database. We only keep counters: how many conversations and product clicks happened per day, for billing and the monthly limit.

        

        

          Which languages does it answer in?
          
The chat window has its texts in Slovak, Czech, English and German. With the line of code from the form (data-lang="auto") it answers in the language the shopper writes in; the screenshot above shows English on a Slovak shop. Product names stay exactly as they are in your feed.

        

        

          Do you have a GDPR Article 28 agreement?
          
Yes. ARLing s. r. o. acts as processor for the messages your e-shop’s visitors send. Template agreement: Data Processing Agreement (DPA). The template is written in Slovak; an English translation is available on request at support@arling.sk.

        

        

          How do I cancel if it does not work out?
          
The free plan up to 100 conversations a month has no commitment and no card, you simply stop using it. A paid plan above that limit is a Stripe subscription. You can cancel or change the subscription at any time in the Stripe customer portal (log in with the e-mail you paid with); it stays active until the end of the paid period.

        

        

          What if my feed is not in any of the supported formats?
          
We support Heureka/Zboží.cz XML (SHOP/SHOPITEM tags, exported by Shoptet or Upgates, for example), Google Shopping RSS/XML (g: tags), Shopify /products.json, WooCommerce REST/Store API JSON, and a generic XML feed with item/name/price/url/description/image tags. Try a different format in the form above; if processing rejects it, write to support@arling.sk with a sample of your feed.

        

      

    

  


  

    

      
Privacy as a feature, not an afterthought

      

      
The widget is built to know as little as possible about your e-shop’s visitors.

      
    
        
  • Conversations are not stored. The content of messages never persists anywhere, not with us, not in any database. We only keep daily counters of conversations and product clicks, for the monthly limit and billing.
    • 
        
  • No cookies and no localStorage. So that a conversation can continue in the same tab, the widget keeps only a random session identifier and a signed conversation token in the browser’s sessionStorage, not the content of messages. They disappear when the tab is closed.
    • 
        
  • Your product feed is the single source of truth. The assistant answers only from what is in your feed and your contact details, and treats product text as data, never as instructions. It can still make mistakes, so try the answers before you switch it on.
    • 
        
  • GDPR Article 28 agreement. ARLing s. r. o. acts as processor for the messages your e-shop’s visitors send. Template agreement: Data Processing Agreement (DPA), written in Slovak; an English translation is available on request.
    • 
        
  • Runs on Cloudflare’s global network. Cloudflare does not publicly guarantee EU-only processing location for Workers AI; the DPA therefore relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) and Cloudflare’s certification under the EU Cloud Code of Conduct.
    • 
        
  • Data about you as the shop owner. When you create an assistant we store your e-mail address, domain, feed URL, chosen language, where the account came from (form or plugin) and account events: ready, live on your site, first question, limit, plan, address verification and which e-mails we sent. We learn that the assistant is live from the domain of the page that loads it; we store nothing about your visitors that way. E-mails: only to an address verified with a 6-digit code (or a company address on the shop’s own domain), at most four in total, chosen from these types: setup instructions, a message if the products do not load, a live confirmation, one reminder, a notice at 80% of the free limit. The link in each of them stops all further ones; if you did not create the assistant, you can say so on the same page and nothing more will reach your address. Legal basis: the setup instructions and messages about the account you created are performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); the live confirmation, reminder and limit notice are our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)), which you can refuse at any time with the link in the e-mail. Who processes the data with us: Cloudflare (running the service and the database), Resend, Inc. in the USA (sending e-mails, so the address and the e-mail content are transferred to the USA) and our own CRM on our own server. How long: account events 24 months, the account with your e-mail until you cancel it; for a paid plan, accounting records for as long as the accounting law requires. To have your account and data deleted within 7 days, including the CRM record, write to support@arling.sk.

FAQ




      

        How long does setup take?
        
Paste your product feed URL and email. A Worker downloads the feed, chunks it, and stores it as embeddings, usually within a few minutes depending on catalogue size (the limit is 5,000 products). Then you add one <script> tag to your e-shop.

      

      

        Which feed formats do you support?
        
Heureka/Zboží.cz XML (SHOP/SHOPITEM tags, exported by Shoptet or Upgates, for example), Google Shopping RSS/XML (g: tags), Shopify /products.json, WooCommerce REST/Store API JSON, and a generic XML feed with item/name/price/url/description/image tags.

      

      

        Do you store customer conversations?
        
No. The content of a conversation is never stored, not by us, not in any database. We only keep counters: how many conversations and product clicks happened per day, for billing and the monthly limit.

      

      

        What language does the assistant reply in?
        
Slovak, Czech, English or German, depending on the widget’s data-lang setting on the page. It suggests only products from your feed.

      

      

        What if the assistant cannot answer?
        
If nothing relevant to the question is found in the feed, the assistant says so clearly and points the customer to contact the shop directly, at the email address entered during setup.

      

      

        Does this work on Shoptet, WooCommerce or Shopify?
        
The script works on any e-shop right away, you just add one <script> tag to the admin or theme. The dedicated WooCommerce plugin is approved on wordpress.org, install it right from the admin under Plugins, Add New, by searching for "ARLing". On Shopify the assistant is added the same way, with one script tag in the theme; we do not have an app in the Shopify App Store. Shoptet and Upgates shops have a separate guide (Heureka XML feed from the admin, script tag through the HTML code editor). The Slovak demo shop shows the widget on a fictional Slovak store.

      

      

        What is free?
        
Up to 100 conversations a month, forever, no payment card. You can try it right on this page with your own feed, the chat appears in the bottom right.

      

      

        Where does the data run?
        
On Cloudflare’s global network (Workers, Vectorize, D1). Cloudflare does not publicly guarantee an EU-only processing location, so the Data Processing Agreement (DPA) between ARLing and the e-shop relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) and Cloudflare’s EU Cloud Code of Conduct.

      

      

        How does payment and billing work?
        
Up to 100 conversations a month, use is completely free, no card. Above that you upgrade to Starter (19 EUR a month, up to 1,000 conversations) or Pro (39 EUR a month, up to 3,000) from your account page (arling.sk/asistent/tenant/?t=your account id), linked right after the account is created. You pay by card through Stripe, Stripe e-mails the receipt and invoice, and you can cancel any time.
