03

Who should stay with Tidio

Stay with Tidio if people answer your customers. A shared inbox, tickets, email and social messages in one place, Tidio Flows: ARLing Asistent replaces none of that. It has no inbox, no agents and no tickets, and we do not plan to add them.

Stay as well if the AI should answer from more than the catalogue, such as shipping or return rules in a knowledge base. ARLing Asistent answers only from the feed and your contact details; anything else gets an honest "I do not know, write to the shop".

Consider ARLing Asistent if most questions are "do you have X", "which fits Y" and "how much is Z", nobody sits in an inbox, and you do not want a third party keeping your customers' conversations. The trial form builds an assistant from your feed in minutes.

04

FAQ

Can ARLing Asistent replace Tidio's live chat?

No. There is no human behind it and no inbox. It covers only the part of Tidio that answers product questions automatically, and only what the feed can answer.

Can I run it next to Tidio?

Technically yes, both are script tags; put the ARLing widget in the other corner with the data-position attribute. Most stores pick one.

Is there anything to migrate?

No. It builds itself from your product feed (Heureka XML, Google Shopping XML, Shopify /products.json, WooCommerce Store API, generic XML) and refreshes it daily.
